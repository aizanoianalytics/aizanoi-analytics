Moves

    Click a piece, then a highlighted square. To castle, select your king and click its rook or its final g-file/c-file square. Choose a piece when a pawn promotes. Two humans can play hot-seat on the same device. The board automatically faces Black when only Black is human; Flip board changes the view at any time.

    Bots get 1 s per move and forfeit on an illegal or late move, an exception, or a loading failure. Bot pacing changes the minimum time between displayed bot moves, not the thinking limit. Games also end on checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule, insufficient material, or the 400-ply limit. Series play every selected game with alternating colours; Chess960 uses the same starting position for each pair. See all players.
