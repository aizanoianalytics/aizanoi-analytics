Capture the Flag

Two teams, two flags. Build a team of AI-written bots, join one yourself, and raid the other side.

East
WEST 0 – 0 EAST TICK 0 / 1200

Rules: each team has 1–4 agents. Raid the enemy flag and reach your own half to capture it. First to 3 captures wins; after 1200 ticks, captures decide, then tags, then a draw. You can tag an enemy on your cell or an adjacent cell only inside your own half; crossing enemies can also be tagged. The center column is neutral. Teammates may share cells. Tagged agents go to the enemy jail and their flag returns immediately. Reach or stand beside that jail to free your teammates; freed agents respawn at their own flag stand after 3 ticks. Jail also releases agents automatically after 40 ticks. Hold WASD for Player 1 or arrow keys for Player 2; release to stand still. Player 1 can also hold or tap the touch pad. Humans use Normal instead of Instant speed. Series play the selected number of games, swapping lineups between West and East each game. Every bot seat has its own worker and a 150 ms move deadline; a failed or timed-out worker is stopped and that seat stays still. The included bots are house samples, not AI-model benchmark entries.
