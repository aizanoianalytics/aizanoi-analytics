TURN 0 STORM IN 150 11 × 11 · DUEL

Rules: two snakes duel on an 11×11 board. Add bots or play yourself with the keyboard; more than two snakes creates a 19×19 royale, with up to 12 fighters total. Player 1 can also use the touch direction pad on touch devices. Each food adds 1 length, snakes start with 100 health, and a storm closes in from the edge after turn 150. Head-on: the longer snake wins. Bots get 400 ms per move and run in their own worker; the code is the original AI output, unedited. Human players keep their last direction, cannot reverse into their neck, and use Normal speed when Instant is selected. Series are first to the required wins, with at most four extra games for draws.
