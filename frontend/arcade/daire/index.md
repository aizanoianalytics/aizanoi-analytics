# Ev

Walkable apartment at `/arcade/daire/`. Built from the owner's sketch and room notes, not from the factory.

Room 4 is the balcony outside room 3, with the combi. The Burn cabinet is a small energy-drink cooler on the kitchen sliding cabinet, not an upper cupboard. The interior is rebuilt as composed furniture from the room notes and the unnumbered sketch. It does not use the factory outdoor ground.

The page reuses the already vendored Three.js r180 runtime at `/arcade/factory/engine.js`. It does not copy that bundle.
