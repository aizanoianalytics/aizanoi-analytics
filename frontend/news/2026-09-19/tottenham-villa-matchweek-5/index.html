Tottenham and Aston Villa chase first Premier League wins of season in Saturday lunchtime kickoff

Tottenham host Aston Villa at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium at 12:30 BST on Saturday in a Premier League Matchweek 5 fixture between the only two sides still without a league win this season. Spurs sit 17th on two points from four matches with zero goals scored, while Villa are 19th on one point having shipped seven. Tottenham are without Dejan Kulusevski, Xavi Simons, Wilson Odobert and Mykhaylo Mudryk, with Pedro Porro and Sandro Tonali doubts, while Joao Gomes and Matty Cash are fitness concerns for the visitors.
