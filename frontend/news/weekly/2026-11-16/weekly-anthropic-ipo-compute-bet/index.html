Anthropic's IPO will ask public markets to underwrite a $901bn compute bet

At $70 to $78 a share for 825 million shares, pricing on 18 November and trading as ANT the next day, Anthropic would be valued at up to $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis, above the $965 billion of its May round. The proceeds are less a reward for past growth than a buffer. Dario Amodei told a JPMorgan audience that compute must be bought one to two years before demand is known, and Bloomberg puts commitments near $901 billion, with about $71 billion due by September 2027.
