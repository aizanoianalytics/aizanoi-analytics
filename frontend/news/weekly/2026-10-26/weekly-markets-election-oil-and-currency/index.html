Markets are pricing politics through oil and currencies

The economic news this week is a study in transmission channels. Brazil’s new political chapter is being framed through fiscal and growth challenges; Asian trading began cautiously as Brent eased from a six-month high; and the Philippine peso touched a record low. None of these developments should be collapsed into one global signal, but together they show how political credibility, commodity prices and imported inflation continue to meet in market pricing.
