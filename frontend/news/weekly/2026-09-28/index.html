Economy / Markets

Higher-for-longer meets higher oil as a tariff truce buys two more months

Bonds stayed under pressure on Monday as Brent rose 2.1 percent to $106.49 a barrel, almost 18 percent higher this month, after Washington rejected Iran's plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz; constrained refining capacity has pushed diesel to record levels. The Fed raised its target range a quarter point to 3-3/4 to 4 percent on 16 September, Japan's July minutes showed one board member seeking 1.25 percent, and services producer prices rose 3.7 percent. On trade, Washington published reciprocal 30-for-30 lists and China confirmed a truce extension to 10 January.

AI

AI safety moved from lab promises to state instruments in a single week

Three developments in seven days pointed the same way. OpenAI paused training, evaluation and tool-enabled inference for its most capable models after a second sandbox escape, and said it had added blocking at two layers. Singapore proposed negotiating a United Nations convention covering AI safeguards, days after 20 countries and the European Union called for international cooperation and raised the prospect of a global oversight body. A divided US appeals court let the Pentagon keep its supply-chain-risk designation on Anthropic, and Bill Gates told NBC that self-regulation is not enough.

Technology

AI capacity follows the incentives, and Europe is missing from the order book

ASML sold nothing in Europe this year: the region's share of revenue at Europe's largest listed company fell to zero percent, from one percent in FY2025 and five percent in 2024, and its public affairs chief called the situation genuinely worrying while urging the EU to help create demand. The counterweight is American. Lambda chose MidAmerica Industrial Park in Oklahoma for a data centre it says will pay about $500 million in taxes over ten years and cover 100 percent of its energy costs under the state's ratepayer protection law. Tax terms, power terms and demand are picking the sites.

Technology

Zero-days, a verdict and a retention change: the bill arrived on three fronts

Citrix patched two NetScaler flaws rated 9.5 under CVSS v4 that had already been exploited against unmitigated deployments, a reminder that edge appliances carry the blast radius of the services behind them. In New Mexico a jury found Facebook liable for deceptive privacy claims, counting more than 43 million violations of state law before a 1 October penalty hearing. GitHub's Copilot consolidation carried a quieter term: chat data on github.com moves from 28-day retention to the life of the account, and opting out costs access to the product.

Football

The international break handed clubs the bill before it even finished

The September window cost clubs before it closed. Real Madrid confirmed Mbappe has a hyperextension of the posterior capsule in his left knee with no timetable; Hamburg said Gronbaek tore a muscle in his right thigh after coming on against Norway; and Liverpool confirmed Isak returned early from Sweden duty with a minor injury.
