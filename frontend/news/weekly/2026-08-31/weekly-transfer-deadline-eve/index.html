Weekly Football: deadline day looms as Premier League clubs chase the last deals of a frenetic window

Premier League clubs have 48 hours to complete signings before Tuesday's 11 p.m. BST deadline, with two-hour deal-sheet grace periods for paperwork filed in time. Aston Villa are in transition after confirming Ollie Watkins's £51 million move to Al-Hilal and signing Nicolas Jackson plus Taylor Harwood-Bellis. Tottenham have been among the busiest, completing a Sandro Tonali deal worth £100 million and adding Mateus Fernandes and Savio, while Manchester City rebuilt midfield with Elliot Anderson and Ayyoub Bouaddi.
