Matchweek 11 gives Arsenal a cushion and everyone else a set of questions

Arsenal's 2-2 draw at Newcastle was the sort of result that usually costs a leader momentum, yet it left them top on 28 points from 11 games. Brighton wasted the chance to close within three, losing 2-0 at Hull to stoppage-time goals, and stay second on 22. Liverpool's 4-0 win over Manchester United exposed Mauricio Pochettino's rebuild, while Manchester City's 1-1 draw with Fulham and Chelsea's 1-4 home defeat by Leeds compressed the pack. In Germany, Dortmund lead on 24, with Bayern and Freiburg on 22 and Leipzig 21.
