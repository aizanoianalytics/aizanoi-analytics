The AI trade has met its first genuine stress test, and the bill is coming due

A week meant to validate the artificial-intelligence boom instead repriced it. The S&P 500 fell 8.6% and closed in correction, the Nasdaq lost 10%, and roughly $4 trillion of market value disappeared as Nvidia dropped 14% on Friday and Anthropic slipped 8.5% from its debut close. The deeper signal is in credit: US high-yield spreads widened to an 18-month high, and Federal Reserve minutes flagged off-balance-sheet leases and circular financing among AI firms. Equity investors are now asking the question bond investors asked first: who pays for the compute if returns arrive late?
