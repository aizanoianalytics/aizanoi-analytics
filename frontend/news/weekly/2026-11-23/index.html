AI

2026-11-23

The AI trade has met its first genuine stress test, and the bill is coming due

A week meant to validate the artificial-intelligence boom instead repriced it. The S&P 500 fell 8.6% and closed in correction, the Nasdaq lost 10%, and roughly $4 trillion of market value disappeared as Nvidia dropped 14% on Friday and Anthropic slipped 8.5% from its debut close. The deeper signal is in credit: US high-yield spreads widened to an 18-month high, and Federal Reserve minutes flagged off-balance-sheet leases and circular financing among AI firms. Equity investors are now asking the question bond investors asked first: who pays for the compute if returns arrive late?

Economy / Markets

2026-11-23

The Fed's inflation problem has become an AI-credit problem

Federal Reserve minutes from the October meeting describe an economy still short of the 2% target, with staff estimating September PCE inflation at 3.6% and core at 3.1%. Almost every participant saw further increases in the target range as likely, and futures price a quarter-point hike by year-end plus two more by summer 2027. The new element is financial stability: officials warned that AI-related debt has grown through complex, opaque structures that could amplify losses if returns disappoint. Wednesday's PCE print tests both stories.

Technology

2026-11-23

Agents are turning into infrastructure, and the hard part is now permission

Three releases this month point the same way. OpenAI reports its agents finish 46% of two-to-four-hour internal research tasks unaided, up from 23% in March, at a median $2,400 of agent spend per researcher per day. Google's Agent Compute Engine gives each agent a hardware-isolated microVM with no credentials inside, and Gemini's Mac app clicks through local apps behind approval gates. Anthropic cut vulnerability disclosure to 30 days because models keep rediscovering flaws. Capability is arriving faster than the audit trail.

Technology

2026-11-23

Memory is the new bottleneck in the AI build-out, and buyers are locking it down

The AI trade's least glamorous constraint is now its most contested. TrendForce puts server DRAM at 43% of total demand this year, up from 38%, while mobile DRAM slips to 32% as capacity shifts to high-bandwidth memory. Suppliers have the leverage: SK Hynix says long-term deals cover more than half of its 2027-28 capacity, Samsung has signed with 10 major customers, and Apple is reportedly near a four-to-five-year DRAM agreement with Samsung. US export rules add another queue. When memory allocation decides model timelines, procurement becomes strategy.

Sources TrendForce · The Korea Herald (via News18)

Football

2026-11-23

Matchweek 11 gives Arsenal a cushion and everyone else a set of questions

Arsenal's 2-2 draw at Newcastle was the sort of result that usually costs a leader momentum, yet it left them top on 28 points from 11 games. Brighton wasted the chance to close within three, losing 2-0 at Hull to stoppage-time goals, and stay second on 22. Liverpool's 4-0 win over Manchester United exposed Mauricio Pochettino's rebuild, while Manchester City's 1-1 draw with Fulham and Chelsea's 1-4 home defeat by Leeds compressed the pack. In Germany, Dortmund lead on 24, with Bayern and Freiburg on 22 and Leipzig 21.
