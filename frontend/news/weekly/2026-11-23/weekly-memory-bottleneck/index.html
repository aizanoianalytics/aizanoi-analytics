Memory is the new bottleneck in the AI build-out, and buyers are locking it down

The AI trade's least glamorous constraint is now its most contested. TrendForce puts server DRAM at 43% of total demand this year, up from 38%, while mobile DRAM slips to 32% as capacity shifts to high-bandwidth memory. Suppliers have the leverage: SK Hynix says long-term deals cover more than half of its 2027-28 capacity, Samsung has signed with 10 major customers, and Apple is reportedly near a four-to-five-year DRAM agreement with Samsung. US export rules add another queue. When memory allocation decides model timelines, procurement becomes strategy.
