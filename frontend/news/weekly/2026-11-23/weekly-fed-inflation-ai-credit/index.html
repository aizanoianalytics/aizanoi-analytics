The Fed's inflation problem has become an AI-credit problem

Federal Reserve minutes from the October meeting describe an economy still short of the 2% target, with staff estimating September PCE inflation at 3.6% and core at 3.1%. Almost every participant saw further increases in the target range as likely, and futures price a quarter-point hike by year-end plus two more by summer 2027. The new element is financial stability: officials warned that AI-related debt has grown through complex, opaque structures that could amplify losses if returns disappoint. Wednesday's PCE print tests both stories.
