Agents are turning into infrastructure, and the hard part is now permission

Three releases this month point the same way. OpenAI reports its agents finish 46% of two-to-four-hour internal research tasks unaided, up from 23% in March, at a median $2,400 of agent spend per researcher per day. Google's Agent Compute Engine gives each agent a hardware-isolated microVM with no credentials inside, and Gemini's Mac app clicks through local apps behind approval gates. Anthropic cut vulnerability disclosure to 30 days because models keep rediscovering flaws. Capability is arriving faster than the audit trail.
