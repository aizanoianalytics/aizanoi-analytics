Anthropic commits $100m to train 10,000 enterprise AI engineers

Anthropic launched Claude Frontier Academy, a $100 million programme that aims to train 10,000 'frontier deployed engineers' by the end of 2027. Early cohorts are drawn from consultancies and banks including Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey, Bain, Capgemini and Morgan Stanley, with sessions running in San Francisco, New York and London. The push to build enterprise deployment skills comes as the company heads toward a widely anticipated listing.
