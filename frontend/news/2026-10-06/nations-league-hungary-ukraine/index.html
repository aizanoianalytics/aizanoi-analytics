Hungary edge Ukraine as Northern Ireland are held in Nations League

Matchday four of the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League closed with Hungary beating Ukraine 2-1 and Northern Ireland drawing 0-0 with Georgia at Windsor Park. Bosnia and Herzegovina beat Poland 1-0 and Sweden won 1-0 in Romania in the other League B fixtures. In League C, Cyprus beat Latvia 2-1 and Montenegro drew 0-0 with Armenia. The league phase resumes in November, with Nations League Finals places still to be settled.
