Foxconn posts a record quarter as AI server demand surges

Hon Hai Precision Industry, known globally as Foxconn, reported third-quarter revenue of NT$3.03 trillion, a record quarterly high and up 47.12% from a year earlier. September sales alone passed NT$1 trillion for the first time, up 38.42% year on year. The contract manufacturer credited robust demand in its cloud and networking division, which builds AI servers, alongside gains in consumer electronics such as iPhones.
