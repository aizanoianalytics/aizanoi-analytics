France score four in 14 minutes to sink Belgium and hand Zidane a first home win

France trailed Belgium until the 77th minute at the Stade de France, then scored four times in 14 minutes through substitutes Desire Doue, Rayan Cherki and a Michael Olise double to win 4-1. Dodi Lukebakio had put Belgium ahead in the 35th minute against a debutant goalkeeper. The result gave Zinedine Zidane his first home victory as France coach and left his side top of Nations League Group A1, three points clear of Italy.
