Italy beat Turkey 3-1 for Mancini's first home win since his return

Italy won 3-1 against Turkey in Bologna, giving Roberto Mancini his first home victory since returning as coach. Gianluca Scamacca volleyed the opener in the 26th minute, Aral Simsir levelled just after the hour, and Riccardo Calafiori restored the lead three minutes later from a Nicolo Barella free kick. Pio Esposito sealed it late, becoming only the second Italy player to reach seven international goals before turning 21. Turkey stay bottom of the group without a point.
