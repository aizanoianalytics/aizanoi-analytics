UEFA Women's Champions League league phase opens its second day with holders Barcelona hosting Paris FC and Chelsea welcoming Austria Wien

Matchday 1 of the 2026/27 UEFA Women's Champions League league phase continues on Wednesday with four fixtures, headlined by holders Barcelona hosting Paris FC at 20:00 CET and Chelsea welcoming Austria Wien to the Cherry Red Records Stadium at 19:00 BST. The day also includes OH Leuven v Roma and Servette Chênois v OL Lyonnes at 18:45 CET. Bayern's home draw with Manchester City on Tuesday opened the phase; the top four sides in the 18-team league advance directly to the quarter-finals, with sides 5–12 entering a two-legged play-off.
