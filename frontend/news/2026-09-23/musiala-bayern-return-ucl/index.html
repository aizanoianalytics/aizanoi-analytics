Bayern Munich confirm Jamal Musiala's first squad selection in six months ahead of Champions League matchday two

Bayern Munich have placed Jamal Musiala in their matchday squad for the first time since a long-term injury, with the German international expected to feature on Wednesday in the UEFA Champions League league-phase fixture against Atalanta at the Allianz Arena. Musiala has not played since the spring after a six-month absence and his return adds to Vincent Kompany's options alongside Harry Kane. Bayern opened the league phase with a 5-0 win at Bodø/Glimt on 10 September and sit third in the Bundesliga on goal difference behind Borussia Dortmund.
