AC Milan and Juventus end short Serie A slides with home wins as Como lose at Frosinone

AC Milan beat Lecce 3-0 at the San Siro and Juventus beat Atalanta 2-0 at the Allianz Stadium on Sunday to bounce back from short Serie A slides. Christian Pulisic marked his first start of the season with Milan's opening goal in the 14th minute, while Francisco Conceicao and Gleison Bremer scored either side of half-time for Juventus. Elsewhere, Fiorentina held Napoli 1-1, leaving Massimiliano Allegri's side with only two wins from six matches in all competitions, and high-flying Como suffered their first defeat of the season, a 2-0 reverse at promoted Frosinone that ended a four-match.
