Atletico Madrid beat 10-man Real Madrid 2-1 in feisty derby to close gap on leaders Barcelona

Atletico Madrid beat 10-man Real Madrid 2-1 in a heated La Liga derby at the Metropolitano on Sunday, with Alejandro Grimaldo converting a penalty and Jonathan David adding a second after defender Dean Huijsen was sent off early in the second half for pulling back Giuliano Simeone in the box. Antonio Rudiger headed a late consolation for Jose Mourinho's side, who dropped to fourth and trail leaders Barcelona by six points after Raphinha's hat-trick saw the Catalans win 3-1 at Sevilla on Saturday.
