Manchester City go three clear as Haaland beats Brobbey hat-trick in 5-3 Sunderland thriller

Manchester City went top of the Premier League on Sunday with a 5-3 win over Sunderland that saw Erling Haaland score City's fifth to complete a perfect five-from-five start to the season and move clear at the summit after Arsenal's Saturday defeat at Brighton. Brian Brobbey scored a hat-trick for the promoted visitors, who led at one point before City's second-half response. Liverpool won 1-0 at Bournemouth through Alexander Isak's second-half strike on Andoni Iraola's return to the Vitality Stadium, Manchester United drew 1-1 at Fulham after Lisandro Martinez's own goal and Matheus Cunha's.
