Gemini Decision 1 turns bulk classification into a cheap API call

Google released Gemini Decision 1, a model that answers batches of closed-ended questions rather than writing prose, joining Jev and OpenAI's Luna decision model. It supports yes/no, multiple-choice and rubric-scoring questions, costs 10 cents per million input tokens with free output, and accepts text, images, audio, video and PDFs. Simon Willison's test on a video returned calibrated probabilities instead of the hard zeros and ones rivals produce.
