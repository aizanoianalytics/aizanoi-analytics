United agree in principle with Pochettino as Carrick's exit nears

Manchester United have a verbal agreement in principle with Mauricio Pochettino to become head coach, Sky Sports reported, with Michael Carrick still in charge on Wednesday but likely to be dismissed before the end of the week. United would owe about £7 million to buy Pochettino out of his US Soccer contract and expect him to stay with the USA for two games against Haiti first.
