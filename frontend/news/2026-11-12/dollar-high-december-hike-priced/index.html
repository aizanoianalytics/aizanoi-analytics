Traders fully price a December Fed hike as the dollar sits at an 18-month high

A Bloomberg gauge of the dollar held at an 18-month high as traders moved to fully price a quarter-point Federal Reserve increase in December, with a small chance of a half-point move. The repricing follows October consumer prices that rose 0.4% on the month and comments from governor Christopher Waller, who said a 50-basis-point hike should be considered. The 30-year Treasury yield slipped to 5.32% on haven demand.
