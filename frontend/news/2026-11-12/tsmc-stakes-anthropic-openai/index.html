TSMC puts up to $1 billion of its own balance sheet behind Anthropic and OpenAI

TSMC's board approved staking up to $500 million in Anthropic at its IPO price and up to $500 million in OpenAI preferred stock, putting supplier capital behind two of the largest buyers of its advanced chips. The same meeting cleared about $40.9 billion of capital appropriations, a $500 million chip-ecosystem investment vehicle and a dividend rise to NT$9 a share from NT$7. TSMC did not say when the OpenAI investment closes.
