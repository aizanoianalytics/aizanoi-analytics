England need a point at Wembley, and Kane needs one cap for the record

England host Croatia at Wembley on Thursday knowing a draw confirms a top-two finish in Nations League Group A3 and a quarter-final place, three days before they meet Spain in Madrid. Harry Kane is expected to start and pass Peter Shilton's record of 125 caps. Marc Guehi has withdrawn with a hip injury and Dan Burn replaced him; Croatia are without the suspended Ivan Perisic and five injured players.
