CoreWeave's quarter restores the AI trade, and Nvidia rises with it

CoreWeave jumped about 25% after reporting a third-quarter loss smaller than analysts expected, raising its full-year revenue forecast and pointing to roughly $9 billion of customer prepayments and deposits due in the next three months. The S&P 500 rose 1.3% and the Nasdaq 1.6%, with Nvidia up 2.9% at $173.73. Baird cut Nvidia to neutral that day, citing its chief executive's position and export risk.
