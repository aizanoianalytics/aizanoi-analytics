Fireworks AI widens its breach notice to customer datasets and partial model weights

Fireworks AI widened its account of an October intrusion: an attacker reached research machines through a third-party cloud provider on 23 October and ran an unauthorised workload the next day, exposing billing details, secrets and a subset of incomplete model weights and datasets for a limited set of accounts. The company says card numbers were not stored and blames a financially motivated group that resells access to language models.
