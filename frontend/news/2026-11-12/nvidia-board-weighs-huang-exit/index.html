Nvidia's board weighs a future without Jensen Huang as the stock slides

Nvidia shares fell 8.6% in after-hours trading after reports that the board met on Wednesday night to discuss chief executive Jensen Huang, who has admitted a relationship with a businesswoman US officials suspect of passing intelligence and advanced chips to China. Bloomberg reported that the company is working with an outside firm to identify possible replacements. The stock had gained 2.9% in the regular session before the late reversal.
