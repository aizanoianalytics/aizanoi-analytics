Anthropic's offering draws several times the shares on sale

Orders for Anthropic's offering have run to multiple times the 825 million shares on sale at $70 to $78, Bloomberg reported, helped by large anchor bids from institutions. At the top of the range the company raises up to $64.4 billion, or about $90.4 billion with a $26 billion private placement to seven chip and software partners. Shares are due to price on 18 November and trade on Nasdaq as ANT the next day.
