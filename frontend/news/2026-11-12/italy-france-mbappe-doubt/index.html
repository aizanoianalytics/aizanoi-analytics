France can seal a quarter-final place in Milan while Mbappe sits out training

France visit San Siro three points clear in Group A1 knowing a win over Italy books a top-two finish. Kylian Mbappe did not train on Wednesday and the staff said only that he is under treatment, while Marcus Thuram left camp with a hamstring problem and Ousmane Dembele, William Saliba and Pierre Kalulu are also out. Italy, a point above Belgium, called up Federico Gatti after Gianluca Mancini withdrew.
