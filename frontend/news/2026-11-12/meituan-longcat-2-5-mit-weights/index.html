Meituan releases its trillion-parameter LongCat-2.5 under a permissive licence

Meituan published the weights of LongCat-2.5 on Hugging Face under the MIT licence, a week after introducing the vision-capable mixture-of-experts model. The release lets teams run a system built for screenshots, video and multi-step coding inside their own networks, while Meituan's hosted API undercuts premium pricing. VentureBeat notes that no US federal restriction specifically names Meituan or LongCat, unlike DeepSeek.
