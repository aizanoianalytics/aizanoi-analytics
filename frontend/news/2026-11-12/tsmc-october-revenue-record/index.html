TSMC books a record October as 2-nanometer output starts to scale

TSMC reported record consolidated sales of NT$586.89 billion, about US$17.8 billion, for October, up 59.7% from a year earlier and 14.7% from September. Sales in the first ten months reached NT$4.49 trillion, 43.3% higher. Analysts credited demand for chips made on its 2-nanometer process, and the month ran ahead of the pace implied by fourth-quarter guidance of US$52.3 billion to US$54.3 billion.
