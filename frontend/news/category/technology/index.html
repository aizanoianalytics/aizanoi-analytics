3M and BOE open a joint laboratory for next-generation displays

3M and BOE have opened a joint research laboratory focused on display materials and manufacturing. The programme will work across LCD, OLED and MLED applications, including optical films, adhesives, foldable screens and ultra-narrow bezel processes.

GSA takes a coordinating role in the US post-quantum migration

The US General Services Administration says its technology-policy office will help coordinate the federal shift to post-quantum cryptography. The work covers identity and building-access systems as agencies move toward encryption intended to withstand future quantum attacks.

Apple sets unified EU app terms and revises commission structure

Apple announced a single set of business terms for developers distributing apps in the European Union, effective 1 October. Under the plan, its per-install Core Technology Fee gives way to a 5% charge on qualifying digital sales made through software obtained beyond the App Store.

GeForce Now adds Firefox to its Windows browser lineup

Nvidia has added Firefox support for GeForce Now on Windows, allowing members to launch supported cloud games from the browser without installing the dedicated application. Firefox joins Chrome, Edge and Opera among the service's supported Windows browsers.
