Aston Villa beat Tottenham 3-2 in north London to hand Roberto De Zerbi's side a third straight defeat

Aston Villa beat Tottenham Hotspur 3-2 at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on Saturday to claim a first Premier League win of the season, with Buendia laying on goals for Jackson and an 80th-minute long-range strike before late goals from Conor Gallagher and Jean Paul van Hecke set up a nervy finish. Villa took the lead in first-half stoppage time while Micky van de Ven received treatment on the touchline, with Jackson chesting the ball and finishing right-footed into the bottom corner. Spurs were booed off after a third defeat in a row.
