Lautaro Martinez out for around a month in a blow to Inter

Inter Milan confirmed that captain Lautaro Martinez underwent clinical and diagnostic tests that revealed a strain in his left hamstring, and Italian media reported the Argentina forward will be sidelined for about a month. The 29-year-old is set to miss several Serie A fixtures, including the Milan derby on 31 October, and Champions League games against Shakhtar Donetsk and Feyenoord, with a possible return around Atalanta on 22 November. The injury came days after Inter's 4-3 home defeat by Club Brugge and adds to a widening injury list across Serie A after the European round.
