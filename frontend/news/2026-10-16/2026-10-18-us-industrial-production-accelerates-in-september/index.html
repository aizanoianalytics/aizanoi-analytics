US industrial production rises 0.7% in September

Federal Reserve data show US industrial production increased 0.7% in September after a 0.1% rise in August. Manufacturing output grew 0.3%, while utilities rose 3.5%; the quarterly measure of total industrial production advanced at a 2.8% annual rate.
