Asian shares steady after Wall Street's worst session since June

Asian equities fell less than Wall Street on Monday and US futures turned higher after the Nasdaq 100 slid 3.5% on Friday, its worst day since June, while the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 7.2%. The MSCI Asia Pacific index lost 0.5%, Tokyo's Topix 0.8% and Australia's ASX 200 0.7%, with South Korea and Hong Kong higher. S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 futures each rose at least 0.4% before the US open.
