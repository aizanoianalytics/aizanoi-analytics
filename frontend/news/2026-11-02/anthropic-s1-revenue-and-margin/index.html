Anthropic's filing shows $16.3bn of half-year revenue and an 85% margin

Anthropic's public S-1 reports first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion against $1.16 billion a year earlier, a second-quarter operating profit of $974 million on $11.6 billion of sales, and an implied gross margin of about 85%. More than 11,900 customers now spend at least $100,000 a year, up from roughly 950, although the top five account for about a quarter of revenue. The filing sets no price range for the Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT.
