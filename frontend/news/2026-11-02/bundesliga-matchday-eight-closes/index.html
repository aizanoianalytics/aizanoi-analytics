Frankfurt beat Hamburg as the Bundesliga's top two stay level

Eintracht Frankfurt beat Hamburger SV 3-1 and Union Berlin drew 1-1 with Köln as Bundesliga matchday eight finished on Sunday. Bayern Munich and Borussia Dortmund, who drew 2-2 in Saturday's Klassiker, remain unbeaten and level on points at the top. Promoted Elversberg produced the weekend's most emphatic result, a 4-1 win over Mainz on Friday, while Leverkusen lost 4-2 at home to Stuttgart.
