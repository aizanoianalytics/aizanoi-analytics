Lenovo and Nvidia promise a 15-day path to an AI factory

Lenovo announced AI Express on 13 October, a quick-start programme with Nvidia that promises order-to-ship in as little as 15 business days for validated AI factory configurations. Three tiers span a small inference system built on two Nvidia RTX 6000 PRO Blackwell Server Edition GPUs, a medium eight-GPU setup, and a large configuration based on HGX B300 for models up to a trillion parameters. Lenovo cites its 2026 CIO Playbook finding that organisations scaling AI expect $2.79 back for every dollar invested.
