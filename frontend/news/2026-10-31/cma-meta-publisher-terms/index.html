UK competition watchdog opens a case on Meta's publisher content terms

The Competition and Markets Authority began an investigation on 30 October into whether Meta's revised commercial terms abuse a dominant position by letting it take content from publishers' websites and use it to develop its own products, including AI features, without businesses getting a choice. Meta had planned to apply the terms on 20 November and has paused them. Meta said there were no grounds for a probe and that it will not change its terms without consulting publishers.
