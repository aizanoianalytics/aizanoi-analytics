Schalke host Leipzig for the first league meeting since their 2023 relegation

Schalke meet RB Leipzig at the Veltins-Arena on Saturday at 15:30 CET, the clubs' first Bundesliga meeting since Leipzig won 4-2 on the final day of 2022-23 before Schalke went down. Schalke are without Bachmann, Gantenbein, Hojlund, Schallenberg, Siebeking, Sylla and Vozar, with Robin Gosens doubtful; Leipzig miss Gebel, Guiu and Ouedraogo and list Christopher Nkunku as doubtful.
