Elversberg thump Mainz 4-1 as Keidel and Campbell light up Friday night

Elversberg beat Mainz 4-1 at the Ursapharm-Arena, Felix Keidel curling in a seventh-minute free-kick, Francis Onyeka scoring his first Bundesliga goal on 26 minutes and Maurice Krattenmacher adding a third in first-half stoppage time. Cole Campbell's stoppage-time scissor kick made it 4-0 before Benedict Hollerbach replied and Mainz's Stefan Posch was sent off for a second booking. Both sides started the night on 10 points from seven games.
