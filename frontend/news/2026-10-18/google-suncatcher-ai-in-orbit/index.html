Google puts a Project Suncatcher prototype satellite into orbit

Google says a Project Suncatcher prototype satellite is now in orbit, advancing a long-term investigation into whether space could host scalable machine-learning infrastructure. The project remains a research moonshot, with hardware survivability, cooling and communications among the practical constraints still to be tested.
