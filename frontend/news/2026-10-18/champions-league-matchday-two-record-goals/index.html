Champions League Matchday 2 produces a 78-goal burst

UEFA’s Matchday 2 round-up records 78 goals across the league-phase programme, including Real Madrid’s 4–3 comeback at Roma, Paris Saint-Germain’s 4–2 win at Manchester City and Liverpool’s 6–3 result at LASK.
