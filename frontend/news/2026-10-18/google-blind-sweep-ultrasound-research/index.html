Google research tests AI-assisted blind-sweep ultrasound screening

Google says researchers worked with Northwestern and Jacarand Health on a blind-sweep ultrasound study, training healthcare workers to collect scans and using machine learning to interpret them. The approach is presented as a way to explore wider access to maternal imaging, not as a replacement for clinical oversight.
