Netherlands edge Serbia 2-1 to top their Nations League group

The Netherlands beat Serbia 2-1 in Eindhoven to move top of Nations League Group A2 on goal difference, closing Xavi Hernandez's first international window in charge unbeaten. Teun Koopmeiners curled in a first-half free kick and substitute Joshua Zirkzee scored the winner from a Tijjani Reijnders cross after a cross from Aleksa Terzic let 19-year-old Mihajlo Cvetkovic equalise. The match marked Virgil van Dijk's 100th cap, and Greece's goalless draw with Germany kept the Dutch top.
