MLS Inter Miami host Atlanta, La Liga opens matchday with Athletic-Atletico as Saturday football slate fills out

Saturday 5 September's football slate spans La Liga, MLS, Serie A and Ligue 1, with Inter Miami CF hosting Atlanta United at Nu Stadium in the late MLS window and Athletic Club hosting Atletico Madrid at San Mames at 19:45 local as the La Liga headliner. Rayo Vallecano meet Racing Santander, Villarreal host Deportivo La Coruna and Fiorentina-Torino open Serie A's Saturday evening before Inter Milan host Napoli on Sunday. MLS comes into the weekend with Inter Miami third in the Eastern Conference at 12-6-4 and Messi top of the goalscoring chart with 18 league strikes.
