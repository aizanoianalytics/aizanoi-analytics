Premier League matchweek 3: seven Saturday fixtures kick off with Newcastle vs Bournemouth at 12:30 BST

The Premier League returns on Saturday 5 September with seven fixtures across matchweek 3, opening at 12:30 BST with Newcastle United hosting AFC Bournemouth at St James' Park before Brentford-Sunderland, Brighton-Leeds, Fulham-Crystal Palace, Manchester City-Coventry and Nottingham Forest-Tottenham kick off at 15:00 BST. Hull City host Aston Villa at 17:30 BST to close the Saturday slate, after Friday's opener saw Liverpool beat Ipswich Town 2-0 with both goals from Alexander Isak. Arsenal-Chelsea and Everton-Man United close the weekend.
