Hisense debuts 116UXS quad-color RGB MiniLED and a world-first FSD LCD architecture at IFA 2026

Hisense unveiled its IFA 2026 lineup in Berlin on 5 September under the theme 'Innovating a Brighter Life', headlined by the 116UXS television with a Chromagic 2.0 quad-color backlight adding a dedicated cyan LED to reach 110 percent of the BT.2020 gamut and 10,000-nit peak brightness. The company also showed a Field Sequential Display LCD architecture eliminating traditional color filters with up to 360 Hz refresh, alongside TÜV Rheinland-certified XR10 and PX4 Pro laser projectors and the 11.2.6-channel UX1 soundbar.
