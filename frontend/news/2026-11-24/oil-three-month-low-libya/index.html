Oil settles at a three-month low as Libyan supply returns

Brent fell 3.46% to $89.37 a barrel and WTI dropped 3.88% to $80.17 on 23 November, their lowest settlements since late August and early August respectively. Libya's Sharara pipeline reopened and crude began flowing to storage at Zawiya, while Middle Eastern exports recovered, outweighing attacks on shipping and a Saudi refinery. Brent was near $89 early on 24 November. Deutsche Bank said observed global inventories rose 52 million barrels in October, implying an oversupply of about 1.7 million barrels a day; JPMorgan sees futures in the high $70s in the first half of 2027.
