Asian chip stocks surge as Huang's return revives the AI trade

Asian equities jumped on 24 November after Nvidia rose 31.5% in New York. MSCI's Asia-Pacific index outside Japan climbed about 4%, South Korea's KOSPI gained more than 6%, Taiwan's benchmark rose 9.1% with TSMC and Foxconn near their daily limits, and Japan's Nikkei added as much as 5% on its return from a holiday. The rebound revived bets on a December Federal Reserve rate increase, lifting front-end Treasury yields and the dollar, while Chinese shares lagged. S&P 500 futures were flat and Nasdaq futures slightly lower.
