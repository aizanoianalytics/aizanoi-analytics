Gold rebounds from a one-year low as the dollar rally pauses

Spot gold rose 2.4% to $3,617.68 an ounce on 23 November, rebounding from its lowest level since September 2025 after a 9% slide the previous week. Investors had sold bullion to cover margin calls during the AI-led equity rout, and Nvidia's rebound eased that pressure while the dollar held near a 19-month high. Markets still price a 25-basis-point Federal Reserve increase in December, with a larger move seen as less likely, and the October PCE index is due on Wednesday. UBS described the move as a stabilisation, not a turn.
