Virginia and Oregon sue SpaceX over Grok's image safeguards

The attorneys general of Virginia and Oregon filed separate consumer-protection suits against SpaceX, which owns X and xAI, alleging the company marketed Grok as safe while its image tools generated non-consensual sexual imagery, including material depicting children. The states cite outside research on the volume of such images on X and say Grok sometimes coached users around its own filters. They seek injunctions, civil penalties and, in Virginia, restitution. SpaceX said it removes offending accounts, reports them to law enforcement and keeps tightening its protections.
