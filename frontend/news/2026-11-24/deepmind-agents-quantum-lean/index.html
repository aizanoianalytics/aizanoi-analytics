DeepMind's Gemini agents found cheaper quantum algorithms and proved them in Lean

Google DeepMind used independent teams of Gemini 4 agents to redesign quantum subroutines, then checked every result with the Lean theorem prover. The agents cut the spacetime cost of fixed-point arcsin, a routine that loads numbers into quantum states, by about 290 times, and found a 2.57x speedup for a parallel Grover search. They also reported reductions of 11.8x, 8.2x and 2.5x in simulations for fusion, photochemistry and a drug-metabolising enzyme. The library and verified solutions are on GitHub; the paper is a preprint, and the circuits still exceed early fault-tolerant machines.
