Galatasaray face Aston Villa without Osimhen and Leao

Galatasaray host Aston Villa in Istanbul on Tuesday with Victor Osimhen out through a knee injury, Rafael Leao sidelined by a hamstring problem and Roland Sallai also unavailable, leaving Leroy Sane and Baris Alper Yilmaz to carry the attack. The Turkish side have won five straight and sit 18th in the league phase; Villa are sixth on nine points after a 3-0 defeat at Barcelona. Matty Cash is a doubt under concussion protocol, while Amadou Onana and Matteo Ruggeri remain out. Kick-off is 18:45 CET.
