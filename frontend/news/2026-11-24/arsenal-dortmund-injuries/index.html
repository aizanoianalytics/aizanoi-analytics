Arsenal meet Dortmund without Raya, Saka and Zubimendi

Arsenal host Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday with David Raya, Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi injured, leaving Kepa Arrizabalaga to start in goal. Arsenal are third on 10 points and unbeaten in 16 Champions League league-phase matches; Dortmund are 12th on nine and top of the Bundesliga after a 1-0 win over Gladbach. Serhou Guirassy is a doubt, while Emre Can and Giannis Konstantelias are out. Arsenal loanee Ethan Nwaneri is eligible for Dortmund but is expected to begin on the bench at the Emirates.
