DeepMind's Gemini agents found cheaper quantum algorithms and proved them in Lean

Google DeepMind used independent teams of Gemini 4 agents to redesign quantum subroutines, then checked every result with the Lean theorem prover. The agents cut the spacetime cost of fixed-point arcsin, a routine that loads numbers into quantum states, by about 290 times, and found a 2.57x speedup for a parallel Grover search.
