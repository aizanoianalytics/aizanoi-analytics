Football

City host Napoli with De Bruyne back at the Etihad and no room for another slip

Manchester City welcome Napoli on Tuesday after four Premier League games without a win, including a 1-1 draw with Fulham. City are seventh in the Champions League on nine points; Napoli are 27th with three and have named a heavily depleted travelling squad. Kevin De Bruyne returns to the Etihad as a Napoli player, and Erling Haaland can reach 40 Champions League goals for City in his 43rd appearance. Matheus Nunes is available again for the hosts, while Antoine Semenyo remains out.
