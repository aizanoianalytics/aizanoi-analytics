Economy / Markets

Warsh opens door to a Fed rate hike as Jackson Hole keynote flags 'more concerning' inflation

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh used his first Jackson Hole keynote on Friday to warn that inflation remains too high and that policymakers have 'work to do' if underlying price pressures do not ease, sending the two-year Treasury yield to 4.31 percent, its highest since late July. CME FedWatch put the implied probability of a quarter-point September hike at about 59.5 percent after the speech, up from 35.4 percent a day earlier, as the chair declined to commit to forward guidance and instead argued for a 'quieter Fed' more deliberate in its communications.

Anthropic opens research preview of Model Hardware Standard for AI agents in the lab

Anthropic on Wednesday published a research preview of its Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a shared driver and tagging specification that lets AI agents operate microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms and other lab instruments through a single Model Context Protocol interface. Early partners include Amazon Web Services Strands Robots, Hugging Face's LeRobot library, Raspberry Pi, Automata and Universal Robots, with Anthropic describing the standard as model-agnostic and intended to become open source once safety evaluations with the founding cohort complete.

Nvidia agrees to buy open-source hub Hugging Face for 12.9 billion dollars

Nvidia has agreed to acquire Hugging Face, the open-source model and dataset repository, for roughly 12.9 billion dollars, The Information reported late Wednesday citing a person familiar with the deal. CISA confirms more than 100 US water systems were targeted in July cyberattacks

The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency disclosed this week that it observed malicious activity targeting over 100 internet-exposed systems in the Water and Wastewater Systems sector during July, with attackers most often gaining entry through programmable logic controllers wired directly to cellular modems. CISA urged utilities to move remote access behind VPNs or gateways, audit which assets really need internet connectivity and enable multi-factor authentication, while continuing to offer free vulnerability scanning and on-site support.

AI infrastructure spending is outgrowing the GPU, analyst brief argues
