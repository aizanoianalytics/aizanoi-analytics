Apple quietly upgrades recent Mac mini orders to M6 and M5 Pro for free

Apple has begun upgrading qualifying recent Mac mini orders to its new M6 and M5 Pro configurations at no extra cost, 9to5Mac reports, citing customers who received shipping notifications for the higher-spec machines after initially ordering the M5 or M4 base models. The free upgrades, which Apple is applying without an official statement, cover orders placed in the days surrounding the M6 Mac mini announcement last week and follow the same quiet approach Apple has used in past transitions when new silicon shipped ahead of demand planning.
