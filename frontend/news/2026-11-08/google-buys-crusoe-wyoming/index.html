Google takes over Crusoe's stalled Wyoming data-centre campus

Crusoe confirmed it sold an unfinished data-centre project in southeastern Wyoming to Google, which plans to invest $17 billion to develop the campus after concerns about cost and construction timeline under Crusoe's management. The project was announced last year with Tallgrass and designed to scale to 10 gigawatts of capacity. Google did not disclose the purchase price and remains a Crusoe customer on a separate site near Amarillo, Texas.
