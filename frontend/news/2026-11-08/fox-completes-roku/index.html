Fox closes its $22 billion purchase of Roku six months early

Fox completed its $22 billion acquisition of Roku, a deal announced in June and originally projected to finish in the first half of 2027. Fox says the combined company is now the third-largest US television business by viewership and gains access to more than 100 million households, which it expects to use for advertising targeting and to reduce reliance on traditional distribution. The Justice Department had issued a second request for information two months before closing.
