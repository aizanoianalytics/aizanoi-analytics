Real Madrid complete a three-goal finish to stun Roma

Real Madrid recovered from two goals down to win 4-3 at Roma, scoring three times in the closing stages through Carlos Espi, Endrick and Antonio Rudiger. Donyell Malen's double had put Jose Mourinho's side 3-1 ahead against his former club, after Kylian Mbappe marked his 100th Champions League appearance by cancelling out Marten de Roon's opener. Endrick was named player of the match.
