NEURA Robotics gives embodied AI a multisensory foundation model

NEURA Robotics introduced Neuron AI, a multimodal foundation model that fuses 3D vision, touch, force sensing, audio, robot state and language with memory in a single perception-language-action architecture. The Metzingen-based humanoid maker says the model runs locally on the robot, keeps production data under the customer's control and lets operators fine-tune their own skills. NEURA plans to show its 4NE-1 humanoid completing an eleven-minute laundry task unaided at Nvidia GTC Berlin on 21 and 22 October.
