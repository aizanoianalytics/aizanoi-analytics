Champions League matchday two sets a 78-goal record

Champions League matchday two produced 78 goals, a record for a single round of the competition, with 46 of them arriving on Wednesday alone. Six of the nine fixtures featured five goals or more. Liverpool won 6-3 at LASK as Bradley Barcola scored a hat-trick, Feyenoord came from behind to beat Como 3-2 in Rotterdam for their first points of the campaign, and Stuttgart won 3-0 at Slovan Bratislava to make it two wins from two.
