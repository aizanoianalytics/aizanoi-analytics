Viking hold Bayern after Olise red card in Stavanger

Bayern Munich were held to a 3-3 draw at Viking in the Champions League on Tuesday after winger Michael Olise was sent off in the 74th minute, booked and then shown a second yellow for throwing the ball at an opponent. Viking led 2-0 at half-time before Harry Kane scored twice and set up Luis Diaz for a 3-2 lead. Herman Haugen equalised in the 90th minute. Olise will miss next week’s home meeting with Arsenal.
