Tresoldi hat-trick stuns Inter as Club Brugge win 4-3

Club Brugge beat Inter Milan 4-3 at San Siro on Tuesday with a hat-trick from 22-year-old forward Nicolo Tresoldi, leaving the Italian champions without a point after two Champions League matchdays. Tresoldi scored from the penalty spot and then twice more as Brugge punished defensive errors. Lautaro Martinez scored twice and Ange-Yoan Bonny added a late third for Inter, whose former goalkeeper Yann Sommer returned to Milan in the Brugge goal.
