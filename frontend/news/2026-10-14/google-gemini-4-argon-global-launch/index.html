Google opens Gemini 4 Argon to developers and subscribers worldwide

Google released Gemini 4 Argon, the first model in its Gemini 4 family, to developers, enterprises and paying consumers on 13 October. The DeepMind model targets multi-step “deep work” across coding, finance, law, cybersecurity and science, and ranks first on the Vals Index. API pricing starts at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input 95 percent off; those introductory rates double after 30 November. Antigravity coding access stays limited to Ultra subscribers.
