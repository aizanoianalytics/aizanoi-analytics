Lewandowski hat-trick as Poland rout Romania 6-0

Robert Lewandowski scored a hat-trick as Poland thumped Romania 6-0 in Warsaw, a win built on a 22nd-minute penalty after Romania's Radu Dragusin was sent off for bringing the striker down. Piotr Zielinski, Jan Bednarek and an own goal completed the scoring. The result tightens Group B1, where Northern Ireland also impressed, beating Ukraine 3-0 to stay top without having conceded a goal in three matches.
