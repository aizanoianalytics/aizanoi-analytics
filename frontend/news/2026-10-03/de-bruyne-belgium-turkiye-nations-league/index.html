De Bruyne double sends Belgium past Turkiye in the Nations League

Kevin De Bruyne scored twice as Belgium beat Turkiye 3-0 in Liege, moving to six points in UEFA Nations League Group A1 and leaving the visitors bottom of the group without a point after three matches. Belgium's win keeps them a point behind France ahead of the two neighbours' meeting in Paris on Monday. The result compounds a difficult window for Turkiye, who have now lost three group games in a row.
