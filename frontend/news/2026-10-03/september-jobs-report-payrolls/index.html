US hiring almost stalls as September payrolls rise just 29,000

US employers added 29,000 jobs in September, far below the roughly 84,000 economists expected, and the unemployment rate rose to 4.2% from 4.1%, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported. July and August were revised down by a combined 60,000. Average hourly earnings rose 0.1% on the month and 3.0% over the year, the slowest annual pace in years. Health care, construction and manufacturing added jobs while government and financial activities shed them. The soft report reduced the odds of a Fed rate rise this month.
