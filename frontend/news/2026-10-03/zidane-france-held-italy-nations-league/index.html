Italy spoil Zidane's first home game as France are held in Paris

Zinedine Zidane's first home match in charge of France ended in a 1-1 Nations League draw with Italy at the Stade de France. Michael Olise put the hosts ahead early in the second half, but Alessandro Bastoni equalised and France finished with ten men after Dayot Upamecano was sent off for a second bookable offence. France stay top of Group A1 on seven points, with Belgium a point behind before Monday's meeting in Paris; Italy have four.
