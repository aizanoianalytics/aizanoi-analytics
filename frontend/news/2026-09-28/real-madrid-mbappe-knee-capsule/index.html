Real Madrid confirm Mbappe has a hyperextension of the posterior capsule in his left knee

Real Madrid's medical services said tests carried out on 26 September diagnosed Kylian Mbappe with a hyperextension affecting the posterior capsule of his left knee. The club's medical report described the injury as awaiting evolution and gave no recovery timetable or estimated return date, leaving the length of his absence from the club's LaLiga and Champions League schedule unstated.
