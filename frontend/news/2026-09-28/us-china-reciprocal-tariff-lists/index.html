White House publishes reciprocal tariff lists as the US-China truce runs to 10 January

The White House has published 30-for-30 product lists under the US-China Board of Trade, naming the goods each side will consider for reduced tariff treatment on a reciprocal basis. Reporting on the lists says the two countries agreed to cut tariffs on about $30 billion of imports each, focused on non-sensitive goods. China's commerce ministry confirmed the truce extension runs to 10 January and said the reciprocal cuts would be discussed under a new trade council.
