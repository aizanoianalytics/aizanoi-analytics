Parma part company with head coach Carlos Cuesta after a shared decision

Parma said on Sunday 27 September that Carlos Cuesta has left his post as the club's head coach. The Serie A club called it a shared decision, saying it and the coach had concluded that the conditions needed to carry the sporting project forward were no longer there, and that Monday's training session would be taken by its technical staff while it looks for a replacement.
