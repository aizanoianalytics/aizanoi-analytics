Champions League league phase draw takes place in Monaco

UEFA holds the league phase draw for the 2026-27 Champions League in Monaco from 18:00 CET on Thursday Aug. 27, allocating 36 clubs into eight matchdays across the new single-league format. The fixture list with kick-off times is due to be published no later than Saturday Aug. 29, with matchday one starting on Tuesday Sept. 8.
