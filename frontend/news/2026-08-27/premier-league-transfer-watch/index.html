Premier League clubs finalise moves as the transfer window narrows

Premier League clubs continued to finalise squad moves through Aug. 26, with Aston Villa announcing Ezri Konsa's move to Arsenal and Aaron Wan-Bissaka's loan to West Ham, alongside Coventry City's additions of Taiwo Awoniyi from Nottingham Forest and Gustavo Hamer from Sheffield United. The summer window closes at 23:00 UK time on Sept. 1, with most clubs still working on at least one late deal before the deadline.
