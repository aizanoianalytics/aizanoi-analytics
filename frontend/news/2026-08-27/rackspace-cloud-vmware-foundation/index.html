Rackspace Cloud launches on VMware Cloud Foundation 9.1

Rackspace Technology launched Rackspace Cloud, a fully managed multitenant platform built on Broadcom's VMware Cloud Foundation 9.1, with initial availability in Dallas followed by Northern Virginia and Chicago. The company frames the launch around demand for governed private infrastructure, citing Broadcom research that 56 percent of organisations are now running or planning production AI inferencing in a private cloud.
