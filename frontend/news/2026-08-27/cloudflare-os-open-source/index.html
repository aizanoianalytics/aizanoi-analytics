Cloudflare open-sources Cloudflare OS for corporate AI apps

Cloudflare released Cloudflare OS under the Apache 2.0 licence, packaging a capability-based security model on top of its open-source workerd runtime and V8 isolates. Each user gets their own copy of every generated application, with Gatekeeper-mediated access to enterprise resources, and the company says its own staff built more than 4,000 internal tools in the 30 days before the public release.
