Gemini Robotics 2.5 lifts task success by up to 100 percent, scores 96 percent at Mercedes

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5 on 10 November, a vision-language-action model it says completes tasks 30 to 100 percent more often, and 20 to 30 percent faster, than its previous baselines. In a proof-of-concept on Mercedes-Benz lines in Kecskemét and Sindelfingen, an Apptronik Apollo 2 humanoid sorted metal brackets into sequence trays with a 96 percent success rate. Access is limited to robotics partners, and DeepMind published an adversarial safety benchmark alongside the model.
