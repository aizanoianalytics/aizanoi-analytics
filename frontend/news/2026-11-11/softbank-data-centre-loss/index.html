SoftBank swings to a $7 billion loss on US data-centre spending

SoftBank Group reported a net loss of 1.115 trillion yen, about $7 billion, for the quarter to September, reversing a 2.5 trillion yen profit a year earlier. The result included a 597.2 billion yen special loss after procurement plans changed for power-generation equipment destined for American data-centre projects, including a large development in Pike County, Ohio. SoftBank said it may recover as much as $2.1 billion through rebates on future purchases of the same equipment.
