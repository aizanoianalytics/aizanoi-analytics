Wall Street slips as a hot October CPI hardens bets on a December hike

US stocks fell on 10 November after consumer prices rose 0.4 percent in October, above the 0.3 percent economists expected and cooler than September's 0.6 percent gain. The S&P 500 lost 0.38 percent to 7,520.93, the Nasdaq Composite 0.74 percent to 25,065.87 and the Dow 0.30 percent to 52,231.89. Traders priced an 84 percent chance of a quarter-point Federal Reserve increase next month, and Nvidia fell more than 5 percent as an investigation into its chief executive weighed on the stock.
