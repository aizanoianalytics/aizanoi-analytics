Anthropic sets a $70 to $78 IPO range and aims for a trillion-dollar listing

Anthropic filed terms on 10 November for an initial public offering of 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each, a range that would raise $57.75 billion to $64.35 billion and list the company on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The filing implies a market value of roughly $917 billion to $1.02 trillion on its post-offering share count, or up to about $1.41 trillion fully diluted. Seven chip and cloud partners agreed to buy $26 billion of Class N stock privately at the offer price.
