Yamal sits out Spain's trip to Czechia with England in view

Lamine Yamal will miss Spain's Nations League match in the Czech Republic on 12 November after reporting discomfort in his right knee, the Spanish federation said, but he is expected to be available against England in Madrid on 15 November. Mikel Oyarzabal also stayed behind with flu. Coach Luis de la Fuente said Yamal could have played in Prague but that it was not worth taking any risk before the England game.
