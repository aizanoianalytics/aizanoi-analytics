Amazon swaps a frontier model for a small decider and cuts ticket costs 98 percent

Amazon replaced Claude Sonnet 4.6 with Strands Decider, a small Gemma 4-based decision model, on a high-volume support routing task and reported equal or better results at about $0.0005 per ticket instead of $0.0222. Its engineers described the switch on 10 November: input tokens per ticket fell from 6,554 to 2,009 and output from 170 to one, taking the cost of a million tickets from roughly $22,200 to $500. The model runs on a single SageMaker instance.
