New York opens RAISE registration for large frontier AI developers

New York opened a registration portal on 10 November for the large frontier AI developers covered by its RAISE Act, which takes effect on 1 January 2027. Covered companies must publish safety frameworks, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours and file quarterly catastrophic-risk assessments, with fines of up to $1 million for a first violation and $3 million afterwards. A new DIGIT office inside the Department of Financial Services will supervise them, and the state launched a research institute at Binghamton University.
