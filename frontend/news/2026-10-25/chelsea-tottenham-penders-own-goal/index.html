A goalkeeper's own goal hands Spurs a rare win at Chelsea

Tottenham won 2-0 at Stamford Bridge on 24 October, their first Premier League victory there since April 2018. Chelsea's stand-in goalkeeper Mike Penders punched a corner into his own net in the 77th minute under pressure from Jan Paul van Hecke, Cole Palmer collected a second yellow card three minutes later, and Mathys Tel settled it with a solo goal in the 87th. Penders is only the second Chelsea goalkeeper to score an own goal in the competition.
