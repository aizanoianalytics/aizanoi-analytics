Dortmund go top overnight as Beier strikes late again

Maximilian Beier curled in an 80th-minute winner as Borussia Dortmund beat Eintracht Frankfurt 1-0 at Signal Iduna Park on 24 October, lifting them back above Bayern Munich overnight. Substitute Jobe Bellingham cut the ball back for the finish, Dortmund's sixth consecutive goal after the 70th minute, and the win ended two straight league draws before Der Klassiker. Eintracht had the better of a goalless first half without taking it.
