Jackson's double ends City's perfect start

Nicolas Jackson scored twice as Aston Villa beat Manchester City 2-1 at Villa Park on 24 October, ending the leaders' run of seven straight league wins. Jackson punished Marc Guehi's loose touch inside six minutes, Erling Haaland headed an equaliser from Iliman Ndiaye's cross, and Jackson restored the lead on the hour when Gianluigi Donnarumma let a low shot slip under him. Villa climb to sixth on 11 points; City stay top on 21.
