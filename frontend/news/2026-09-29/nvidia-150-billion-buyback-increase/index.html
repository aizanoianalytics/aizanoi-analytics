Nvidia adds $150 billion to its buyback authorisation

Nvidia's board of directors authorised an additional $150 billion under the company's existing share repurchase programme, lifting the total remaining authorisation to $235 billion. The company framed the increase as a statement of confidence in the cash its AI business generates, at a moment when rising memory costs and growing customer interest in custom silicon are pressing on gross margins.
