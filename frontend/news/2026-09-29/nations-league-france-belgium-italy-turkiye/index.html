Olise settles France in Brussels as Italy run up four in Bursa

Michael Olise came off the bench to score two minutes from time and give France a 1-0 win in Belgium, keeping Zinedine Zidane's record at two wins from two in Nations League Group A1 and leaving Les Bleus three points clear. Italy answered their opening defeat with a 4-1 win in Turkiye, leading 3-0 inside 27 minutes through Alessandro Bastoni, Davide Frattesi and debutant Michael Kayode before Francesco Pio Esposito added the fourth after Baris Alper Yilmaz pulled one back.
