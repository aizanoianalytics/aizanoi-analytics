England travel to Czechia with their Nations League group already slipping

Matchday two closes on Tuesday with England away to Czechia and Spain hosting Croatia in League A Group 3, after England lost 2-3 to Spain at Wembley and Czechia went down 1-2 to Croatia in their opener. Ten matches across the four leagues are scheduled, including Scotland against Switzerland and Slovenia against North Macedonia. Czechia and England kick off at 20:45 local time in Prague.
