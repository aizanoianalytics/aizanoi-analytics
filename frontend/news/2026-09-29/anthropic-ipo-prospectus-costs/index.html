Anthropic's IPO prospectus pairs trillion-dollar ambition with steep losses

Anthropic's IPO prospectus, reported by Reuters, sets sweeping AI ambitions against the cost of pursuing them: revenue of $4.59 billion alongside a heavy operating loss, and long-term cloud, compute and infrastructure commitments of roughly $518 billion. The filing is also unusual for what it tells prospective investors about risk, warning that the company's technology could pose existential risks to humanity. A successful listing at the valuations under discussion would test how much public-market appetite remains for the spending underpinning the AI build-out.
