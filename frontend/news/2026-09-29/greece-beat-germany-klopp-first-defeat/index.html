Greece hand Klopp his first defeat in charge of Germany

Dimitris Kourbelis came off the bench to score the only goal in the 74th minute, a low strike that deflected off captain Joshua Kimmich, giving Greece a 1-0 win in Augsburg and a first victory over Germany in 11 meetings between the sides. Germany held most of the ball but could not convert it, leaving the hosts with one point from two Group A2 matches and Jurgen Klopp without a win in his first two games, having drawn with the Netherlands in stoppage time.
