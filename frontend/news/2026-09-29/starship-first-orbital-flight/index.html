Starship's first orbital flight lifts SpaceX's next-generation Starlinks

SpaceX's Starship reached orbit for the first time in a full ascent profile and released a batch of next-generation Starlink satellites, moving the vehicle past a milestone it had previously only approached. The flight gives the company an orbital debut for the larger satellites it needs to keep expanding its broadband constellation.
