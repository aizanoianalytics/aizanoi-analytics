Scotland earn first win under Pocognoli as Robertson hits 100 caps

Scotland won 2-0 in North Macedonia for their first victory under head coach Sebastien Pocognoli, with Kieron Bowie scoring his first international goal and John McGinn adding a second-half penalty. Captain Andy Robertson became only the second Scotland player to reach 100 caps. Scotland finished with 10 men after Scott McKenna's straight red card, their second dismissal in a week, but held on to claim the three points.
