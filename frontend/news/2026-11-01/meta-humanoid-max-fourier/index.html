Meta built a humanoid prototype with China's Fourier, report says

The Information reported on Saturday that Meta assembled a humanoid, internally codenamed Max, with Chinese startup Fourier in about three months. The roughly 1.5-metre machine uses Nvidia's Jetson Thor chip and carries a display that mimics facial expressions, speakers and five-fingered hands for small objects. A second prototype began in September with a redesigned torso and stair sensors. Meta told the outlet the work is internal research, with no plans to sell units.
