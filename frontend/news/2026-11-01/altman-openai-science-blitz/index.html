Altman says OpenAI will keep publishing faster than academia can check

OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman said on the Cheeky Pint podcast that humanity deserves fundamental knowledge and the company should not sit on a major discovery, confirming it holds more unpublished results. He conceded the push had "got out of control" at times. Researchers described the effect to Wired in blunter terms: UC Santa Barbara's Daniel Lokshtanov called it "a giant bomb dropped into the middle of the field", and much of the output remains unpeer-reviewed.
