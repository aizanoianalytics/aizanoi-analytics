Ten-man United lose at Chelsea as Carrick's winless run reaches nine

Morgan Rogers ran through a static United defence to open the scoring five minutes after half-time, and Cole Palmer converted a penalty on 64 after Lisandro Martínez collected a second booking for raising an arm to Wesley Fofana. Chelsea won 2-0, extending United's winless run in all competitions to nine matches, their longest since 1984, and leaving Michael Carrick's position under renewed scrutiny.
