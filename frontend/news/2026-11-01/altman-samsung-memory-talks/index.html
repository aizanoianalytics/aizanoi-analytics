Altman takes OpenAI's memory squeeze to Samsung's boardroom

OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman met about 50 Samsung Group executives in Yongin, south of Seoul, on Saturday, with memory supply and AI infrastructure on the agenda alongside a wider session on how AI may reshape their industries. Samsung said the talks build on earlier cooperation to supply memory for OpenAI's Stargate build-out. Memory accounts for roughly a third of data-centre spending, and shortages across memory categories are pushing chip prices higher.
