Torino's third straight win spoils Juric's return

Torino beat Monza 3-2 for a third consecutive Serie A victory, their first such run since 2019-20, and climbed to eighth. Kian Fitz-Jim opened the scoring before half-time, Gustavo Varela equalised two minutes after the restart following a Pietro Comuzzo error, and Rafik Belghali curled in a superb strike on 61 minutes. Nikola Vlašić added a third, and Nathan Patterson's stoppage-time own goal only narrowed the margin. Monza, now coached by former Torino boss Ivan Jurić, are 14th.
