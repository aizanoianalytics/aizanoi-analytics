Ipswich punish Forest's short backpass to take a point

Nottingham Forest's wait for a second win under Oliver Glasner goes on after a 1-1 draw at Ipswich, where Neco Williams' back-post volley from Daniel Muñoz's cross was cancelled out four minutes before the break. Nikola Milenković dwelt on the ball and under-hit his backpass, letting Julio Enciso collect and slot past Matz Sels for his first Ipswich goal since arriving in the summer. Glasner called a second-half challenge on James McAtee a 'clear red card'; the referee gave a yellow after review.
