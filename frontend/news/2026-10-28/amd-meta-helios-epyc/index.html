AMD says Helios racks and EPYC are now running inside Meta

AMD says its Helios rack-scale systems are running at Meta, that EPYC processors power Meta's Muse agent, and that its hardware has day-one support for Meta's open models. The two companies presented the arrangement at AMD's own newsroom as a deepening of years of co-design across EPYC, Instinct GPUs, Pensando networking and ROCm software. The notable claim is not another accelerator win but that AMD's CPUs sit inside a large customer's consumer-facing agent, the workload Intel has long owned.
