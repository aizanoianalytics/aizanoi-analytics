Fleetwood knock the champions out of the Carabao Cup on penalties

Fleetwood Town, 84 places below Arsenal in the pyramid and in the bottom half of League Two, knocked the Premier League champions out of the Carabao Cup, winning 5-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw at Highbury Stadium. Aaron Loupalo-Bi scored in the 21st minute, Viktor Gyokeres equalised after the break, and Arsenal's 16-year-old Max Dowman was sent off before Jay Lynch saved Bruno Guimaraes' kick and Conor Haughey converted the winner. Mikel Arteta called it a very sad day.
