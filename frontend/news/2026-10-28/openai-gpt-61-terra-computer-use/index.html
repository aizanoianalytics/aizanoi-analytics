OpenAI halves the price of computer use with GPT-6.1 Terra

OpenAI has replaced GPT-5.6 Terra with a faster, cheaper model built around computer use. GPT-6.1 Terra can read what is on a screen, click through applications and type into them, and it ships at $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens, half the standard price of the model it replaces. OpenAI says Terra matches its larger GPT-6 Astra at a tenth of the per-token cost and finishes tasks more than three times faster. It is rolling out in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API.
