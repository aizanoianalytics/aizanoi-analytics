Manchester United keep Carrick for Chelsea after eight games without a win

Manchester United will keep Michael Carrick in charge for Saturday's trip to Chelsea, BBC Sport reported, despite a run of eight matches without a win in all competitions and a 3-0 home defeat by Bournemouth that left the club 16th, one point above the relegation zone. United's hierarchy has looked at Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann, but Carrick will be given the chance to turn the season around, with Roma in the Champions League and Aston Villa to follow.
