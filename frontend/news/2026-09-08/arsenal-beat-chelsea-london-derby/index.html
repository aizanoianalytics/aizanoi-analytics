Arsenal comeback beats Chelsea 2-1 to stay perfect in Premier League

Defending champions Arsenal came from behind at the Emirates to beat Chelsea 2-1 on September 6 and stretch their Premier League start to three wins from three. Morgan Rogers, a Chelsea summer signing, put the visitors ahead after 77 seconds before Kai Havertz equalised from the edge of the box and Martin Ødegaard finished a Christos Tzolis pass via a Havertz dummy to complete the turnaround. The match is being read as an early marker on Arsenal's title credentials and on Chelsea's challenger potential under new manager Liam Rosenior.
