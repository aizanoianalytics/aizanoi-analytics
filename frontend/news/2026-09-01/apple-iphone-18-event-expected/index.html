Apple's September 2026 keynote expected around September 8 or 9, with iPhone 18 Pro and a foldable iPhone

Multiple outlets now expect Apple to hold its fall hardware keynote on September 8 or 9, 2026, with the line-up limited to the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max and Apple's first foldable iPhone, while the standard iPhone 18 and a budget iPhone 18e slip to spring 2027. The Pro models are tipped to run the A20 Pro chip on TSMC's 2nm process and to start at roughly $1,299, with the foldable expected to cross $2,000 and to carry Touch ID instead of Face ID.
