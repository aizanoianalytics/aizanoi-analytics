UEFA Champions League league phase fixtures confirmed after Monaco draw, with matchday 1 kicking off September 8-10

UEFA held the 2026-27 Champions League league phase draw in Monaco on Thursday, August 27, setting the fixtures for the 36-team format that begins with matchday 1 on September 8-10, 2026. The line-up includes Pot 1 seeds Paris Saint-Germain, Barcelona, Manchester City, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, Liverpool, Inter and Atletico Madrid, with AEK Athens, Bodø/Glimt, Sabah and Viking among the first-time or returning clubs at this stage.
