Newcastle reject Tottenham's £80m bid for Sandro Tonali as Magpies hold out for closer to £100m

Newcastle United turned down a bid from Tottenham Hotspur worth about £80 million for Italy midfielder Sandro Tonali earlier in the week, with the Magpies believed to be holding out for a fee closer to £100m as Manchester City and Arsenal also monitor the 26-year-old. Tonali, who joined Newcastle from AC Milan three years ago for £55m, returned to selection in August 2024 after serving a 10-month ban for breaching FA betting rules.
