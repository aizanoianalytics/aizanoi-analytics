Premier League transfer deadline day 2026 arrives with Chelsea, Tottenham and Manchester City leading summer outlay

English clubs had until 23:00 BST on Tuesday, September 1 to complete their summer 2026 business, with the Premier League's most expensive signing so far being France winger Bradley Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain to Liverpool in a package worth about $162 million, followed by Chelsea's British-record $158 million move for Morgan Rogers from Aston Villa. Top-flight clubs can still complete signings after the deadline via a two-hour grace period if they have filed a deal sheet in time, and free-agent signings remain possible throughout the season.
