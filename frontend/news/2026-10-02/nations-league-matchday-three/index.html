Klopp's Germany break through as Portugal surge clear

Jürgen Klopp claimed his first win as Germany coach at the third attempt, a 2-0 Nations League victory over Serbia in Munich built on goals from Tom Bischof and Florian Wirtz. Portugal won 4-2 in Copenhagen to make it three wins from three and move six points clear at the top of their group, while Wales came from behind to beat ten-man Norway 2-1 in Cardiff for their first win in the top tier of the competition. Greece and the Netherlands drew 2-2 in the other League A fixture.
