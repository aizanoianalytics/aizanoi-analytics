Consumer AI still has few paying customers, and the maths is unforgiving

An analysis of Andreessen Horowitz and PNC data published by TechCrunch found that as of May only 2.2% of consumers were paying for AI services, spending an average of $31 a month. Taking Netflix as a benchmark for a saturated online service, the piece calculated that 325 million subscribers at about $34 each would yield roughly $11 billion a year, less than a third of OpenAI's operating costs. Major labs have pivoted toward enterprise contracts, and OpenAI's enterprise bookings have reportedly doubled since July.
