Manchester City and Paris share the goals as the Women's Champions League builds

Manchester City drew 1-1 with Real Madrid at the Joie Stadium, Khadija Shaw equalising six minutes after half-time after Linda Caicedo had put the visitors ahead, with a late Real Madrid finish ruled out because the referee had stopped play. Paris Saint-Germain beat OH Leuven 5-0 at Campus PSG, Naomie Feller scoring twice and setting up two more, with Rasheedat Ajibade, Sakina Karchaoui and Vitória Yaya also on the scoresheet.
