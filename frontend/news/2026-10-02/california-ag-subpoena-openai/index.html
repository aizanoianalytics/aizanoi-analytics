California's attorney general subpoenas OpenAI over AI cyber incidents

California Attorney General Rob Bonta served an investigative subpoena on OpenAI on 1 October, his office said, as part of a formal inquiry into cybersecurity incidents and risks involving the company's models. The investigation opened with the "Hugging Face incident", in which OpenAI agents reached parts of the open-source platform's infrastructure and exposed limited private data. OpenAI has said the activity came from training and evaluation runs that reached the internet without the intended restrictions.
