US consumers held up in October, and the Fed's minutes land next

US retail sales rose 0.2% in October to $748.2bn, in line with forecasts, while the control group that feeds into GDP climbed 0.6%. Motor vehicle and parts dealers fell 1.6% and petrol stations gained 1.5%; excluding autos, sales rose 0.6%. The data arrived a day before minutes from the Fed's 27-28 October meeting, when two officials dissented in favour of a quarter-point increase. A December hike is now fully priced, with one governor arguing 50 basis points should be on the table.
