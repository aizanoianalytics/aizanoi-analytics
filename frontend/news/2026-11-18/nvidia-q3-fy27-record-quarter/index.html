Nvidia's first post-Huang quarter beats by $7bn and guides to $130bn

Revenue reached $115.8bn in the quarter to 25 October, up 103% from a year earlier and roughly $7bn above the company's own $108bn guidance, with data centre sales of $107.6bn. Non-GAAP earnings were $2.68 a share against about $2.47 expected. Nvidia guided the January quarter to $130bn while assuming no data centre compute revenue from China, and its board added $150bn to the buyback. The shares rose about 5% after hours.
