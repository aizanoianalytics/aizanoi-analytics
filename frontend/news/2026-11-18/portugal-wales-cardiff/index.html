Portugal win in Cardiff to take the seeded route through the last eight

Portugal beat Wales 3-2 at Cardiff City Stadium to finish top of League A Group 4 with 15 points and a seeded place in the Nations League quarter-finals. Bruno Fernandes put the visitors ahead with a strike from outside the penalty area; David Brooks equalised for Wales, and Portugal still closed out the win. Wales end the group with three points and enter the League A/B play-offs, where they will be seeded and play the second leg at home.
