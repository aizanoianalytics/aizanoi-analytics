Anthropic prices into a market that has stopped paying up for AI

Anthropic sets the price of its Nasdaq offering on Wednesday evening, with trading due to begin on 19 November under the symbol ANT. It is selling 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78, which would raise up to $64.4bn and value the company near $1.4tn on a fully diluted basis, with as much as a fifth reserved for retail buyers. Orders closed on Tuesday with demand reported at about seven times the shares on offer, even as the Nasdaq has fallen roughly 5% in a month.
