OpenAI splits GPT-6.2 into a frontier model and an everyday one

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 in two tiers: Astra, built for hard professional and scientific work at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, and Sol, an everyday agentic model at $2 and $10. The company says Astra costs less than a third of GPT-6 per task, while Sol lands near a fifth of Astra's cost on coding. Artificial Analysis ranks Astra second on its intelligence index, behind Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2, and found no gain on its coding agent benchmark.
