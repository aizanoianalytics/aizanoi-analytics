OpenAI hands NATO's cyber defenders its Daybreak models

OpenAI said NATO's core institutions now use its Daybreak cyber models through the alliance's communications and information agency, with usage reaching up to 300 billion tokens a month. The disclosed work covers reviewing software, investigating suspicious activity, validating vulnerabilities and testing fixes. NATO's Crossed Swords exercise in Tallinn this month ran a stream in which participants directed AI agents under human oversight. EU and national cyber agencies, and Ukraine's digital ministry, also hold Daybreak access.
