Haaland's stoppage-time penalty sends Norway through in Copenhagen

Norway beat Denmark 3-2 at Parken after trailing 2-0 inside 14 minutes, with Erling Haaland scoring twice, the second a penalty deep into stoppage time after a handball by Joachim Andersen. Martin Odegaard had equalised in the 83rd minute. Norway finish second in League A Group 4 on nine points, level with Denmark but ahead on their head-to-head record, and go to the quarter-finals. Denmark drop into the League A/B play-offs.
