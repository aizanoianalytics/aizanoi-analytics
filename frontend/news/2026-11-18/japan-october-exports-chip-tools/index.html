Japan's chip-tool exports jump 49.7% and carry a record trade month

Japan's exports rose 20.5% in October to a record ¥11.77tn, a fourteenth consecutive monthly gain, as semiconductor manufacturing equipment and chip shipments each climbed 49.7%. Exports to Taiwan rose 42.7% and to the United States 23.9%. Imports grew 21.6% as the value of crude oil purchases jumped 60.5% on slightly lower volumes, leaving a ¥476.5bn deficit. The economy had contracted at an annualised 0.1% in the third quarter, before this export run.
