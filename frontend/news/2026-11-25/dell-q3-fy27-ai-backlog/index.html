Dell doubles revenue on AI servers and books a $132.3 billion backlog

Dell reported record quarterly revenue of $54.9 billion for its fiscal third quarter, up 103% year on year, with adjusted earnings of $9.45 a share against roughly $6.59 expected. Its infrastructure unit sold $22.0 billion of AI-optimised servers, up 291%, and the company closed the period with a record $132.3 billion AI backlog after booking $60.2 billion of orders it says were vetted for customer quality and access to power. Dell raised full-year revenue guidance to $202.0 billion and added $25 billion to its buyback authorisation. It has not said when the backlog converts.
