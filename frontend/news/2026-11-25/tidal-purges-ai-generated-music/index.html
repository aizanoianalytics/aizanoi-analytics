Tidal will delete 29 million wholly AI-made tracks from 9 December

Tidal will start removing wholly AI-generated tracks on 9 December and expects about 29 million songs to be gone from the catalogue by the start of next year, its platform lead Robert Anderson said. The service says 43% of everything distributed to it in the past 90 days was detected as entirely AI-made, and that 27 million tracks, 6.3% of the catalogue, currently carry that tag; 96% of them drew no streams in three months. Tidal expects to reject 80,000 to 85,000 uploads a day and has already removed 65,000 tracks flagged as artist impersonations.
