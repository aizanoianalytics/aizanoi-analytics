Nvidia's SSD write hotfix cuts the damage but leaves a trace

Nvidia released GeForce hotfix 621.35 to stop its display container service writing excessive log data to a file called nvlog.nvlgstg in the background, a bug that drove some machines to roughly 30GB of writes an hour while idle. The hotfix carries that single fix, builds on the 621.24 Game Ready driver of 12 November, and is offered only through Nvidia's customer-care site rather than through normal driver distribution. PCMag's testing measured about 36GB of writes over nine hours on the new driver - far less than before, but not zero - and rolling back to 621.01 remains a workaround.
