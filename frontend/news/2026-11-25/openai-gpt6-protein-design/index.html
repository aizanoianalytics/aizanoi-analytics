OpenAI says its GPT-6 models designed proteins with reasoning and code alone

OpenAI published a preprint arguing that models in its GPT-6 family can design protein structures and amino-acid sequences from reasoning and ordinary coding tools, without calling specialised biology models. In lab work with partner Onava, 35 of 322 minibinders built from a pre-release GPT-6.2's designs bound their intended targets, with at least one hit for each of five proteins including PD-L1. Specialised models still supplied sequences and chose candidates for synthesis, and predicting a complete protein structure remained a clear weakness.
