Gul's header settles a game with one shot on target, and Villa's run reaches six

Galatasaray beat Aston Villa 1-0 in Istanbul with the match's first shot on target, a 77th-minute header from substitute Deniz Gul after Villa failed to clear a corner. Gul took a boot in the face from Nicolas Jackson while scoring and continued bandaged. Villa did not register an attempt on target at all, the first time they have failed to do so in a Champions League match since at least 2004-05, and their winless run reached six games in all competitions. Galatasaray have won three straight in Europe and eight in a row overall.
