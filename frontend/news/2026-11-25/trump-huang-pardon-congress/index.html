Trump leaves a Huang pardon on the table before a week of testimony

President Donald Trump told the New York Post he would pardon Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang if needed, and said Huang should not invoke the Fifth Amendment when he faces Congress next week. Huang is due for a transcribed interview with House committees on 1 December and public testimony before the Senate Banking Committee on 3 December, over his relationship with a woman US intelligence agencies suspected of working for Chinese intelligence. Trump also said he was open to the government taking an equity stake in Nvidia. The shares fell 5.9% on Tuesday after gaining 31% on Monday.
