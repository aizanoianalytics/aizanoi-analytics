Asia's chip complex surrenders the Huang rally in a single morning

Asia's semiconductor complex gave back the week's relief rally in one session, with Taiwan's Taiex down as much as 7.9% in morning trading and South Korea's Kospi more than 6%. Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 3.6%; Hong Kong's Hang Seng rose as much as 1.3% and was the region's exception. The selling concentrated in the hardware that carries the AI trade: Tokyo Electron fell 13.5%, Advantest 14.5%, Samsung Electronics 9.1% and SK Hynix 9.9%, while TSMC and Foxconn both dropped more than 9%. Wall Street had already slipped on Tuesday, the S&P 500 by 0.5% and the Nasdaq by 1.5%.
