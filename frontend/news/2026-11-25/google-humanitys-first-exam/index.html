Google's new benchmark scores models on getting simple things right every time

Google DeepMind launched Humanity's First Exam on Kaggle, a benchmark of 544 elementary text and image prompts across 20 foundational capabilities. Instead of asking whether a model can solve a task once, it asks whether the model solves it, and slight variations of it, on every attempt - the reliability gap that lets a system write fluent code and still misread a simple sign. DeepMind opened a three-week Kaggle competition, running to 15 December, for everyday questions that current models still get wrong, and said frontier systems have improved markedly on the set over six months.
