Arsenal dominate Dortmund and still lose two points to a single counter

Arsenal outplayed Borussia Dortmund and drew 1-1, dropping points that complicate a push for a top-two finish. Viktor Gyokeres put them ahead after ten minutes with a swerving shot from about 25 yards, and Maximilian Beier equalised early in the second half by cutting inside through the middle of a vacant midfield. Arsenal finished with 2.34 expected goals to Dortmund's 0.92, 30 shots to 13 and 56 touches in the box to 23, and could not find a winner. They sit fifth on 11 points with Real Madrid at home and a trip to Real Betis still to come.
