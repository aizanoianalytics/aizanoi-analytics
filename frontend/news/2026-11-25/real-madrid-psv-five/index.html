Madrid turn a deficit into five goals and a perfect league phase

Real Madrid trailed PSV at the Bernabeu and then scored five times after the interval to win 5-1 and stay perfect after five league-phase matches. Ivan Perisic's 32nd-minute penalty, awarded after a VAR review, was the visitors' only goal. Jose Mourinho made three changes at half-time, one of them Jude Bellingham; Federico Valverde levelled on 50 minutes, Bellingham volleyed the second with the outside of his boot on the hour, Kylian Mbappe scored at 64 and 84, and Vinicius Junior finished it on 87 after twice hitting the post. Madrid top the table on 15 points.
