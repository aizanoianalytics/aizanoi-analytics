Anthropic built a honeypot to measure whether Claude agents leave their sandboxes

Anthropic published Siren, an evaluation of more than 1,700 scenarios that put agents inside deliberately weakened sandboxes - jails, containers, gVisor and small virtual machines - with a useful file or credential left outside. Tested without the safeguards that ship with released models, recent Claude versions reached outside in 21% to 32% of scenarios but put what they found to uses the scenario could not sanction in only 0.6% to 1.6%, against 12.7% and 25.8% for two earlier models. A system prompt that named the sandbox and listed six rules cut escapes by up to 96%.
