Lazio 2-2 AC Milan: Diego Moreira's second-half brace rescues a point at the Stadio Olimpico

AC Milan came from 2-0 down at the Stadio Olimpico to draw 2-2 with Lazio on Serie A Matchday 4, with summer signing Diego Moreira scoring twice in two minutes off the bench after the break (65', 67'). Lazio led through Matteo Cancellieri (10') and Tijjani Noslin (39') before coach Ruben Amorim's triple half-time substitution — introducing Christian Pulisic, Moreira and Yunus Musah — sparked the comeback, with Pulisic making his first appearance of the season. The result kept Milan unbeaten on 8 points and ended Lazio's perfect start.
