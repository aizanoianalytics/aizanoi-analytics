Raphinha hat-trick powers Barcelona to 7-2 rout of Racing Santander

Barcelona extended their perfect La Liga start to six wins from six matches with a 7-2 victory over Racing Santander at the Spotify Camp Nou on Wednesday, opening up a three-point lead over Real Madrid at the top of the table. Raphinha scored a hat-trick, with Joao Cancelo adding a brace, an Asier Villalibre own goal and late strikes from Gabriel Jesus and Lamine Yamal completing the rout, while Maguette Gueye and Yassir Zabiri replied for the visitors.
