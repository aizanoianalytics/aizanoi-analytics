Insilico releases open LongevityBench toolkit and benchmark in Cell

Insilico Medicine on Thursday announced a study published in Cell introducing LongevityBench, an open benchmark of 17 tasks spanning clinical data, genetics, epigenetics, transcriptomics and proteomics, alongside a family of five compact Longevity-LLMs ranging from 0.6 to 9 billion parameters and the Longevity Claw agentic research platform.
