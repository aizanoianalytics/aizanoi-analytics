Real Madrid visit Atletico in Sunday La Liga derbi preview

Real Madrid travel across the city to face Atletico Madrid at the Metropolitano on Sunday in a La Liga matchweek-six derbi that pits the second-placed side against their neighbours after Espi's stoppage-time winner at Elche moved Real level on points with Barcelona. Atletico drew 1-1 at home to Osasuna on Wednesday, while Hansi Flick's Barcelona lead the table by three points after Wednesday's 7-2 rout of Racing Santander.
