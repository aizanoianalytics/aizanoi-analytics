iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max go on sale in India with early queues

Apple's iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max went on sale in India on Friday 18 September with customers queuing outside Apple stores in Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru, including at the Select Citywalk Mall in Saket and the Imagine Apple premium reseller at Mall of Asia in Bengaluru, before doors opened at 8 am IST.
