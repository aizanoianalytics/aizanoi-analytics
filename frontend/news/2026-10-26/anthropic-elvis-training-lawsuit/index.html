Anthropic faces a copyright claim over music used in AI training

Anthropic is facing a lawsuit alleging that 274 Elvis Presley songs were used in training AI models. The case adds another high-profile test of how courts may treat copyrighted music in model development, although the complaint’s allegations are not a final finding.
