China opens its 33rd Yangling agricultural technology fair

The 33rd China Yangling Agricultural Hi-tech Fair has opened, putting agricultural technology and applied innovation at the centre of a major industry event. The fair is a useful counterpoint to the model-centric AI cycle: deployment in food production depends on equipment, field operations and adoption as well as software.
