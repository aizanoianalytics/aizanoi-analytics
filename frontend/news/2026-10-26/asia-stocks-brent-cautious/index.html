Asian stocks trade cautiously as Brent retreats from its high

Asian markets opened cautiously while Brent crude moved down from a six-month peak, according to Reuters. The combination leaves investors balancing energy-price pressure against the possibility that a pullback in oil can ease some inflation concerns.
