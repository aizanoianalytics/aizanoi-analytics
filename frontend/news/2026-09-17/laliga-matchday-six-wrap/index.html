La Liga round-six wrap as Real Madrid sit top with Barcelona

Real Madrid moved level on points with Barcelona at the top of La Liga after Tuesday's stoppage-time 3-2 win at Elche through Carlos Espi, leaving both sides on the same tally after six rounds. Earlier Wednesday fixtures saw Sevilla take a 1-0 win at Deportivo La Coruña and Atlético Madrid and Osasuna draw 1-1 at the Metropolitano, with Barcelona and Racing Santander completing the Wednesday card. Round-six results left the title race finely poised with Barcelona and Real Madrid separated only by goal difference, while Espi's winner continued José Mourinho's side's perfect league start.
