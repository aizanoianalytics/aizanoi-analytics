iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max reach stores Friday amid shipping slippage

Apple's iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max launch in stores on Friday 19 September in more than 65 countries after pre-orders opened on Saturday 13 September, but shipping estimates have slipped across major US carriers. Apple's own online store lists iPhone 18 Pro delivery dates of 30 September to 7 October and Pro Max dates of 7 to 14 October across all colours and storage tiers, while Verizon is shipping 18 September through 6 October for nearly every configuration. Pro and Pro Max come in black, silver, glacier and a new burgundy finish, from $1,199 and $1,299.
