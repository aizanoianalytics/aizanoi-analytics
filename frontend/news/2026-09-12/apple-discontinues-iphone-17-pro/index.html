Apple discontinues iPhone 17 Pro and Pro Max on iPhone 18 launch day, no refurbished stock listed

Apple removed the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max from its online store on Wednesday after unveiling the iPhone 18 Pro line at its 'Surprise and Shine' event, ending direct sales of last year's premium models in line with its usual successor-cycle policy. As of Thursday the company's Certified Refurbished store had no iPhone 17 generation devices listed and no schedule for when they might appear.
