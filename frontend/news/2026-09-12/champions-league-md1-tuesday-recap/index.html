Champions League Matchday 1 Tuesday recap: Real Madrid, Man City, Dortmund and Aston Villa open with wins

The new Champions League league phase opened Tuesday with Kylian Mbappé and Federico Valverde steering Real Madrid to a 2-1 home win over Inter, Erling Haaland scoring twice in Manchester City's 2-0 win at Porto and Serhou Guirassy bagging a brace in Borussia Dortmund's 3-2 victory over Villarreal. Aston Villa began their return with a 3-2 win at Club Brugge featuring Nicolas Jackson's first Champions League goal, while AEK Athens beat LASK 1-0 and Lille lost 3-2 at home to Real Betis.
