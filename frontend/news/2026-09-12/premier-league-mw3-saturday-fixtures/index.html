Premier League Matchweek 3 kicks off Saturday with Chelsea-Hull, Liverpool-Fulham and Spurs-Everton

Premier League Matchweek 3 opens Saturday with seven fixtures across the 3 p.m. kick-off slot including Chelsea hosting Hull City at Stamford Bridge and Liverpool facing Fulham at Anfield, before Tottenham Hotspur host Everton at 17:30 on Sky Sports and Sunderland face Arsenal at 20:00 on TNT Sports. Aston Villa meet Nottingham Forest, Bournemouth host Brentford and Crystal Palace play Ipswich Town in the other Saturday matches.
