iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max pre-orders open Saturday with deliveries from September 18

Apple confirmed in its September 9 press release that pre-orders for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max open at 5 a.m. PT on Saturday, with retail availability and first deliveries on Friday September 18. The iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,199 for 256 GB, $100 more than the model it replaces, with the Pro Max starting at $1,299 and a 2 TB top configuration across both lines.
