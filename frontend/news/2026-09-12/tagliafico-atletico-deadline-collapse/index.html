Tagliafico's deadline-day move to Atlético Madrid collapses over La Liga salary cap

Olympique Lyon's Nicolás Tagliafico saw his deadline-day transfer to Atlético Madrid collapse after the Spanish club failed to clear La Liga salary cap space, with the French window closing four hours earlier than the Spanish one leaving Lyon unable to register his replacement in time. The 34-year-old Argentina left-back will now remain at Lyon through the end of his 2027 contract despite agreement between the parties.
