IFA 2026 closes in Berlin with experimental phones, robot cleaners and AI-driven home devices

Europe's biggest consumer electronics exhibition, IFA 2026, closed at Messe Berlin after a five-day run that put AI-enabled home appliances, flexible laptops and experimental smartphones in the spotlight. Highlights included Tecno's bezel-less concept phone, Lenovo's ThinkBook Project Swan with an expanding 14- to 17-inch OLED, iRobot's two-piece Roomba Duo concept, and Oukitel's solar-lid laptop.
