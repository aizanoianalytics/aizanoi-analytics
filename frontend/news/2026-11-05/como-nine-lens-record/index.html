Como put nine past Lens to match the biggest win in Champions League history

Como beat Lens 9-1 in northern France, equalling Bayern Munich's nine goals against Dinamo Zagreb in 2024 and recording the largest away win in the competition's history. Tasos Douvikas scored a hat-trick to reach six for the campaign; Nico Paz, Lucas Da Cunha, Marc-Oliver Kempf, Mattia Liberali, Martin Baturina and Matteo Papaccioli also scored, with Ruben Aguilar replying for Lens. Como sit fourth in the league phase.
