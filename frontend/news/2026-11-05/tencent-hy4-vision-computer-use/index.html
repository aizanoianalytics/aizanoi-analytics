Tencent puts vision and a computer-use mode inside its 770B Hy4

Tencent released Hy4, the finished version of its flagship model, at 770 billion total parameters in a mixture-of-experts design with a 1M-token context. It is the Hy line's first model that reads images and the first that can drive both command-line and graphical software, with targeted training on Blender and CAD. Weights and a system card are promised in the coming days; API pricing is unchanged at roughly $0.90 per million input tokens and $2.70 per million output.
