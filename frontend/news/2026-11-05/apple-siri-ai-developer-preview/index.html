Apple hands every developer a way into Siri AI, months before customers get it

Apple opened a Siri AI developer preview in the iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS 27.3 betas, letting any app route its content and actions through App Intents into the assistant's personal-context, onscreen-awareness and natural-language features. Developers can also write skills that chain several actions into one request. The integrations reach customers in a few months, App Store submissions open in spring, and Siri AI stays unavailable in the EU on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
