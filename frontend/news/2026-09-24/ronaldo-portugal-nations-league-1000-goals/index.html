Ronaldo says scoring his 1,000th goal and winning the UEFA Nations League are his two remaining targets

Cristiano Ronaldo, the 41-year-old Al Nassr forward and Portugal captain, told the Portuguese Football Summit in Oeiras on Wednesday that his remaining targets in football are to score his 1,000th career goal and to lift the UEFA Nations League. Portugal, under new coach Jorge Jesus after Roberto Martínez's departure, begin their Nations League campaign against Wales on Thursday with Ronaldo confirmed in the squad. Ronaldo praised the appointment, calling it 'a new era.'
