Lask overturn Sporting in Lisbon for a first Champions League win

Lask came from behind at the Estadio Jose Alvalade to beat Sporting 3-2 and claim their first Champions League win of the season. Luis Suarez put the hosts ahead from the penalty spot after five minutes, Christoph Lang equalised 10 minutes later, and Sasa Kalajdzic and Krystof Danek struck to send the Austrian champions 3-1 up. Suarez scored again late for Sporting but the Portuguese side could not find an equaliser. The result leaves the Austrian title holders with a first European victory of the campaign after two defeats.
