Silva sets Madrid rolling as Mourinho's side put four past Leipzig

Real Madrid made it three wins from three in the Champions League by beating RB Leipzig 4-1, with Bernardo Silva opening the scoring from close range late in a tight first half and Arda Guler doubling the lead soon after the interval. Kylian Mbappe bent home a third, Tidiam Gomis pulled one back for the visitors and Yan Diomande volleyed a fourth late on. Mbappe has now scored in each of Madrid's European matches this season and has 12 goals in 12 games, and he stressed that every point matters in the race for a top-eight finish with five fixtures left.
