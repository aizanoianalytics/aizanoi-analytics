Inter Milan stage stunning comeback against Udinese to go level with Roma at Serie A summit

Inter Milan staged a remarkable comeback from two goals down to beat Udinese 5-3 at the San Siro on Monday, preserving their perfect start to the Serie A season and moving level with Roma on 12 points after four matches. James Abankwah and Jurgen Ekkelenkamp put Udinese two up inside 16 minutes before Carlos Augusto's long-range strike and Nicolò Barella's equaliser pulled Inter level before half-time; Marcus Thuram completed the turnaround in the 59th minute. Idrissa Gueye scored a stoppage-time consolation, but Inter's night was marred by a hamstring issue for John Stones.
