Leeds thrash Newcastle 4-1 at Elland Road to climb to third in the Premier League

Leeds United demolished Newcastle 4-1 at Elland Road on Monday night to climb to third in the Premier League, inflicting a punishing first defeat on new Magpies boss Matthias Jaissle. A Lewis Miley own goal opened the floodgates before Jayden Bogle, Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Noah Okafor completed the rout, with substitute Bazoumana Touré netting a 90th-minute consolation. Calvert-Lewin led the press and the goals, bulldozing past Sven Botman to slot home a third before Okafor volleyed a fourth. Leeds sit third on eight points before hosting Crystal Palace on Saturday.
