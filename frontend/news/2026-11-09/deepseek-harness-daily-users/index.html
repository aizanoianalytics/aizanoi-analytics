DeepSeek's Harness agent passes 1.5 million daily users in under three months

DeepSeek said its open-source agent application Harness reached 1.54 million daily active users on 4 November, up from about 271,000 at the end of September, counting only traffic that runs through its own API. The company put consumption at roughly 92 trillion tokens a day, about 60 million per active user, and said around 35,000 third-party plugins exist, with 60 percent of users having installed or created one. A plugin marketplace and a developer support programme are in preparation. The figures are company disclosures, not independently audited.
