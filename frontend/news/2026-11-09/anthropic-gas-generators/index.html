Anthropic takes over 1.7 gigawatts of gas generators for $3.4 billion

Anthropic agreed in late September to take over commitments for 1.7 gigawatts of heavy-duty gas generators from San Antonio-based OnePWR Solutions for about $3.4 billion, the Financial Times reported. OnePWR had ordered the Caterpillar units two years earlier and needed financing to complete the purchase; the deal includes engineering and construction work but no staff transfer. It is the largest known single purchase of power equipment by an AI lab, alongside Anthropic's plan to spend more than $895 billion on computing capacity and infrastructure.
