Manchester United's 4-4 with Villa leaves them in the bottom three

Manchester United and Aston Villa shared eight goals at Old Trafford on Sunday, a result that leaves United 18th with eight points from ten games, above only Sunderland and Crystal Palace. Villa stay tenth on 13. Arsenal lead the table on 27 after beating Hull 1-0, with Brighton on 22 and Manchester City on 21. It is United's fifth draw of the campaign and leaves them with one win from ten league matches.
