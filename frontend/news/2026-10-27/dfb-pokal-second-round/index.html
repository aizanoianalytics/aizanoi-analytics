Dortmund and Bremen headline a cup night of Bundesliga ties

The second round of the DFB-Pokal is decided across Tuesday and Wednesday, with 16 places in the last 16 at stake and four all-Bundesliga ties in the draw. Borussia Dortmund host Werder Bremen on Tuesday at 20:45, shown by ZDF, while Hamburger SV meet Eintracht Frankfurt and third-tier Rot-Weiss Essen welcome RB Leipzig at 18:00. Bayern Munich face Magdeburg on Wednesday, live on ARD.
