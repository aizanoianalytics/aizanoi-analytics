Anthropic files to list on Nasdaq with steep losses and fast revenue growth

Anthropic has filed a public S-1 for a Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT, opening its books for the first time. The Claude developer reported about $16.3bn of revenue in the six months to June and a net loss of $80.7bn, most of it tied to fair-value changes on convertible instruments rather than cash burn. No share count or price range was set, and the document puts the cost of building frontier models on the public record.
