Tesla and Nvidia fund $2.8bn of AI chip-substrate capacity in Korea

Tesla and Nvidia are putting a combined 3.7 trillion won, about $2.8bn, into Samsung Electro-Mechanics' chip-substrate capacity, according to Korean press reports. The money targets FC-BGA substrates that sit beneath AI accelerators, with Nvidia taking the larger share and Tesla tying its contribution to a long-term supply deal. Nvidia and the Korean supplier did confirm a joint investment in dedicated ABF substrate lines at an event this month.
