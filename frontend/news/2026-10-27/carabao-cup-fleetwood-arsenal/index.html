Fleetwood's first competitive meeting with Arsenal arrives at Highbury

Fleetwood Town host Premier League champions Arsenal on Tuesday in the fourth round of the Carabao Cup, the first competitive meeting between the clubs. The League Two side have won all three home ties without conceding, beating Chesterfield, Shrewsbury and Sheffield United at the 5,300-capacity Highbury Stadium. Liverpool against Chelsea at Anfield on Wednesday is the tie of the round, with a quarter-final place at stake.
