Apple puts a 2nm A20 Pro and an OLED screen into the iPad mini

Apple has given the iPad mini its first OLED display: 8.5 inches, 1,200 nits peak brightness and an optional nano-texture finish, inside a 5.4mm body rated IP67 for water and dust. The A20 Pro is built on a 2-nanometer process with neural accelerators in every GPU core, which Apple says lifts AI performance up to 4.5 times. Pre-orders open on 28 October, with units on sale from 6 November.
