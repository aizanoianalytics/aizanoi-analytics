Lobotka goal lifts Napoli past Bologna; Kane hits 99 in Bayern colours at Elversberg

Stanislav Lobotka scored the only goal as Napoli beat Bologna 1-0 at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona on Sunday to end a three-match losing run and hand Massimiliano Allegri his second Serie A win. The result eases pressure on Allegri after Tuesday's 1-0 Champions League defeat by Arsenal. Saturday had already delivered talking points: Harry Kane struck his 99th Bayern Munich goal in a 2-1 win at promoted SV Elversberg, while Juventus lost 3-2 at home to Sassuolo before Thursday's Europa League visit of NEC.
