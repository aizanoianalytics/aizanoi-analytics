Apple delays Messages Suggestions and Passwords security alerts past the iOS 27 launch

Apple's own iOS 27 page lists two promised features — Messages Suggestions and Passwords security alerts with auto-upgrade — as 'coming in a future software update' with no date attached, even as the rest of iOS 27 ships on 14 September. Apple has also pushed Live Rewind and Siri Recap on watchOS 27 to 'later in 2026' without a firmer window, while five non-English languages for Siri AI are pencilled in for October without leaving beta. MacObserver notes the pattern complicates any single-date claim about the platform being 'complete' on day one.
