Haaland goal hands 10-man Manchester City a 1-0 derby win at Manchester United

Erling Haaland scored on the hour at Old Trafford on Sunday as Manchester City beat Manchester United 1-0 in the Premier League derby, even after Phil Foden was shown a red card. The result, in front of 74,161 supporters, widened the gap to Arsenal to five points and inflicted a second defeat of the season on United. Manchester City resume at home to Brentford on 19 September; United travel to the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium a day later.
