iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max reach stores worldwide on Friday

Apple's iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, introduced a week ago by new chief executive John Ternus, go on sale worldwide on Friday with pre-orders having opened on September 12. The lineup brings a 48-megapixel Fusion camera with a physical variable aperture, the A20 Pro chip on a 2-nanometer process and a redesigned vapor chamber, with iPhone 18 Pro Max rated for up to 45 hours of video playback on the eSIM-only variant.
