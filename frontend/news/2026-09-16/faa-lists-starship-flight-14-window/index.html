FAA lists Starship Flight 14 for September 22 primary window

The US Federal Aviation Administration's Current Operations Plan Advisory dated September 15 sets a primary launch window for SpaceX's Starship Flight 14 from Starbase, Texas on September 22, with September 23 reserved as backup. The mission is described as Starship's first revenue-generating flight and is expected to carry production Starlink V3 satellites, while chief executive Elon Musk said Flight 15 would attempt the first tower catch of the upper stage.
