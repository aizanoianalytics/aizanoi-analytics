AirPods 5 and Apple Watch Series 12 ship alongside iPhone 18 Pro

Apple confirmed that AirPods 5, Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 will reach stores worldwide on Friday alongside iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, with Mac mini and Mac Studio following on September 22. AirPods 5 use an open-ear design with multiport acoustics and adaptive equaliser, while Apple Watch Series 12 adds a Health Sensing System and S11 chip, plus a readiness score to help users plan their day.
