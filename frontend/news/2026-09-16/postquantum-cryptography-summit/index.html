US federal Post-Quantum Cryptography Summit convenes Tuesday

The US General Services Administration hosts a virtual Post-Quantum Cryptography Summit on September 16 covering migration timelines, cryptographic inventory tooling and procurement guidance for federal agencies. The event follows recent moves by NIST to finalise post-quantum standards and signals continued coordination between civilian agencies and the vendor ecosystem on transitioning to quantum-resistant algorithms across the federal estate.
