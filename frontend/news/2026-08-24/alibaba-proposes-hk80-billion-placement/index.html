Alibaba proposes HK$80 billion share placement for AI investment

Alibaba proposed placing newly issued shares with non-US investors for an aggregate HK$80 billion, subject to market and other conditions. The company says all net proceeds would fund its full-stack AI capabilities, including expanded infrastructure.
