Google makes Gemini's task agent free in six countries and lets it call, pay and sign in

Tasks in Gemini, the errand-running mode previously sold with AI Pro and Ultra plans, opened to free users on Tuesday in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India, running on the cheaper Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. US accounts get the sharper edges: calls to local businesses with an AI disclosure, checkout through Google Pay with a spending cap, site logins through Password Manager, and jobs sent by text or forwarded email. Europe is excluded, and calling is blocked in five states.
