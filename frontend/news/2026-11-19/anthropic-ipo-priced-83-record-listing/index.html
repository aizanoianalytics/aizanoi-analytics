Anthropic prices at $83 and raises $81.3bn in the largest listing on record

Anthropic sold 980 million Class A shares at $83 on Wednesday, above the $70 to $78 range it had marketed and about 19% more stock than first planned, raising $81.3bn and valuing the company near $1.5tn. The Claude developer begins trading on Nasdaq on Thursday under ANT, with the offering due to close on Friday and underwriters holding a 30-day option on 147 million more shares. The deal overtakes SpaceX's $75bn June float and lands the first frontier lab on public markets.
