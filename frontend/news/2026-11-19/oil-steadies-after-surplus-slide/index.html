Oil steadies after its slide as surplus fears outweigh mines in the Gulf

Brent traded near $92.12 a barrel and WTI near $83.17 on Thursday morning, a day after the US benchmark fell 3.2% to its lowest settle since 26 August as Hormuz shipments returned to roughly pre-war levels and US crude stocks rose 3.4 million barrels to 417.8 million. Iran's navy said mines disabled three tankers east of the strait, and Ukrainian drone strikes have cut Russian refining runs by about 45% since late October, but traders are pricing surplus ahead of those risks while the Fed leans towards higher rates.
