OpenAI says its models found up to 14% more headroom in AMD's flagship GPU designs

OpenAI reported on Wednesday that its models rewrote the SystemVerilog descriptions of AMD circuits to lift power, performance and area by as much as 14% against baselines the chipmaker's engineers had already tuned, work it calls roughly a year of progress in under five months. AMD has put some model-written edits into a design sent for manufacturing and verified single-digit gains on blocks of a next-generation CPU. On OpenAI's own Jalapeño inference chip, standard-cell area fell about 13% in the vector unit.
