Nvidia's Vera Rubin ramp lifts Korean memory stocks as HBM4 shipments begin

Samsung Electronics rose 3.6% to 199,200 won and SK hynix 6.5% to 1,169,000 won in Seoul on Wednesday, helping the KOSPI close 1.99% higher at 5,067.44 after four losing sessions, as investors read Nvidia's quarter as proof that memory demand outlasts its leadership turmoil. Nvidia reported $115.8bn of revenue, guided to $130bn and said Vera Rubin NVL72, built on fourth-generation high-bandwidth memory, is ramping faster than any product in its history, with packaging and substrate partners scaling alongside.
