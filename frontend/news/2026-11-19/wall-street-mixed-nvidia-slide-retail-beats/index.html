Nvidia's beat fails to lift the tape as discount retail carries the session

US stocks ended mixed on Wednesday: the Dow fell 1.11% to 50,198.17 and the S&P 500 slipped 0.23% to 7,323.63 while the Nasdaq added 0.42% to 24,429.89. Nvidia, up as much as 2.7% after guiding to $130bn, closed 2.9% lower and has lost more than a third of its value since early October. Target rose about 4% and TJX 8% on holiday outlooks, and hawkish Fed minutes kept the December rate debate at the centre of the tape.
