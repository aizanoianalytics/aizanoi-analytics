Nvidia's interim chief keeps the open-model strategy and the $12.9bn Hugging Face deal

On her first earnings call since Jensen Huang resigned, interim chief executive Colette Kress said Nvidia's commitment to open models is unchanged and that the $12.9bn purchase of Hugging Face remains on track to close in the first half of 2027. Nvidia puts the platform at more than 18 million developers and 200,000 companies; the deal is its second-largest after Groq's assets. Huang was the acquisition's most visible champion, and his exit had raised questions about how open Nvidia's software layer would stay.
