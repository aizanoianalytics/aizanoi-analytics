Ollie Watkins completes £51 million move from Aston Villa to Al-Hilal

England striker Ollie Watkins completed a £51 million transfer from Aston Villa to Saudi Pro League side Al-Hilal on a three-year contract, ending a six-year spell at Villa Park where he became the club's all-time Premier League top scorer with 91 goals. Villa have replaced Watkins with Nicolas Jackson from Chelsea and agreed a £30 million package for Southampton defender Taylor Harwood-Bellis.
