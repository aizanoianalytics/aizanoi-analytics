Sony Music and Warner sue Anthropic over alleged training-data piracy

Sony Music Publishing, Warner Chappell and other publishers filed a federal lawsuit in California accusing Anthropic of a brazen campaign to torrent, scrape and download copyrighted works to train Claude. The complaint leans on the same factual record that produced the $1.5 billion Bartz judgment, and Anthropic says it will defend itself robustly.
