Chelsea edge Brighton 4-3 to extend Xabi Alonso's perfect Premier League start

Chelsea beat Brighton 4-3 at Stamford Bridge in a frenetic seven-goal game that included a Cole Palmer strike and a stoppage-time Pascal Gross header. The result is Alonso's second consecutive Premier League win since taking charge, but a third straight game conceding three leaves defensive questions lingering ahead of the trip to Arsenal.
