Sequoia and IDG back Flova with $80 million for an AI-native video production platform

Flova.ai, founded by former FaceU and ByteDance executive Guo Lie, raised more than $80 million from Sequoia Capital, IDG Capital and Yunji Capital to build an AI-native video production environment. The platform positions itself around scripts up to 100,000 characters long and a library of reusable production skills aimed at professional creators.
