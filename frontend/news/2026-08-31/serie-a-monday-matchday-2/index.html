Serie A Monday double-header closes matchday two with Roma and Atalanta favoured

Serie A's second matchday closes with Atalanta v Bologna at 19:45 local time and Lecce v Roma at 17:30, both teams coming off opening-weekend victories. Roma arrived in Lecce on a six-match league winning streak after a 4-0 start under Gian Piero Gasperini, while Atalanta opened with a 2-1 win to set up an early-season test against Bologna.
