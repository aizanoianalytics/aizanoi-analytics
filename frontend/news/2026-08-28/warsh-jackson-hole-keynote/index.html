Warsh to deliver first Jackson Hole keynote as Fed chair on Friday

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh takes the podium at the Kansas City Fed's annual symposium in Jackson Lake Lodge, Wyoming, on Friday morning for his first keynote as chair. The 2026 programme runs Thursday 27 through Saturday 29 August under the theme "Financial Innovation: Implications for Payments and Policy", and his twenty-minute address will be parsed for signals on the September 16 FOMC decision 19 days later.
