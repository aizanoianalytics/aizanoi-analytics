Salesforce posts record $11.35B quarter as Agentforce ARR tops $1.5B

Salesforce reported fiscal second-quarter revenue of $11.35 billion after the bell on Wednesday, up 11% year-on-year, with adjusted EPS of $5.90 beating the $3.27 consensus. Agentforce annual recurring revenue crossed $1.5 billion and combined Agentforce and Data 360 ARR reached nearly $3.9 billion, while the company credited a $2.6 billion gain on its Anthropic investment for pushing net income up 87% to $3.53 billion.
