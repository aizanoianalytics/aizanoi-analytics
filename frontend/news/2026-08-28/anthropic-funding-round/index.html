Anthropic set to close $30B funding at over $900B valuation

Anthropic is expected to close a new funding round as soon as next week with commitments exceeding $30 billion at a post-money valuation above $900 billion, according to a Bloomberg report. The round would extend Anthropic's lead-up to a potential IPO after its $65 billion raise in May 2026 brought the company to a $965 billion valuation and made it one of the highest-valued private AI labs worldwide.
