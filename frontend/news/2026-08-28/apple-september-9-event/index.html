Apple confirms September 9 launch event for the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Ultra

Apple is widely expected to hold its annual fall event at 10:00 a.m. Pacific on 9 September at the Steve Jobs Theater, with invitations expected to land on 26 August. Reports point to the iPhone 18 Pro with an A20 Pro chip on a 2nm process, a slimmer Dynamic Island and a variable-aperture wide camera, plus the long-rumoured iPhone Ultra and a first foldable iPhone, with prices tipped to climb $200–$300 across the lineup.
