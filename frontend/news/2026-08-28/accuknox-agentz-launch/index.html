AccuKnox launches AgentZ to govern enterprise AI agents at scale

Menlo Park-based AccuKnox unveiled AgentZ on Wednesday as a platform for building, running and governing AI agents across teams and workflows. The hosted platform and its open-source repository bundle agents, execution environments, tools, permissions and audit logs into a single stack, with sandboxed execution and tool-level permissions designed to make agent rollouts auditable rather than experimental.
