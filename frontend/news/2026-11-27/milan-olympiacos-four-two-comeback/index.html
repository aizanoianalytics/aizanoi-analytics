Hutchinson drives Milan from two down to a first away win in Greece

Milan recovered from two goals down to win 4-2 at Olympiacos, their first away victory against Greek opposition. A Mike Maignan own goal and a João Silva strike had Olympiacos 2-0 up and unbeaten in the league phase. Omari Hutchinson pulled one back with a solo run, then crossed for Alphadjo Cisse to head in and for Christian Pulišić to volley home. Diego Moreira sealed it on the break in stoppage time. The result ended Olympiacos' unbeaten start and gave Milan a second win in five league-phase matches.
