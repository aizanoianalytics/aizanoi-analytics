S&P puts Nvidia's AA rating on negative watch over governance and key-man risk

S&P Global Ratings placed Nvidia's AA issuer rating on CreditWatch negative on 25 November, citing rising management and governance risk after Jensen Huang's removal and reinstatement as chief executive. The agency said it could cut the rating by one notch to AA- within 90 days, pointing to weak executive oversight, absent succession planning and key-man risk. Nvidia would stay investment grade, but a cut could raise borrowing costs for the neoclouds whose debt it backstops, including under a $500bn financing plan announced in August.
