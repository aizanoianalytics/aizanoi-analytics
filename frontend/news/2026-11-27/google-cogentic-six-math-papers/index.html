Google's Cogentic agent posts six maths papers, led by a claimed factoring bound

Google Research added six mathematics papers to its public repository on 25 November, each attributed to Cogentic, a multi-agent proof system built on Gemini 4, with no human input beyond the initial prompt. The lead paper claims an unconditional integer-factorisation algorithm running in expected time L[1/3, 105], improving on the proven L[1/2] bound of Lenstra and Pomerance from 1992. Its constant of 105, against about 1.92 for the number field sieve, means no practical attack on RSA. None of the papers is peer reviewed and the promised Lean 4 proofs are unreleased.
