Google will double Gemini 4 Argon API prices on 1 December

Google's published Gemini API pricing doubles Gemini 4 Argon from 1 December: input moves from $2.00 to $4.00 per million tokens, output from $10.00 to $20.00, and context caching from $0.10 to $0.20. The page lists the current rates as applying through 30 November. Argon, the cheaper Gemini 4 reasoning tier, is not available on the free tier and bills thinking tokens and internal tool instructions. Teams with Gemini-dependent workloads have four days to reprice them.
