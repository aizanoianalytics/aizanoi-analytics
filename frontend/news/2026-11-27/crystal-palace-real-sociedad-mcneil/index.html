McNeil completes Palace's late turnaround for Frank's first win

Crystal Palace beat Real Sociedad 2-1 at Selhurst Park for Thomas Frank's first win as manager, after Jon Martín's 71st-minute second booking turned the game. Mamadou Sarr had put the visitors ahead on 66 minutes; Jean-Philippe Mateta equalised from the penalty spot two minutes after the dismissal and substitute Dwight McNeil scored his first Palace goal on 82. Palace move to eight points from five league-phase games; Real Sociedad have five.
