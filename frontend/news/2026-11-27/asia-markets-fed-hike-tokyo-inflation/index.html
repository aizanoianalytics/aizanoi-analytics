Asian shares steady as Tokyo inflation firms a December BOJ case and the Fed stays hawkish

Asian equities steadied on Friday after a bruising month, with Seoul up about 2% and Tokyo 0.7% as chipmakers recovered. Tokyo core inflation hit a 16-month high of 2.9% in November, strengthening bets on a Bank of Japan hike in December, while futures imply a 99% chance the Federal Reserve raises rates by a quarter point next month. MSCI's Asia-Pacific ex-Japan gauge was still down 8.2% for November. Brent slipped to $96.14 a barrel but was up 3.9% on the week, and the dollar headed for a third monthly gain.
