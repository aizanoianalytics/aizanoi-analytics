China's industrial profits rise 16% as electronics carry the year and autos drag

China's industrial profits rose 16.0% year on year to 6.74 trillion yuan in January-October, up from 15.5% in the first nine months, the National Bureau of Statistics said. October alone was up 20.1%, from 14.4% in September. Electronics manufacturing profits doubled and contributed 9.7 percentage points of the headline growth, while high-tech manufacturing rose 50.9%. The split remained stark: auto profits fell 26.0% and furniture 52.4%. Revenue rose 6.7% to 118.41 trillion yuan and the margin improved to 5.69%.
