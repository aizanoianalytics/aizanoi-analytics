BT lifts EE's London 5G speeds 26% with a remote AI scheduler upgrade

BT activated Ericsson's AI-native Scheduler for Link Adaptation across EE's London network, reporting a 26% rise in average download speed and more than 13% better spectral efficiency. The upgrade was pushed remotely to RAN Compute hardware already installed, with no new radios, site visits or added energy use, BT said. It follows a North London trial and makes BT the first UK operator to deploy the software commercially across a whole network area. Ericsson's model predicts the best downlink rate from live channel data instead of relying on fixed rules.
