OpenAI folds Chat and Work together and puts ChatGPT and Codex on one token allowance

OpenAI has begun moving a small group of Pro subscribers onto a single Chat and Work interface, product lead Thibault Sottiaux said, with a wider rollout planned for December. From December, Plus, Pro and Business plans will draw on one token allowance shared across ChatGPT and Codex, according to OpenAI's help centre. Instant conversations stay unlimited and do not count against the pool. OpenAI says most users will not see more frequent resets, but a share of the heaviest will hit limits sooner, drawing complaints from subscribers.
