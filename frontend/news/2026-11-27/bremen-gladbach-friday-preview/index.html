Bremen can climb to fourth while bottom club Gladbach arrive at the Weserstadion

Werder Bremen host Borussia Mönchengladbach on Friday night with the table at its most lopsided: Bremen are sixth on 18 points from ten games, Gladbach last on four. A Bremen win would lift them to 21, level with Leipzig and one behind Bayern and Freiburg. Gladbach have one win, one draw and eight defeats, with 12 goals scored and 26 conceded. Bremen are without Grüll, Musah, Ndiaye, Stage and Topp; Gladbach miss Kleindienst, Leopold, Leszczyński and Ullrich. Kick-off is 20:30 CET at the Weserstadion.
