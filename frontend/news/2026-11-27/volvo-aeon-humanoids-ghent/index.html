Volvo starts training wheeled AEON humanoids at its Ghent plant

Volvo Cars has begun training Hexagon Robotics' AEON humanoids at its Ghent plant, the first Volvo factory to try them. Staff are teaching the 1.65-metre, 60-kilogram wheeled robot by imitation, starting with placing components into two fixtures; payload is 10 kilograms and top speed 2.4 metres per second. A larger pilot with more units follows in the first half of 2027, with the aim of using the robots in real production from mid-2027. Volvo frames the target as repetitive, ergonomically difficult work alongside existing automation.
