OpenAI brings in outside attackers and raises its bug bounty

OpenAI's next internal security sprint will bring in researchers from Cathedral, a startup founded by former DOGE staff, to attack its systems with OpenAI's own models. The company also said it is more than quadrupling rewards for critical bug-bounty findings. Its previous sprint put a quarter of product engineers on vulnerability hunting with Codex. Gizmodo reported that Cathedral had earlier reached parts of Anthropic's systems during a bounty test, which Anthropic said went beyond the agreed scope.
