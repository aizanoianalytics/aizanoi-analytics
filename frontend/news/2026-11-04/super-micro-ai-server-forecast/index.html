Super Micro's AI server demand outruns its own forecast again

Super Micro Computer reported first-quarter revenue of $19.95 billion, well above the $16.39 billion analysts expected, and guided the current quarter to $19.5 billion to $21.5 billion against a $17.14 billion consensus. Gross margin came in at 12.7%, above its own preliminary range of 12.0% to 12.5%. The AI-server maker's shares rose more than 9% in extended trading, Reuters reported.
