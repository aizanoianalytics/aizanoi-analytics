Cunha rescues Manchester United from two down, then gets sent off

Manchester United came from two goals down to beat Roma 3-2 at Old Trafford, ending a run of nine matches without a win. Matias Soule and a Donyell Malen penalty put Roma ahead; Bruno Fernandes converted a retaken penalty, Matheus Cunha equalised in the 83rd minute and scored the winner in the 86th. Cunha was sent off in stoppage time for a second booking. Carlos Baleba went off with a hamstring injury.
