Chips rebound, Apple drags the Dow as Wall Street splits

Wall Street split on election day: the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.83% to 25,372.01 and the S&P 500 added 0.36% to 7,568.92 as chip shares rebounded, while the Dow fell 0.38% to 51,786.72 after Apple's margin guidance. The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.952% and Brent settled 1.12% lower at $103.60 a barrel. The CBOE volatility index held above 20 through the session.
