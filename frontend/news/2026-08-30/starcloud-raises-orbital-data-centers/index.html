Starcloud raises $250M Series A to build orbital data centers with Nvidia

Starcloud has raised a $250 million Series A round backed by Nvidia to build a network of orbital data centers, joining SpaceX's separately filed Starmind plan to put up to a million data-center satellites in orbit. The funding highlights a new race to move compute off the planet as ground-based AI demand strains power and cooling capacity, with Starcloud positioning its constellation as a power- and water-light alternative for hyperscale training and inference workloads.
