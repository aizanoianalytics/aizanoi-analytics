HOW WE PUBLISH

Sources before claims.

Aizanoi News publishes original concise summaries rather than copied article bodies. Every story links the sources used to prepare it.

Source selection

For important, disputed or fast-moving stories we prefer multiple independent sources and/or a primary source. Rehosts of the same wire report do not count as independent corroboration, and direct original-publisher links are preferred when available.

AI-assisted production

AI-assisted tools may support source discovery, drafting, classification and production. They are not treated as sources. Publication remains governed by the repository's source, validation and corrections policy.

Corrections

Material corrections update the story metadata and preserve a visible correction note on the permanent article page.

Images

We do not copy third-party editorial images simply because they appear in a source article. Story imagery must be original, appropriately licensed, public-domain or otherwise permitted, with provenance retained when required.
