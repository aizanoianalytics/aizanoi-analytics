Füllkrug rescues Bremen at the death after Gladbach let a two-goal lead go

Niclas Füllkrug equalised in the second minute of stoppage time to give Werder Bremen a 3-3 draw with Borussia Mönchengladbach and leave the visitors still bottom of the Bundesliga. Gladbach led through Kevin Diks's 27th-minute penalty and a Franck Honorat double either side of Eren Dinkçi's first goal, and were 3-1 up with 12 minutes left. Dinkçi scored again on 85 before Füllkrug, now on 11 league goals, rescued a point. Gladbach remain 18th on four points.
