OS-Omni scores computer agents across six platforms, and mobile is the weak leg

A 300-task benchmark published on arXiv tested nine agents on Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android and the web, plus twelve workflows that pair a desktop with a phone. Claude Opus 5.5 led at 68.5% average success against a 77.3% human reference, with GPT-6 Astra at 66.1% and Gemini 4 Argon at 58.6%. Every frontier model beat humans on Linux, while the best iOS score was 41.7%, 33.5 points behind people. Tasks are graded on final application state rather than on screen appearance.
