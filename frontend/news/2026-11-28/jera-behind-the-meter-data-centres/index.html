JERA courts Anthropic and Meta for data centres wired straight into its power plants

JERA, Japan's largest power producer, is in talks with Anthropic, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft and Google about placing data centres beside its thermal plants around Tokyo, chairman Yukio Kani told Nikkei. The behind-the-meter model draws power directly from the plant, skipping grid connection queues that can run for years. The template is an October memorandum with Dell and RHAELM for a site at the Chiba plant, planned at up to 400MW and more than $15 billion, with operations from 2028. None of the five companies had commented when Reuters reported the talks.
