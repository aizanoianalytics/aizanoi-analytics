Black Friday online spending passes $9.7 billion by late afternoon

US shoppers spent $9.7 billion online on Black Friday by 6:30 p.m. Eastern, according to Adobe data reported by Reuters, putting the day on course to exceed $13 billion. Thanksgiving Day set a record $7.1 billion, up 10.4% year on year, Adobe Analytics said, with traffic referred by AI assistants up 162% and converting 74% better than other traffic. Adobe projects $275.1 billion of online spending across November and December, a 6.7% increase, as retailers discount more deeply than expected.
