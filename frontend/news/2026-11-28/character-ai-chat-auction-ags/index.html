Forty-two attorneys general move to stop Character.AI auctioning its chat logs

A bipartisan coalition of 42 state attorneys general has objected in Delaware bankruptcy court to Character.AI's plan to sell its platform and chat histories at a 1 December auction. The states say the data covers more than 21 million US accounts, over 2.1 million of them registered to minors, along with IP addresses and risk scores, and that the buyer would not have to honour Character.AI's privacy policy or users' deletion requests. The only disclosed bidder, Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC, was incorporated a day before it was chosen as the opening bid.
