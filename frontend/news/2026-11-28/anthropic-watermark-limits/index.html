Anthropic's watermark help page spells out where Claude's mark disappears

Anthropic's help centre now sets out how Claude marks generated text and where the mark fails. The embedded watermark travels with copied text and may survive light editing, but the page says heavy paraphrasing, translation, short passages and stripped file metadata can defeat detection, and that non-default temperature, top_p or top_k settings may leave older models' output unmarked. Detection remains a private preview for regulators, media, fact-checkers and researchers. Anthropic says a match shows Claude processed text, not that Claude wrote it.
