Gibbs-White's double deepens Chelsea's slump and ends a 29-year wait for Forest

Morgan Gibbs-White scored twice as Nottingham Forest beat Chelsea 3-2 at the City Ground, a first home league win over the London side since 1997. Igor Jesus volleyed Forest ahead after five minutes and Gibbs-White converted a penalty on 51 after Wesley Fofana fouled Liam Delap. Emmanuel Emegha pulled two goals back, either side of Gibbs-White's second on 72. It was Chelsea's third straight league defeat and a fourth loss in five, leaving Xabi Alonso's side eighth in the table.
