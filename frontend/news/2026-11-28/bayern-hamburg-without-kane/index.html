Bayern go to Hamburg without Kane and on the back of a first defeat

Bayern Munich visit Hamburger SV on Saturday second in the Bundesliga on 22 points, two behind Borussia Dortmund, after a 2-1 loss at Lille ended their unbeaten season. Vincent Kompany's side are without Harry Kane, Jamal Musiala, Manuel Neuer, Ismaël Saibari and Michael Olise, and were held 1-1 by Köln in their last league game. Hamburg are 12th with 12 points and conceded four at Werder Bremen last week. Bayern are unbeaten in 31 away Bundesliga matches.
