Q.ANT's light-based coprocessor reaches an IONOS pilot while its energy claim stays simulated

Q.ANT has put its second-generation photonic processor, which performs discrete Fourier transforms in light rather than electrons, into a pilot with cloud provider IONOS in Germany. The Stuttgart company showed the chip paired with an AMD GPU running a language model at SC26 and says more than 30 engineering teams are writing models for it. Chief executive Michael Förtsch told WIRED that simulations point to four or five times lower energy use alongside CPUs and GPUs, but that real hardware has not yet demonstrated it.
