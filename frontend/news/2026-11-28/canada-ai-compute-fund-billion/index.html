Canada lifts its AI compute fund to C$1 billion and pays more for domestic clouds

Canada's AI minister, Evan Solomon, added C$700 million to the AI Compute Access Fund, taking it from C$300 million to C$1 billion, and raised the share of eligible compute costs that small and medium firms can recover to 80% when they buy from a Canadian-owned provider, or 50% elsewhere. The announcement came with more than C$52 million for 45 companies, bringing commitments to over C$118 million across 89 firms. Officials had expected a few hundred applicants and received about 1,500 requests.
