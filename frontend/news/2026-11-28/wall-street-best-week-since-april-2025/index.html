Wall Street closes a violent November with its best week since spring 2025

US stocks rose in Friday's holiday-shortened session, with the S&P 500 up 1.6% to 7,297.49, the Nasdaq Composite up 2.31% to 24,129.50 and the Dow up 1.18% to 50,774.94. For the week the S&P gained 5.6% and the Nasdaq 6.7%, their best runs since April 2025, while the Dow's 5.5% was its strongest week in four years. The rebound followed a slide of roughly 8% the week before. Gold rose 2.9% to $3,668.50 and Brent crude slipped to about $90 a barrel.
