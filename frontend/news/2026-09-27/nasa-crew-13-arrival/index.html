NASA’s Crew-13 reaches Kennedy ahead of October station launch

NASA’s SpaceX Crew-13 astronauts arrived at Kennedy Space Center for quarantine and final preflight work before a targeted October 1 launch to the International Space Station. The four-person crew includes NASA astronauts Jessica Watkins and Luke Delaney, CSA astronaut Joshua Kutryk and Roscosmos cosmonaut Sergey Teteryatnikov.
