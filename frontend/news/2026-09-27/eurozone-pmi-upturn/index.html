Euro-zone business activity accelerates to strongest pace since 2023

The flash Euro-zone Composite PMI Output Index rose to 53.1 in September from 52.0 in August, its highest reading since April 2023, according to S&P Global data reported by Reuters. Services led the improvement while manufacturing output also edged higher, although firms reported stronger cost pressures.
