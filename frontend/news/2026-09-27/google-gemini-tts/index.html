Google releases Gemini voice models aimed at expressive speech creation

Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Flash-Lite TTS for expressive speech generation. The company positions the larger model for directed character voices and dialogue, and the lighter model for higher-volume dubbing and voice-agent workloads across its developer and creative products.
