CrowdStrike in talks to buy Zscaler in a cybersecurity mega-deal

CrowdStrike is in talks to acquire Zscaler, according to Bloomberg and Reuters, in a deal that would pair endpoint security with cloud-delivered network security and rank among the largest in the sector. Zscaler, valued at about $32 billion at Friday's close, rose roughly 17% after hours while CrowdStrike fell about 8%. Both reports cite people familiar with the matter and say no agreement is guaranteed; neither company commented. Any transaction would likely need the backing of Zscaler chief executive Jay Chaudhry, its largest shareholder.
