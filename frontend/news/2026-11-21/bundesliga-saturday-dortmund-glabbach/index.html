Dortmund can go top at Gladbach as the Bundesliga fills Saturday

Borussia Dortmund, a point behind Bayern with a game in hand, visit Borussia Mönchengladbach on Saturday knowing a win takes them top on 24 points. Five more matches fill the afternoon: Leverkusen host Paderborn, Union Berlin meet RB Leipzig, Eintracht Frankfurt face Hoffenheim, Elversberg welcome Freiburg and Schalke, 17th, take on Stuttgart in the late kick-off. Bayern, held by Köln on Friday, are next in action at Lille in the Champions League on Wednesday.
