Anthropic mathematician posts a Lean proof claim for the Riemann hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, an Anthropic mathematician, said on 20 November that he and Claude had found a proof of the Riemann hypothesis and, in the same development, the generalized version covering Dirichlet L-functions. He released a four-page note and a Lean formalisation rather than a paper, leaving reviewers to confirm that the theorem statements and definitions match the standard ones. MIT's Andrew Sutherland, who compiled it himself, called it an historic moment; Scientific American reported that independent mathematicians consider the proof almost certainly correct while checks continued.
