Wall Street closes in correction as the AI trade cracks

The S&P 500 fell 3.70% to 6,910.39 and the Dow lost 1.93% to 48,147.33 on Friday, putting both in correction territory, while the Nasdaq dropped 5.28% to 22,617.73 and erased its gains for the year. Nvidia fell 14.01% and AMD, Broadcom and Micron all fell by double digits; all 20 chip stocks in the Nasdaq-100 were in a bear market. The VIX closed at 35.89, up 26.2%. For the week the S&P 500 lost 8.6%, its worst since April 2025, and the Nasdaq 10%.
