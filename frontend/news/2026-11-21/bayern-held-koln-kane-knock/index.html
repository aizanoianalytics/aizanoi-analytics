Bayern drop points at home as Köln hold on and Kane limps off

Bayern Munich were held 1-1 by Köln at the Allianz Arena on Friday, Mikey Moore rounding Jonas Urbig on 12 minutes before Nathaniel Brown levelled from the edge of the area on 37. Harry Kane came off with a knock after 20 minutes and Michael Olise was replaced at half-time, while Jamal Musiala returned on 70. The draw leaves Bayern on 22 points from ten games, one clear of Borussia Dortmund, who visit Borussia Mönchengladbach on Saturday. Köln stay 14th on ten points.
