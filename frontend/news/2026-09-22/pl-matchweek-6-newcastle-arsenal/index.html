Premier League Matchweek 6 sets up a Newcastle-Arsenal title-race fixture at St James' Park

Premier League Matchweek 6 opens on Saturday September 27 with Brentford versus Manchester United and closes on Monday September 29 with Everton hosting West Ham United. The headline fixture is Sunday's Newcastle United against Arsenal at St James' Park, where Mikel Arteta's side has historically struggled. Liverpool, the only side still on a perfect 15-point Premier League run, travels to Crystal Palace on Saturday in a Community Shield rematch.
