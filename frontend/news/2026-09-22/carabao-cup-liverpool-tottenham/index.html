Szoboszlai's stoppage-time stunner sends Liverpool past Tottenham and into the Carabao Cup fourth round

Liverpool advanced to the Carabao Cup fourth round with a 3-1 win over Tottenham at Anfield on Tuesday, with Alexis Mac Allister (21'), Cody Gakpo (54') and Dominik Szoboszlai (91') on the scoresheet and Conor Gallagher replying for Spurs in the 69th minute. The result extended Tottenham's winless run at Anfield to 15 years and ended a 339-minute Premier-League-level goal drought for Roberto De Zerbi's side, who travel to Aston Villa on Saturday in the Premier League.
