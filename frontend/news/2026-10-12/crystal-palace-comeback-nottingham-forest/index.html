Crystal Palace end a 14-year wait against Nottingham Forest

Crystal Palace came from behind to beat Nottingham Forest 3-1 at Selhurst Park on Sunday, ending a 14-year wait for a Premier League win over the visitors. Forest led inside three minutes after a misplaced header from Anan Khalaili let Dan Ndoye score, but Daichi Kamada levelled on 41 minutes and Jefferson Lerma struck in first-half stoppage time. Yeremy Pino added a third on 59 minutes, his first goal after 19 Premier League appearances without one.
