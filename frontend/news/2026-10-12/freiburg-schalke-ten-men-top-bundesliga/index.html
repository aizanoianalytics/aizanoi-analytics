Ten-man Freiburg beat Schalke to go top of the Bundesliga

Freiburg beat Schalke 2-1 while a man down to move top of the Bundesliga on goal difference after matchday five. Matthias Ginter's header from a corner was parried and Maximilian Eggestein tapped in from close range inside ten minutes, but Schalke levelled on the half-hour through an effort that deflected in off captain Kenan Karaman. Freiburg played the last 26 minutes with ten men after a dismissal, and substitute Keisuke Goto scored the winner late on. Freiburg end the weekend on 13 points.
