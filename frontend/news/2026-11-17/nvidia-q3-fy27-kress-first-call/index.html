Nvidia reports into the unknown, with Kress in the chair and $109bn expected

Nvidia reports fiscal third-quarter results after Tuesday's US close, the first since Jensen Huang resigned and chief financial officer Colette Kress became interim chief executive. The company guided in August to $108bn of revenue, give or take 2%, with a 74% gross margin; analysts expect about $109bn and adjusted earnings near $2.47 a share. Investors are watching the fourth-quarter outlook, margin trajectory and any signal from Kress on the permanent succession search.
