Football

Six quarter-finalists are set and Friday's draw cannot repeat a group fixture

UEFA draws the Nations League quarter-finals on Friday at 12:00 CET in Nyon, pairing France, Germany, England and the Group A4 winner with Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain and the A4 runner-up. Teams that met in the group stage cannot be drawn together again, which rules out Germany against the Netherlands and England against Spain. The quarter-finals are played over two legs between 25 and 30 March; the finals host has not been announced.
