Nasdaq closes in correction as Nvidia's leadership crisis becomes a market problem

US stocks fell sharply on Monday as Nvidia's leadership crisis and doubts about AI valuations spread across chipmakers. The Nasdaq Composite lost 2.49% to 24,509.76, more than 10% below its 6 October closing high, the S&P 500 fell 1.92% to 7,416.59 and the Dow dropped 739.38 points, or 1.41%, to 51,608.17. The Philadelphia semiconductor index lost 5.3% and Nvidia closed 4.1% lower at $156.14 on the eve of its results.
