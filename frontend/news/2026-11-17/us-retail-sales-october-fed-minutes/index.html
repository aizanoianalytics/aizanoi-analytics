October retail sales test the US consumer two days before the Fed's minutes

October US retail sales are due at 8:30am New York time on Tuesday, with consensus at a 0.2% monthly gain after September's 0.8% and the GDP-bearing control group seen up 0.4%. Gasoline stations accounted for most of September's increase, so the detail matters more than the headline for a Federal Reserve weighing a December move. Minutes of the 27-28 October meeting follow on Wednesday, with the 10-year Treasury yield at 5.12%.
