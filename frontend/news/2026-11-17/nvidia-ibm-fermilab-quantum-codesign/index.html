Nvidia and IBM put quantum error correction on a supercomputing schedule

Nvidia, IBM and the US Department of Energy's Fermilab are building a modular quantum-classical codesign platform, colocating Nvidia DGX B200 systems with IBM quantum computers including the Nighthawk processor. Separately Nvidia released a decoder for its CUDA-Q library that it says speeds error-correction research by up to 13,000 times, cutting one design evaluation from 12 weeks to 45 minutes. The work targets error correction and high-energy physics rather than a commercial machine.
