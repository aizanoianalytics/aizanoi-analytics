APEC lands in Shenzhen with an AI roadmap and a three-way summit on the agenda

Leaders of 21 APEC economies gather in Shenzhen on 18 and 19 November, with China pushing a Shenzhen declaration, a Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific document and a roadmap on artificial intelligence and the digital economy. Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin are due in the same room on 19 November, the first three-way session of its kind, after the September truce cutting tariffs on $30bn of each other's goods was extended to 10 January.
