Denmark need a point, Norway need a win, and Group A4 finishes in Copenhagen

Denmark host Norway in Copenhagen on Tuesday needing a point to finish second behind Portugal, who are already through on 12 points and play Wales in Cardiff. Norway, on six points, would draw level with a win and edge ahead on their head-to-head record after beating Denmark 3-2 in September. Denmark's goal difference also keeps top spot live if Portugal lose. Both matches kick off at 20:45 CET.
