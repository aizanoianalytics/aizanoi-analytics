OpenAI opens a free CSAM classifier and starts pausing ChatGPT accounts for a day

OpenAI released an image classifier for child sexual abuse material, including AI-generated images, and is giving vetted organisations free access through its API. Built with the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, the model flags 48% fewer images for human review while finding roughly the same number of abusive images, the company said. ChatGPT also gains warning screens, removal of the flagged conversation and a 24-hour access pause, with repeat offences potentially closing accounts.
