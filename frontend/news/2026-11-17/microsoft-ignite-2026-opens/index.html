Microsoft opens Ignite with agents, not devices, at the centre of the keynote

Microsoft opens its Ignite conference in San Francisco on Tuesday, with chairman and chief executive Satya Nadella and commercial chief Judson Althoff leading a keynote expected to centre on agents rather than new hardware. The company has been positioning Agent 365, its agent control plane, as an observability layer spanning models, assistants and third-party agents. Sessions run through 20 November; Microsoft has not published its announcements in advance and its Book of News is due after the keynote.
