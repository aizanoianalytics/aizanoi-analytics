Bitcoin's sixth weekly loss leaves $62,000 as the line that matters

Bitcoin fell to about $62,000 over the weekend, its lowest since July, before recovering towards $64,000, extending a sixth consecutive weekly decline. Liquidations of leveraged long positions reached $165m on Saturday night after $629m on Friday, according to CoinGlass data cited by CNBC. Spot bitcoin ETFs shed $1.065bn in the week to 13 November. The token sits well below its 200-day moving average near $73,000, with Nvidia's results the next test.
