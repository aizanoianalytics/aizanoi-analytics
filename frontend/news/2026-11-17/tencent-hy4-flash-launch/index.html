Tencent's Hy4 flash pushes small-model economics to seven cents per million tokens

Tencent released Hy4 flash, a 224-billion-parameter model with 12 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context, priced at about $0.07 per million input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens. The company says it edges out its larger Hy4 preview on an internal WorkIndex benchmark at a fraction of the cost per task, and that reinforcement learning halved average output tokens. It is live across Tencent's consumer apps, with OpenRouter integration promised.
