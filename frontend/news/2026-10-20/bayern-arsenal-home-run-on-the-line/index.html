Bayern's 39-match home run meets Arsenal's league-phase streak

Bayern Munich host Arsenal on Wednesday with two long sequences in play: Bayern have gone 39 home matches unbeaten across the group and league phases, while Arsenal have won their last 14 league-phase games. Harry Kane, who scored twice in the 3-3 draw at Viking, has 15 goals in 22 appearances against Arsenal and can score in a 10th consecutive Champions League match. Real Madrid, perfect at home this season, face a Leipzig side beaten 6-1 in Munich on Saturday.
