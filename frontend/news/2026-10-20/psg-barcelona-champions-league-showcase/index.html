PSG and Barcelona meet in Paris with the league phase on the line

Paris Saint-Germain host Barcelona on Tuesday in the headline fixture of Champions League matchday three, with Michael Oliver refereeing at the Parc des Princes. PSG arrive from a 4-2 win at Manchester City; Barcelona won 4-1 at Galatasaray. Both sides are short at fullback, with Nuno Mendes out for PSG and Eric Garcia and Jules Kounde missing for Barcelona. The clubs last met in last season's league phase, when PSG won 2-1 in Montjuic.
