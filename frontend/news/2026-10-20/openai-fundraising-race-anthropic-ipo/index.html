OpenAI lines up about $30 billion before Anthropic's market debut

OpenAI is assembling commitments for a round of roughly $30 billion that two people familiar with the talks say could value it above $1.4 trillion, with the timing set by Anthropic's expected mid-November listing. The order of events carries the message: once Anthropic's disclosures are public, investors can compare the two AI businesses directly, so OpenAI is trying to price itself first.
