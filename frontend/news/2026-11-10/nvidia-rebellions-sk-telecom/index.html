Nvidia links Korean AI chips to NVLink Fusion in sovereign-AI deal

Nvidia said on 9 November that Rebellions will adopt NVLink Fusion to connect its next-generation R200 neural processing units to Nvidia's rack-scale inference platform, and that SK Telecom plans to deploy those systems in its AI data centre. The companies frame the collaboration as a sovereign-AI stack that pairs Korean silicon and models with Nvidia's interconnect and MGX infrastructure. Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang said Korea has the talent and industrial ambition to become a world leader in AI.
