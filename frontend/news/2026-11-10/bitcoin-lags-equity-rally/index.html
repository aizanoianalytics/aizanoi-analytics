Bitcoin stalls near $75,000 while the Nasdaq rallies nearly 3 percent

Bitcoin traded around $74,800 late on 9 November, barely changed, after bouncing from an 11-week low near $73,500 over the weekend, even as the Nasdaq Composite climbed 2.74 percent. The token is roughly $30,000 below its level a year ago and has stayed under $75,000 as digital assets lag the equity rebound. Traders cited a heavy run of setbacks for crypto risk appetite, including last week's midterm results, a large exchange hack and the sell-off in AI-linked stocks.
