Burn replaces the injured Guéhi as England prepare for Croatia and Spain

Newcastle defender Dan Burn, who has 11 caps, was added to England's squad on 9 November after Marc Guéhi withdrew through injury, joining Thomas Tuchel's group at Tottenham's training ground. England meet Croatia at Wembley on 12 November and Spain in Madrid on 15 November to close their Nations League group, needing to avoid defeat against Croatia to secure a quarter-final place. Captain Harry Kane, who equalled Peter Shilton's 125-cap record last window, could become the most-capped England men's player.
