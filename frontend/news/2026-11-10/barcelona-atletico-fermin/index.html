Fermín López double sends Barcelona three points clear at the top

Fermín López scored in first-half stoppage time and again after the break as Barcelona came from behind to win 2-1 at Atlético Madrid on 8 November, restoring a three-point lead over Real Madrid at the top of La Liga. Giuliano Simeone had given Atlético a 10th-minute lead before López equalised in first-half stoppage time and finished a Lamine Yamal assist on 66 minutes. Barcelona have 33 points from 12 games, Real Madrid 30 and Atlético 28. The match drew 69,788 to the Riyadh Air Metropolitano.
