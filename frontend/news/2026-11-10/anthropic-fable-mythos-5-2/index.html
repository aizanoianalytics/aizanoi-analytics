Anthropic cuts prices with Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 ahead of its IPO

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on 9 November, priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, 20 percent below the 5.1 generation and cheaper than OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. The company says outputs arrive up to 30 percent faster and that Fable targets coding, reasoning and agent work while Mythos is restricted to vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences organisations. It is Anthropic's first model release since a METR assessment found AI accelerating the lab's own research.
