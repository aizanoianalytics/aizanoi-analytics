October CPI lands Tuesday without a headline annual rate

The Bureau of Labor Statistics releases October consumer prices at 8:30 a.m. Eastern on 10 November, and for the first time in the current cycle the report will omit a year-over-year headline figure because October 2025 prices were never collected during that year's government shutdown. Economists expect a 0.3 percent monthly rise in headline prices and 0.2 percent in the core measure, after a 0.3 percent core increase in September. September's annual headline rate was 3.7 percent.
