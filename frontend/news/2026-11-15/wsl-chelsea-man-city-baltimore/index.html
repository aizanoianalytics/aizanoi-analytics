Baltimore's strike puts Chelsea back on top and hands City a first defeat

Sandy Baltimore drove Chelsea in front in first-half stoppage time and Manchester City, who lost goalkeeper Ayaka Yamashita to a red card, could not answer, so Chelsea leapfrogged them at the top of the Women's Super League and ended a run in which City had scored in each of their previous 21 matches in all competitions. Manchester United beat Crystal Palace 2-0, Liverpool won 3-0 at Brighton and Tottenham drew 0-0 with Everton; Arsenal host London City Lionesses on Sunday.
