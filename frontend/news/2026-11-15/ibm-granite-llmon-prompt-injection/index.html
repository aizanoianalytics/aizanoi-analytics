IBM trains two small Granite models to read documents without obeying them

IBM published 3B and 8B previews of Granite 4.1 trained on LLMON, a markup that labels instructions and data as separate addressable blocks so text pulled from a document cannot pass itself off as an order. On the OPI benchmark the attack success rate falls from 100% to 0.1% for the 3B model and from 96.3% to 0.02% for the 8B. IBM calls the effect learned behaviour rather than an enforced guarantee and limits the weights to evaluation.
