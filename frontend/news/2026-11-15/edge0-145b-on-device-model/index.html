Edge0 releases a 145-billion-parameter model aimed at a single laptop

Edge0 published weights for Edge0-145B and Edge0-120B, billing the larger as the first open model at that scale to run entirely on a personal machine. The company reports roughly 10 billion parameters active per step, peak memory near 29GB on a 48GB laptop, decoding above 80 tokens a second and a context window of 128K to 256K. The comparisons are its own, the technical report is still to come, and the release post names no licence.
