United becomes the first US airline to close its booking flow to AI agents

United's website terms, updated on 11 November, now require written permission before any automated agent searches, books, buys or manages a trip on united.com or in its app, and bar the use of its fares and availability data to train or ground a model. Search-engine indexing stays exempt, and bookings agents made before that date are being honoured. United cites credential sharing, unintended purchases and check-in rules that require a human to confirm what is in the bags.
