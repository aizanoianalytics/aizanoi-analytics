Three teams, two places: Group A1 goes to the last night in Brussels and Paris

Italy and France sit level on 10 points from five games, with Belgium one behind, and Sunday's closing fixtures run simultaneously at 20:45 CET: Belgium host Italy, France host a Türkiye side already fixed in fourth. Italy top the group by winning, or by drawing if France fail to win. Belgium reach the quarter-finals only by beating Italy; France need a draw against Türkiye, or a Belgian slip, to be sure of joining them.
