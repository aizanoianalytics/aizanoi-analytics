November's tape is the wildest since the tariff shock, and dip buyers keep paying

The S&P 500 has averaged a 1.6% daily move this month and the Nasdaq 100 2.4%, the largest swings for both since the tariff turmoil of April 2025, even as the S&P still managed a 2.5% weekly gain, its first since early October. Beneath the index level, money keeps chasing the most violent corners: a group of double-leveraged Nvidia funds drew about $900 million in a week in which the shares fell 13% after the chief executive's departure.
