Brussels proposes an autonomy dial for AI agents that shop on your behalf

The European Commission's draft Digital Fairness Act, published on 11 November, would replace the 2005 unfair commercial practices directive with a regulation that applies directly across the bloc. Article 26 covers software that concludes contracts for a consumer and would require a first-use choice: approve every contract, approve only categories the consumer defines by criteria such as a price ceiling and a time limit, or let the agent buy unsupervised. Traders would have to provide tools to change that setting later.
