Apple ships AirPods 5 with ANC to open earbuds, Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 with hypertension sensor

Apple has expanded its wearables line with AirPods 5, Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, putting Apple Intelligence at the centre of the refresh with Live Translation, Audio Intelligence and a new Readiness score. AirPods 5 start at $129 in the US and bring Active Noise Cancellation to the open-ear design for the first time, while Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 introduce the new Health Sensing System and S11 chip with hypertension detection support.
