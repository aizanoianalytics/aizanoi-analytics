Fenerbahçe open Champions League return against Roma with Malen and Dybala in form

Fenerbahçe end an 18-year Champions League absence on Thursday when Roma visit Ulker Stadyumu in the league-phase opener, with Ismail Kartal's side relying on Vedat Muriqi, Mason Greenwood and an ad-hoc tactical set-up to disrupt Gian Piero Gasperini's pressing system. Roma arrive on the back of three straight Serie A wins and 10 goals, with Donyell Malen on five league goals already and Paulo Dybala pulling the strings in a 3-4-1-2 shape.
