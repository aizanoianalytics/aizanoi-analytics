Bayern chase a 23rd straight Champions League opener win against in-form Bodø/Glimt

Bayern Munich open their 2026-27 Champions League campaign at home to Norwegian champions Bodø/Glimt on Thursday night, with Vincent Kompany's side targeting a 23rd consecutive opening Champions League win since 2003. The visitors arrive on an 18-game unbeaten run and have already beaten Manchester City, Atletico Madrid and Inter Milan in the competition last season, while Harry Kane and Jamal Musiala are set to return after being rested at Schalke.
