Maignan's red card sets up Bournemouth's 3-0 win over Milan

Bournemouth beat ten-man AC Milan 3-0 at the Vitality Stadium for a third straight Europa League win. Koni De Winter's handball gave Justin Kluivert an eighth-minute penalty, and Milan captain Mike Maignan was sent off in the 28th minute for pushing Tyler Adams in the face at a corner; Marcus Tavernier converted the second penalty and Amine Adli added a free kick in the 84th. Milan, also beaten at home by Benfica, host Ferencvaros next.
