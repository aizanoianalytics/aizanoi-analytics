Rodriguez's double leaves Celtic without a point

Celtic led through Mika Baur's strike a minute into the second half at Celtic Park, but Celta Vigo's Javi Rodriguez headed an equaliser in the 68th minute and drove in the winner three minutes later for a 2-1 away win. Celtic have now lost all three league-phase matches and have no points, after defeats by Ferencvaros and Benfica. A section of the Green Brigade ultras boycotted the game in protest at the running of the club.
