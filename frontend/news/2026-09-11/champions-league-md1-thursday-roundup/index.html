Champions League Matchday 1 Thursday: Bayern, Man Utd and Como cruise, Fenerbahce-Roma ends level

Bayern Munich opened the new Champions League league phase with a 5-0 home win over Bodo/Glimt after the break, while Manchester United marked their return to the competition with a 4-0 victory over Sabah at Old Trafford and Como debuted with a 4-1 home win over RB Leipzig. In Istanbul, Fenerbahce and Roma shared a 1-1 draw in their first Champions League matches in 18 and seven years respectively, with Archie Brown cancelling out Bryan Cristante's opener.
