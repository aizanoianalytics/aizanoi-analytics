Samsung taunts Apple over foldable iPhone Duo launch with 'reheating our leftovers' campaign

Samsung Mobile US has responded to Apple's $1,999 iPhone Duo launch with sarcastic social posts including 'Let us know when you're done reheating our leftovers' and 'So far, so same,' running alongside billboards in Seoul, Tokyo and London that highlight the Galaxy Z Fold 8's thin-and-light design. Counterpoint Research projects Apple could capture about 25 percent of the foldable market, with Samsung holding roughly 32 percent, framing the next phase of the premium phone battle.
