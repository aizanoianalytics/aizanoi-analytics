Fenerbahce and Roma share 1-1 draw on Champions League return in Istanbul

Fenerbahce and Roma played out a 1-1 draw at Ulker Stadyumu on Thursday, with Bryan Cristante's first-half strike for Roma cancelled out three minutes into the second half by Archie Brown after a Mason Greenwood through ball. The match was Fenerbahce's first Champions League appearance in 18 years and Roma's first since 2019, with Nathan Ake heading a late Fenerbahce effort against the crossbar in an evenly contested game.
