Suno launches v6 music models trained on licensed Warner, BMG and Believe catalogs

Suno has rolled out its v6 generation of AI music models, built in partnership with Warner Music Group, BMG and Believe, with revenue-sharing arrangements for participating rightsholders. The release introduces a flagship v6 model, a 'less predictable' v6-wild, and a free v6-mini tier, alongside new editing, isolation and mashup features aimed at professional music workflows. Suno says it will retire its previous models as v6 deploys.
