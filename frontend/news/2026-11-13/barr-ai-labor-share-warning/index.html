Fed's Barr warns AI concentration could widen inequality

Federal Reserve governor Michael Barr said the United States should prepare for serious short-term disruption in the labour market from AI and warned that market concentration could widen inequality. In remarks at Yale, he noted labour's share of national income fell to a record low 52.8% in the second quarter of 2026, from roughly 60% to 65% after the Second World War, and said competition will decide whether AI's gains are broadly shared.
