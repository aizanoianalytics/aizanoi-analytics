Crystal Palace sack Pierre Sage with the club bottom

Crystal Palace sacked manager Pierre Sage after 16 matches, with the club bottom of the Premier League on seven points from ten games. Sage, appointed in June on a three-year contract, won two league matches in that run. The club said three members of his coaching staff left with him and that an update on the appointment process would follow in due course; Sky Sports reported Palace are working on a deal for Thomas Frank.
