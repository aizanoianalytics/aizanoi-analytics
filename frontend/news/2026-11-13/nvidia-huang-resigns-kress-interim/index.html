Nvidia names Colette Kress interim CEO after Huang resigns

NVIDIA's board said Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive and director effective immediately, ending a 33-year tenure at the company he co-founded. Chief financial officer Colette Kress will hold the interim CEO role while keeping her finance post until a permanent successor is named, and the board appointed former Synopsys chief executive Harvey C. Jones as its first chair. NVIDIA kept third-quarter results scheduled for November 17.
