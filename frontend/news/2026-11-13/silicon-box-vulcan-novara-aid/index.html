Italy backs Silicon Box's €3.2bn packaging plant in Novara

Italy cleared €1.3 billion in state aid for Silicon Box's Vulcan Project, a €3.2 billion advanced packaging and testing plant in Novara that would be the Singapore company's first factory outside Singapore. The site is designed for panel-level packaging that combines multiple chiplets into one package for AI, high-performance computing and automotive customers, with about 1,600 jobs planned and construction due to begin in early 2027.
