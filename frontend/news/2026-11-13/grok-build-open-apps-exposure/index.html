Audit finds most published Grok Build apps need no sign-in

Claroc Research said 35,578 of 37,571 live apps published under SpaceXSI's grok.me domain opened without a sign-in, about 94.7% of those it tested. The security firm said 24 of the open apps were built by SpaceXSI and X staff and held a confidential onboarding plan, a sales territory map of 594 accounts and advertising proposals, and that apps from about 30 other organisations exposed internal data. SpaceXSI removed its own staff apps after press questions.
