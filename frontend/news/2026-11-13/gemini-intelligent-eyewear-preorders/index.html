Gemini eyewear opens for pre-order from $395

Google and Samsung opened pre-orders for Intelligent Eyewear built with Gemini, with Warby Parker frames starting at $395 and Gentle Monster models from $449. The glasses weigh about 41 grams, carry a 12-megapixel camera, four microphones and up to nine hours of battery, and reach stores on December 10 in the United States and South Korea. At launch they pair with recent iPhones, Pixel 7 or newer and Samsung Galaxy S or Z phones.
