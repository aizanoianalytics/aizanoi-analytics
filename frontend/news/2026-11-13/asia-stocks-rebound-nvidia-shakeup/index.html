Asian stocks steady as investors digest Nvidia leadership shock

Asian equities recovered part of Thursday's slide as investors reassessed Nvidia's leadership change: the MSCI Asia Pacific Index rose 0.3%, South Korea's Kospi gained 1.97% and Japan's Nikkei 225 added 1.48%. Nvidia fell as much as 14% before closing 4.3% lower, while Brent held near $96 a barrel and Treasury yields stayed close to multi-week highs. US index futures were mixed after the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 both closed lower.
