Google ships Gemini 4 Hydrogen as its fast default model

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen as the successor to Gemini 3.8 Flash, priced flat at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens across a one-million-token context. Google says it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster at default settings, scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index against 50 for Gemini 4 Argon, and lifts Terminal-Bench 4.1 from 18.2% to 57%. Four thinking levels, including a new xhigh tier, ship with it.
