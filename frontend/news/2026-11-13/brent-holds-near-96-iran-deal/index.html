Brent holds near $96 as Iran deal hopes dim

Brent crude held near $96 a barrel after rising 3.3% on Thursday, when President Donald Trump said the United States could forgo a deal with Iran, dimming hopes of an agreement to end the war that has disrupted Gulf supplies. West Texas Intermediate traded below $89. Reports of fresh friction around the Strait of Hormuz kept risk premiums elevated even as Brent headed for a second consecutive weekly loss.
