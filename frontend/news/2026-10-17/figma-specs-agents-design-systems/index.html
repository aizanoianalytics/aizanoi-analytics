Figma adds a specification layer for human and software designers

Figma announced Specs, a Markdown-based editor where teams can describe design language, connect libraries and give agents a shared reference for brand rules. The company also moved code layers toward public beta and expanded its model-context integrations, treating design-system context as a way to make AI-generated prototypes more predictable.
