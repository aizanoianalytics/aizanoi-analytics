Amazon’s Bee adds two personal AI devices

Amazon-owned Bee introduced the wrist-or-clothing Bee Frontier and the phone-mounted Bee Eclipse, expanding its push into ambient personal computing. The devices turn conversations into notes, summaries and tasks; the Frontier starts at $74.99, while Bee also offers a paid tier with longer memory and integrations. The product makes privacy controls central to a category built around microphones and continuous context.
