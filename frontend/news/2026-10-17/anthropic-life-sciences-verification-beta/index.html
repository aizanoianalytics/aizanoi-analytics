Anthropic opens beta verification program for life-science teams

Anthropic has opened applications for a beta program that gives life-science professionals access to its Mythos, Opus and Sonnet models with safeguards tuned for biology-related work. The company says the program is aimed at drug discovery, research biology, clinical development and manufacturing, while initially limiting participation to teams and institutions.
