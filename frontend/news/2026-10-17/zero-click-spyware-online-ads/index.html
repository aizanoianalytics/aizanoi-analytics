Citizen Lab warns online ads can deliver zero-click spyware

Citizen Lab says governments are abusing online advertising systems to target people with zero-click spyware, a threat that can compromise devices without a user opening a malicious link. The report adds ad-tech supply chains to the surveillance-security conversation and raises questions about how platforms vet buyers, creatives and delivery infrastructure.
