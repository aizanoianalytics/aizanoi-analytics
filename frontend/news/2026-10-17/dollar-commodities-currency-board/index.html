Dollar moves keep commodities and currency desks on alert

Reuters’ currency and commodities snapshot shows the dollar, oil, gold and copper still moving together as investors price geopolitical risk and the outlook for growth. The cross-asset picture matters for importers and central banks alike: a stronger dollar can tighten financial conditions while changing the local cost of energy and raw materials.
