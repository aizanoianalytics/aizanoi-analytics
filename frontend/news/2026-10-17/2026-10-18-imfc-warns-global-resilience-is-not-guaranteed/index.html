IMFC warns that shock resilience is becoming harder to sustain

The IMF’s policy-setting committee says the global economy has absorbed recent shocks better than expected, but warns that wars, trade disruptions, energy pressures, debt and higher refinancing costs are tightening the room for manoeuvre. Its statement puts the burden on governments to rebuild buffers rather than assume another shock will be manageable.
