IBM study puts AI incidents on more EMEA board agendas

IBM says eight in ten surveyed EMEA enterprises now escalate AI-related incidents to a board or C-suite, while governance and accountability are lagging behind adoption. The finding frames agent deployment as a management-control problem as much as a technical one, with organizations under pressure to define who owns failures before autonomous systems spread further.
