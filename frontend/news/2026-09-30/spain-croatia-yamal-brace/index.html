Yamal's brace carries Spain past Croatia and extends a 40-match run

Lamine Yamal scored twice and set up another goal as Spain beat Croatia 4-1 in Seville on Nations League matchday two, extending their unbeaten run inside 90 minutes to 40 matches. Yamal opened the scoring in the second minute, Marc Pubill restored the lead after Dion Beljo had equalised on his third cap, Yamal struck again on 63 minutes and Nico Williams completed the scoring late on. Spain finished with 64% possession and 15 shots to Croatia's five, leaving the world champions top of Group A3.
