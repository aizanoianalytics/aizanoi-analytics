Trump and six AI chief executives sign a voluntary safety accord

President Donald Trump said he and the heads of Google, Anthropic, Meta, Nvidia, OpenAI and X signed a voluntary accord at the White House covering four commitments: internal cybersecurity controls, an internal team verifying that monitoring works, an independent external assessor, and a board committee to receive the reports. Trump called the document 'morally binding' and said he is weighing a ten-person committee to oversee the sector, while repeating that his administration would not slow AI development. Reuters reported that bipartisan Senate AI legislation has stalled.
