Rangers host Kilmarnock at Ibrox on a five-match winning run

Rangers welcome Kilmarnock to Ibrox on Saturday in Scottish Premiership matchday eight, with the hosts unbeaten in their last five matches across league and cup, a run that includes a League Cup quarter-final win over Celtic and a 1-0 league victory at Celtic Park. Kilmarnock arrive mid-table after a draw with Heart of Midlothian and a win at Hibernian, having lost their earlier League Cup quarter-final to Aberdeen on penalties. Hearts and Celtic continue to set the pace at the top of the table.
