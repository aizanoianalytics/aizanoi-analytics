Barcelona target a record eighth straight win as Getafe visit Camp Nou

La Liga leaders Barcelona host Getafe at Camp Nou on Saturday in matchday eight, chasing an eighth straight league win that would equal their best-ever start to a top-flight season. Hansi Flick's side have won all seven league games, scoring 31 goals and conceding seven, and lead second-placed Atletico Madrid by five points. Forward Raphinha is out with a thigh problem and Frenkie de Jong and Andreas Christensen also miss out; Getafe sit 11th with eight points and only four league goals this season.
