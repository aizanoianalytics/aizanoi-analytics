Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit set for September 22-24 in Maui with a 2nm Gen 6 flagship leading the agenda

Qualcomm's ninth annual Snapdragon Summit runs September 22-24 in Maui, where the company is expected to unveil the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 as the first 2nm mobile flagship silicon to reach scale in the Android tier. The Gen 6 is positioned as the chip behind next year's premium Android lineup, including the Galaxy S27 and Xiaomi 18, and the keynote also frames Qualcomm's annual pivot beyond the smartphone into PC, automotive and XR reference designs. Apple's earlier A20 Pro launch on September 9 sets up a back-to-back 2nm mobile comparison that is the throughline of the fall hardware season.
