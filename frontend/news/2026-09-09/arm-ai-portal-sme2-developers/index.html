Arm's AI Portal widens as 22 million developers land on Execuite, LiteRT and ONNX-RT day-one paths

Arm's newly launched AI Portal is exposing pre-optimised models from Alibaba's Qwen, Google's Gemma and Ultralytics' YOLO across ExecuTorch, LiteRT and ONNX Runtime, the first three runtimes shipping on day one alongside the CSS for Mobile 2 platform. CSS for Mobile 2 is the first wired-up hardware tier, with the Mali G2-Ultra NX GPU and its dedicated neural accelerators feeding the agentic AI and neural-graphics workloads Arm expects to dominate the next device cycle. The portal also lets developers bring proprietary models for target-specific optimisations.
