Mistral closes €3 billion Samsung-led Series D as Europe's largest tech round ever

French AI lab Mistral closed a €3 billion Series D led by Samsung Electronics, with co-leads EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity, lifting its valuation above €21 billion as Europe's largest-ever private tech round. The same day in Paris, Samsung chairman Jay Y. Lee and Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch signed a parallel strategic partnership covering joint manufacturing AI and R&D cooperation, extending Samsung's reach into the European open-weight ecosystem while ASML and a16z also backed the round.
