Serie A matchday 2 wrapped with wins for Milan, Juventus, Inter, Roma and Atalanta, Napoli beaten at home

Italy's Serie A completed matchday 2 by 31 August 2026, with AC Milan beating Venezia 2-0, Juventus downing Parma 2-0, Inter winning 1-0 away at Cagliari, Roma thrashing Lecce 4-0, Atalanta edging Bologna 1-0 and Napoli surprisingly losing 1-2 at home to Como. The early-season standings already separate the unbeaten top four from promoted sides Frosinone and Monza, with Sassuolo's 2-1 home win over Torino another of the round's talking points.
