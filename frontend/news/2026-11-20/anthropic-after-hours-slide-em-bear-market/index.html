Anthropic slips below its debut low after hours, and emerging markets enter a bear market

Anthropic, which completed the largest initial public offering on record on Thursday, fell 4.7% in after-hours trading to $92.60, below its lowest level during the debut session, according to Bloomberg. The shares had closed 17% above their $83 offer price. Global equities headed for a seventh straight decline, the longest since September 2023, and MSCI's emerging-market index extended its fall from a recent peak to 20%, the threshold of a technical bear market. The dollar was set for a fourth weekly gain, helped by overseas investors converting currency to buy the new stock.
