BSI's survey finds 54% of business leaders using AI to cut headcount, up from 41%

A BSI poll of 1,287 business leaders across nine countries, published on 17 November, reports that 54% now say AI is helping reduce headcount, against 41% in 2025. Forty-nine per cent say AI has already reduced entry-level opportunities, and 42% say they would be less likely to promote someone who sends them AI-generated content. The same release reports 53% of leaders using AI to justify efficiencies or recruitment freezes, and notes separate BSI research in which 75% of firms reported at least one AI failure. BSI frames the risk as lost pathways to expertise rather than lost posts alone.
