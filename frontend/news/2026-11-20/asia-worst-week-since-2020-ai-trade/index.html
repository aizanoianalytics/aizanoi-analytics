Asia heads for its worst week since 2020 as the AI trade loses its bid

MSCI's Asia-Pacific index fell 2.5% on Friday and 7% over the week, its worst run since March 2020, with the Nikkei down 4.1% and the KOSPI down 4.7% on the day. US futures pointed lower and the dollar held at 103.86 after touching its strongest level since April 2025. Fed funds futures now price a quarter-point hike next month as close to certain, and Brent settled flat at $91.98 for a third weekly decline. Anthropic's record listing, which Capital.com's Kyle Rodda said was 'not enough to assuage the overall concern about the outlook for the AI trade', did not steady the region.
