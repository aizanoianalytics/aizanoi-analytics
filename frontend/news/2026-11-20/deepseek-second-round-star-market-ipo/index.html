DeepSeek closes a second round of about 100bn yuan and lines up a STAR Market listing

Business registration changes filed on 17 November show a partnership representing DeepSeek's new backers holding roughly 17.3% of the Hangzhou developer, Caixin reports, completing a second external round of about 100 billion yuan ($15 billion). The company is preparing an initial public offering on Shanghai's STAR Market. Its first round, closed in July, raised 50 billion yuan at a 350 billion yuan post-investment valuation. With American export rules constraining Nvidia hardware, Caixin reports DeepSeek is building on Huawei Ascend supernodes and planning a gigawatt of capacity in Ulanqab.
