Football

Kompany returns to the bench with Bayern level on points and Kane on four blank games

Bayern Munich open matchday 10 at home to Cologne on Friday night with 21 points from nine games, level with Borussia Dortmund, and Vincent Kompany back in the dugout a month after collapsing on 23 October. Cologne are 15th on nine points and without a win in four. Harry Kane has not scored in four Bundesliga matches, 389 minutes, his longest drought in Germany, though he leads the assist chart with seven. Bayern are without Davies, Neuer, Diaz, Gnabry and Saibari with Jamal Musiala pushing for a return to the squad. Cologne are missing Dallinga, Hubers, Skhiri and van den Berg.
