Intuit sells Mailchimp for $3.68bn, a third of what it paid, and buys into the buyer

Intuit agreed to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons at an enterprise value of $3.68 billion: $3.31 billion in cash plus $368 million of the proceeds reinvested as new Bending Spoons stock. Intuit paid about $12 billion for the business in 2021. Mailchimp revenue fell 1% to $313 million in the quarter to 31 October, and Intuit cut full-year revenue guidance on a weaker Credit Karma outlook even as first-quarter revenue rose 12% to $4.354 billion. Shares fell by double digits after the bell. The deal is expected to close in early 2027, and Intuit intends to spend the cash on buybacks.
