Apple's job listings describe an agent that uses the computer, not just the app

Job postings reported by Bloomberg and relayed by 9to5Mac show Apple recruiting a senior engineering manager for a 'proactive, agentic AI assistant' that browses the web, works across apps, runs commands, writes and debugs code and coordinates specialised sub-agents, while keeping the work private. The listings follow Apple's tighter macOS Full Disk Access controls for third-party agents in the 27.3 beta. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman has reported Apple wants Siri to behave more like an autonomous agent, possibly next year. The postings read as early-stage hiring.
