Frank inherits a Palace side that is last, and says the league comes before Europe

Thomas Frank held his first news conference as Crystal Palace manager on Thursday, three days after replacing Pierre Sage, and said he would prioritise the Premier League over the Europa League to lift the club off the bottom. 'Reading the table, I can see we are last. That needs to be changed,' he said. Palace are bottom for the first time in nine years and visit Coventry City on Saturday. Takehiro Tomiyasu should train next week; Ismaila Sarr's groin injury has no date. Frank said Palace are close to hiring Chelsea's set-piece coach.
