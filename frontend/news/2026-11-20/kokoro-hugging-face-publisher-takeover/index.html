A lapsed domain was enough to take over the account behind a 13-million-download voice model

Researcher Alon Hertz and his team registered hexgrad.com, the expired domain listed in the Kokoro project's public DONATE.md, and used it to reset the password on the Hugging Face account publishing Kokoro-82M, a text-to-speech model with nearly 13 million monthly downloads. They then fine-tuned its weights in 87 seconds on a gaming GPU so the audio no longer matched the input text, with no change to inference code. The altered weights were never uploaded and the account was secured. The lesson is distribution trust: pin revisions.
