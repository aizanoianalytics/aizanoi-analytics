Guimaraes returns to St James' Park with the champions, and Newcastle go in short up front

Newcastle host Arsenal on Saturday at 17:30 GMT with Joelinton awaiting a specialist opinion on a thigh injury, Tino Livramento managed carefully and a strikeforce of Yoane Wissa, William Osula and Matias Fernandez-Pardo that has two Premier League goals between them this season. Matthias Jaissle said he tried everything to keep Bruno Guimaraes, who left for Arsenal in the summer and returns to Tyneside for the first time, and called Mikel Arteta 'marvellous'. Newcastle drew 1-1 at Fulham before the break and now face the reigning champions at St James' Park.
