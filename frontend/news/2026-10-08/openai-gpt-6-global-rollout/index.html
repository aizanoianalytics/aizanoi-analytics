OpenAI opens GPT-6 to all ChatGPT users with a new interactive answer format

OpenAI began rolling GPT-6 out across ChatGPT, starting October 7 for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts and reaching Free and Go users on October 8. Paid tiers receive GPT-6 Sol while Free and Go get GPT-6 Luna. The release also introduces Intelligent UI, which lets answers combine text, charts and interactive elements as the model writes. OpenAI says GPT-6 Instant begins answering search-heavy prompts about 44% sooner than GPT-5.6 Instant, and that its 1.2 billion weekly users will eventually be covered.
