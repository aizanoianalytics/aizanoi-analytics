Amazon puts Transcribe Turbo speech-to-text into public preview for voice agents

Amazon made Transcribe Turbo, its next-generation speech-to-text model available in public preview in US East (Ohio) for batch and streaming. Streaming covers six languages and cuts word error rate 14% in English and 11% elsewhere versus the current production model. Two features ship at no extra charge: semantic turn-taking, which responds in about 615 ms with a 5% interruption rate, and runtime ASR hints, which raised menu-routing accuracy from 87% to 97% on English customer-service data.
