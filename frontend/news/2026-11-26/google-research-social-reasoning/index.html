Google Research open-sources a social-reasoning benchmark with verifiable ground truth

Google Research published a framework that tests whether LLM assistants can infer a target's hidden motive from a user's subjective account. A target agent with a controlled motive interacts with other agents, including a user proxy that consults the assistant; the seeded motive provides verifiable ground truth. Across 12 models, assistants were less accurate than when observing events directly and adopted unfounded user suspicions. The framework and a dataset of more than 20,000 generated examples are open-sourced.
