Fernandes scores twice as Sporting hold United to a 2-2 draw

Manchester United were held 2-2 by Sporting in Lisbon, denying Mauricio Pochettino a first win. Luis Suarez scored from the spot in the 33rd minute after Lammens saved his first penalty, and Bruno Fernandes levelled from a penalty on 48 minutes before going ahead two minutes later when a shot bounced off a divot over goalkeeper Kaique Pereira. Suarez equalised in the 80th minute after Lammens spilled a high ball, a late United goal was disallowed, and Tielemans was sent off late.
