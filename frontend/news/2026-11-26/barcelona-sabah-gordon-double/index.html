Gordon scores twice as Barcelona beat Sabah 4-1 in Baku

Barcelona beat Sabah 4-1 in Baku as Anthony Gordon scored twice, with Adeyemi and Gabriel Jesus also scoring. Gordon broke the deadlock in the 31st minute when goalkeeper Stas Pokatilov failed to hold a cross, and struck again just after half-time from a rebound after Pokatilov denied Jesus. Alejandro Balde set up Adeyemi four minutes later, and Jesus converted on the hour after Pokatilov parried Kounde's header from a Gordon corner. It was a fourth win in five league-phase games.
