JetBrains releases Mellum2.1 Instruct, a faster open coding model for sub-agents

JetBrains released Mellum2.1 Instruct, the non-reasoning sibling of its open 12B mixture-of-experts coding model. It answers directly, using reinforcement-learning post-training on repository-level tasks. JetBrains says it generates about half the tokens of the Thinking variant on agentic coding and doubled its score on SWE-bench Verified over the previous Instruct release. A multi-token prediction head makes generation up to 1.8 times faster. Weights are on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0.
