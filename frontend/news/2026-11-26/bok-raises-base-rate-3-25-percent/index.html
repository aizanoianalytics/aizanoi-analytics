Bank of Korea raises base rate to 3.25% and lifts 2027 growth forecast to 4.1%

The Bank of Korea raised its base rate by 25 basis points to 3.25 percent with five members in favour and two voting to hold at 3.00 percent. It lifted its 2026 growth forecast to 3.9 percent from 3.3 percent and its 2027 forecast to 4.1 percent from 2.9 percent, citing strong semiconductor exports and investment. Inflation was projected at 2.7 percent this year and 2.4 percent next, above the 2 percent target, with further hikes dependent on inflation, growth and financial stability.
