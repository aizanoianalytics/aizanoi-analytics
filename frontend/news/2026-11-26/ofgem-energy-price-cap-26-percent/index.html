Ofgem raises UK energy price cap 26% to £2,166 from January

Ofgem said the price cap on default tariffs will rise 26 percent for 1 January to 31 March 2027, lifting the annualised bill for a typical direct-debit gas and electricity household to £2,166 from £1,723. 20 million households on standard variable tariffs are affected; 10 million on fixed deals are shielded. Ofgem blamed Middle East conflict for higher wholesale gas prices, which rose faster than electricity. The government said 20 million households will get £100 towards bills from January.
