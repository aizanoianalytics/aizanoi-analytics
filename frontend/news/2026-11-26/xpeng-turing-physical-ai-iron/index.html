XPENG unveils Turing physical AI architecture and debuts IRON humanoid

XPENG unveiled its Turing Physical AI Architecture (VLA 3.0) at its eighth AI Day on November 25, linking cognition, real-time decisions and physical execution across vehicles and robots. Three layers run from body control to vision-language task planning. XPENG also debuted the IRON humanoid, with 76 degrees of freedom and more than 1,500 tactile sensing points. VLA 2.0 version 6.4.0 raises agent-driven closed-loop training speed by 10 to 12 times.
