FCC authorizes SpaceX to deploy one million orbital data centers

The FCC authorized SpaceX on November 25 to deploy up to one million orbital compute modules for its Starmind constellation, the first U.S. authorization for a constellation of data centers in space. The order allows radiofrequency backup telemetry, tracking and command links, and optical inter-satellite links backed by 18.8-19.3 GHz downlink and 28.6-29.1 GHz uplink communications. Other orbital data center proposals remain under review.
