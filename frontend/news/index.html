Google puts TPUs in orbit to test the case for space-based AI compute

Google has launched a prototype satellite carrying its Trillium TPUs, a first step in a research moonshot it calls Project Suncatcher. The company published a preprint describing solar-powered satellites in dawn-dusk sun-synchronous orbit, linked by free-space optical lasers, and reported proton-beam tests in which the chips withstood nearly three times the expected five-year radiation dose.

Sources Google Research · CNBC

AWS open-sources a small decision model for agent workflows

Amazon Web Services released Strands Decider 2B, a roughly 2-billion-parameter model that scores supplied answer options instead of generating text. The company said it fine-tuned Alibaba's Qwen3.5-2B base, removed the component that predicts the next word and replaced it with a pointer over the available choices, adding a small LoRA update, and published the weights under an Apache 2.0 licence.
