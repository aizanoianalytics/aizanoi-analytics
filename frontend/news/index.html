ChatGPT becomes first AI chatbot to fall under the EU's Digital Services Act as a 'very large' platform

The European Commission added ChatGPT to its list of very large online platforms under the Digital Services Act on August 31, the first AI chatbot to face that level of scrutiny, alongside Reddit and Roblox.
