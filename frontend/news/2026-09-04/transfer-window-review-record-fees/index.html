Premier League's record summer: £125m Fernández move caps window that reshaped the title race

The Premier League's summer window closed on 1 September with Manchester City signing Chelsea midfielder Enzo Fernández for a British-record £125 million on deadline day, per Sky Sports and BBC Sport deal trackers, after a window in which Chelsea bought Morgan Rogers from Aston Villa for £117 million and Liverpool paid £123 million for Bradley Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain. Arsenal added Newcastle's Bruno Guimarães for £75 million as clubs finalised squads until January. English clubs now break for international duty before league play resumes on 12 September.
