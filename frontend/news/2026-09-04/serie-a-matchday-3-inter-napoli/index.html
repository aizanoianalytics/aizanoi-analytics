Serie A matchday 3 brings Inter-Napoli and Juventus-Milan as the early table splits

Serie A's third matchday runs 4-7 September with a headline Saturday meeting of Inter and Napoli at San Siro, both teams on maximum points after two rounds, and a Juventus-Milan clash at the Allianz Stadium on Sunday. Roma, who opened with a 4-0 win over Fiorentina, host Atalanta on Saturday, while Genoa-Como opened the round on Friday evening. The fixture list was confirmed by the league before the season and leaves matchday 3 as the first genuine test of the early pace-setters.
