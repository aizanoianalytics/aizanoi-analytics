Premier League, Bundesliga, LALIGA and Serie A demand FIFA governance reform

The Premier League, Bundesliga, LALIGA and Lega Calcio Serie A issued a joint statement entitled “To Unify the Game, FIFA Must Reform” on 25 September. It says football’s foundations have been threatened by a fundamental governance problem, argues the organisation is not fit for purpose, and criticises the promotion of exploitative commercial ideas tied to the scrapped FIFA Forward Enterprise proposals.
