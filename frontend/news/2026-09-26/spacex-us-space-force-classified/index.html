SpaceX flies classified USSF-385 Space Force mission from Vandenberg

SpaceX launched the classified USSF-385 mission from Space Launch Complex 4E at Vandenberg at 7:00 a.m. PT on Saturday, and booster B1100 landed on the drone ship Of Course I Still Love You about eight and a half minutes later. It was the booster’s tenth flight and tenth successful landing, and USSF-385 is the fourth Space Force launch from the same pad in roughly six weeks.
