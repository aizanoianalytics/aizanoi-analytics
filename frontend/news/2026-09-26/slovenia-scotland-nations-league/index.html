Scotland open the Pocognoli era with a goalless Nations League draw in Ljubljana

Slovenia and Scotland finished 0-0 in Ljubljana on Saturday, handing Sébastien Pocognoli a point from his first match in charge. Sky Sports reported that Scotland were held to a goalless draw, with the visitors missing the chance to take all three in a physical League A meeting.
