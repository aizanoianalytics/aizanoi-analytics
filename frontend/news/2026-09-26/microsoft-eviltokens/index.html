Microsoft disrupts AI-assisted cybercrime platform EvilTokens

Microsoft says its Digital Crimes Unit and industry partners disrupted EvilTokens, a subscription-based phishing-as-a-service platform that used AI across the attack chain. The company linked the service to more than 12,000 compromised inboxes, and UK police arrested two suspects.
