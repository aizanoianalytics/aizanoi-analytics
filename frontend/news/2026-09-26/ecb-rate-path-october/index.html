ECB leaves the October tightening question open after its 2.50% decision

With the deposit rate already lifted to 2.50% in September, the Governing Council’s guidance and the updated staff projections leave a further 25 basis point increase possible in October. Traders are now pricing that path against a euro-area inflation print that the energy shock has kept above the 2% target.
