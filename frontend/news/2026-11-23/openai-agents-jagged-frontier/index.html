OpenAI says its agents now finish 46% of its own multi-hour research tasks

OpenAI's second research acceleration report puts autonomous agent success on tasks estimated at two to four hours of human work at 46% in October, up from 23% in March. For four-to-eight-hour tasks the rate rose from 13% to 35%. Results are uneven: agents succeeded 79% of the time at monitoring training runs but 25% at writing inference and serving code. Median agent spending reached $2,400 per researcher per day, and agent runtime rose elevenfold to 5.7 workdays a day.
