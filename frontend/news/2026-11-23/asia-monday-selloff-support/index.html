Seoul leads a fresh Asian sell-off as officials reach for market support

South Korea's Kospi fell as much as 5.8% on Monday before paring losses to about 3.2% by 04:30 GMT, with Samsung down 5.6% and SK Hynix 6% in pre-market trade. Hong Kong's Hang Seng and the CSI 300 were each about 0.6% lower. South Korea, Taiwan and China have rolled out stabilisation measures including short-selling curbs, buyback support and talk of a market stabilisation fund, while US equity futures rose roughly 2% after Friday's rout.
