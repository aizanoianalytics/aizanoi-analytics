OpenAI's chief scientist proposes a shared speed limit built on perplexity

Jakub Pachocki used a 22 November essay to argue that perplexity, or cross-entropy loss, should become a common yardstick across model developers and the basis for shared safety limits. He published OpenAI's internal numbers, placing GPT-3.5 near 0.99 and GPT-4 near 0.91 on its main evaluation set, with current models below 0.8 and a further capability window expected around 0.68 to 0.7. Pachocki proposed that labs commit not to cross agreed thresholds until monitoring improves.
