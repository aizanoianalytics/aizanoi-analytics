McBurnie's stoppage-time double punishes ten-man Brighton at Hull

Oli McBurnie scored in the 92nd and 95th minutes as Hull City beat Brighton 2-0 at the MKM Stadium after a match Brighton had controlled. Costinha was sent off for a second booking in the 89th minute, and Hull broke twice against ten men, McBurnie finishing passes from Lucas Gourna-Douath and Mohamed Belloumi. Goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis made five first-half saves among a string of stops. Hull climbed to 11th on 14 points; Brighton stay second on 22, six behind Arsenal.
