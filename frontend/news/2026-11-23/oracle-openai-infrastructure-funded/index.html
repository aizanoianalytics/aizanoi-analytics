Oracle says its OpenAI data-centre contract is fully funded after $20bn raise

Oracle said on 20 November that its infrastructure contract with OpenAI is fully funded after a final capital raise earlier in the month, and that any further talks with equipment vendors are options rather than necessities. The companies plan 4.5 gigawatts of additional US data-centre capacity beyond the Abilene, Texas site, which Oracle says is nearly complete and trained OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. The next gigawatt-scale campus in Shackelford County is fully permitted, and Nvidia Vera Rubin rack handovers are on track.
