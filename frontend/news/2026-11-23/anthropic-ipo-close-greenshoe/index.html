Anthropic closes its IPO at $93.5bn after underwriters take every extra share

Anthropic said its initial public offering closed on 20 November with gross proceeds of about $93.5 billion after underwriters fully exercised their option on 147 million extra shares. The deal sold 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83, and Anthropic itself received about $91.6 billion. A concurrent $26.0 billion private placement to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners lifted aggregate proceeds to roughly $119.5 billion. The stock trades on Nasdaq as ANT.
