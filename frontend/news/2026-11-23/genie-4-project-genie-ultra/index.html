Google opens Genie 4 to AI Ultra subscribers with real-time world editing

Google rolled out Genie 4 inside Project Genie to eligible AI Ultra subscribers, letting them change weather, add characters and hear adaptive audio by typing prompts while a generated world runs. The update adds a Project Genie agent that builds environments through conversation, plus a community gallery for sharing and remixing worlds. AI Pro members can join a waitlist. Google first announced the world model in late October and had limited it to trusted testers before this wider release.
