Gemini can now drive a Mac in the background, with approval gates

Google added computer use to the Gemini app for macOS, letting the assistant click, type and fill forms across local files, native apps and websites from an isolated background window with a live preview in the chat. Users can pause or stop it and must approve important actions such as purchases. The feature launched in English in the United States for personal accounts aged 18 and over on Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra, and needs an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS Sequoia 15 or later.
