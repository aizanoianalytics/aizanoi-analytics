Bremen's second-half burst settles the Nordderby in a 4-0 rout

Werder Bremen beat Hamburg 4-0 in the 111th Bundesliga Nordderby after a goalless first half at a sold-out Weserstadion. Captain Marco Friedl scored from a corner rebound in the 55th minute, Justin Njinmah added two more in the 66th and 70th on his first start since August, and Niclas Füllkrug completed the scoring in stoppage time with his 10th league goal of the season. The win lifted Bremen to within three points of the Champions League places.
