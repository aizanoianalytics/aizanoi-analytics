Bitcoin's two-year low leaves a $1.25bn week of ETF outflows behind it

Bitcoin slid to about $55,250 on Saturday, its lowest level in two years, before rebounding above $59,900. The move extended a seventh straight weekly decline and followed roughly $2.4 billion of crypto futures liquidations on Friday. US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded about $1.25 billion of net outflows in the week to 20 November, including $582 million on 19 November alone. Traders cited higher real Treasury yields and renewed bets on Federal Reserve tightening as the main pressure.
