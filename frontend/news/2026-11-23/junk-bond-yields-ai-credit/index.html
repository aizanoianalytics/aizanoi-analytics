US junk-bond yields hit a 19-month high as AI credit doubts spread

The yield on US high-yield debt rose to 8.51% on Friday, a 19-month high, with spreads widening to 344 basis points, the widest in 18 months, as investors reassessed the financing behind the AI build-out and priced further Fed hikes. Treasury yields fell as investors sought safety, leaving the 10-year at about 4.94% and the 30-year at 5.30%. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent warned on Sunday that the Fed risks overtightening ahead of this week's PCE inflation release.
