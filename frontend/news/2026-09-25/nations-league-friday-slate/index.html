Italy–Belgium and Türkiye–France headline Friday’s Nations League slate

The UEFA Nations League’s first matchday continues Friday with Italy hosting Belgium and Türkiye hosting France among the League A fixtures. UEFA’s official schedule also lists Georgia–Northern Ireland, Hungary–Ukraine, Poland–Bosnia and Herzegovina, Sweden–Romania and Montenegro–Cyprus.
