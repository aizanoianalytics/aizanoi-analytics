Google adds a live visual persona to Gemini’s enterprise dialogue model

Google has introduced Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar, pairing low-latency streaming video with the company’s real-time dialogue model. The feature is available in Gemini Enterprise and is designed to give enterprise conversations a visible, responsive persona rather than a voice-only interface.
