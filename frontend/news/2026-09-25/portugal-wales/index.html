Portugal open Nations League defence with narrow Wales win

Portugal began the 2026/27 UEFA Nations League with a 1–0 home victory over Wales, João Félix scoring the decisive goal. UEFA’s match report says the holders created the clearer chances but had to withstand a stronger Welsh spell late in the game before protecting the lead.
