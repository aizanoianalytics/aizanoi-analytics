ChatGPT Ads expands into seven more Asian markets

OpenAI says ChatGPT Ads will begin rolling out in Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan. The expansion takes the advertising product beyond 60 countries, while OpenAI says paid Plus, Pro and Enterprise plans will remain ad-free and ads will be clearly separated from answers.
