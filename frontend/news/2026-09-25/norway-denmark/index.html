Haaland-inspired Norway edge Denmark 3–2

Norway beat Denmark 3–2 in the opening round of the 2026/27 Nations League, with Erling Haaland scoring twice in a match that changed direction repeatedly. The result gives Norway an early advantage in League A Group A4 before Sunday’s meeting with Portugal.
