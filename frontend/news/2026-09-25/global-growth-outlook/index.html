IMF keeps 2026 global growth outlook in focus as inflation eases

The IMF’s World Economic Outlook page lists global growth projections of 3.3% for 2026 and 3.2% for 2027, alongside an expectation that global inflation will continue to fall. The baseline points to steady expansion rather than a boom, leaving trade, energy and financial conditions as key swing factors.
