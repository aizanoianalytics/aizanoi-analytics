Bayern head to Mainz with a thinner squad as Dortmund lose Guirassy

Bayern Munich visit Mainz on Saturday without Luis Díaz, Serge Gnabry, Jamal Musiala, Ndiaye and Saibari, with Harry Kane leading the line. Borussia Dortmund confirmed Serhou Guirassy will miss the visit of Elversberg with a muscular problem, joining Emre Can and Konstantelias on the sidelines. Leipzig host Augsburg, Paderborn welcome Eintracht Frankfurt and Stuttgart meet Werder Bremen in the late game, with Köln against Leverkusen and Freiburg against Union to follow on Sunday.
