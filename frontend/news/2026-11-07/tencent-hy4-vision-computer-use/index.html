Tencent's Hy4 adds vision and computer control to a 770-billion-parameter model

Tencent released the full version of Hunyuan Hy4, a 770-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with a one-million-token context that can read charts, screenshots and documents and operate command-line and graphical interfaces. Tencent says it trained the model on professional tools including Blender and CAD software. Hy4 is live through Tencent Cloud and third-party gateways at unchanged pricing, while the weights and a fuller system card are promised in the coming days.
