Mourinho's Madrid chase an eighth straight win at Mestalla

Real Madrid visit Valencia on Saturday bidding for an eighth consecutive win in all competitions; a victory would draw them level with Barcelona on 30 points before the leaders face Atlético Madrid on Sunday. Madrid have scored 21 goals and conceded eight during a seven-match run that includes a Clásico win at Camp Nou and a 3-2 success at AEK Athens, with Kylian Mbappé scoring in six straight games. Valencia are 19th with nine points from 11 matches.
