Six pallets of Nvidia hardware vanish between Oakland and Texas

A shipment of Nvidia hardware valued at $11.4 million disappeared after leaving Oakland on 29 August for Fort Worth, Texas. Six pallets were collected as scheduled by a truck carrying the logo of a food transport company, and investigators lost the vehicle in San Bernardino County; Oakland police confirmed a grand theft report filed on 13 September. No arrests or recovery have been announced, and police have not said what the equipment was.
