Oil falls for a third week despite attacks on Riyadh and Hormuz

Oil posted a third straight weekly decline despite Houthi attacks on Riyadh and reports of tanker disruption in the Strait of Hormuz. WTI settled at $83.98 a barrel, up 0.93% on Friday, and Brent at $92.57, up 0.64%, but both were down roughly 5% on the week as strong Middle East exports kept supply plentiful. A stronger dollar added pressure on dollar-priced commodities.
