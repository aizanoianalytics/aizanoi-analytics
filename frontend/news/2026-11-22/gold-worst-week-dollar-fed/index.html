Gold slides toward its worst week since March as the dollar firms

Spot gold fell 2.7% to $3,525 an ounce on Friday and was down about 9% for the week, its steepest weekly decline since March, as a firm dollar and expectations of a December Federal Reserve rate rise weighed on bullion. Markets priced a 96.8% chance of a quarter-point hike. Silver, platinum and palladium also fell, and analysts described forced liquidation as the AI-led equity selloff forced investors to raise cash.
