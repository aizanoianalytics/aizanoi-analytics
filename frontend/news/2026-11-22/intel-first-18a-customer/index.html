Intel signs its first outside customer for 18A chips

Intel has signed its first outside customer for its most advanced manufacturing process, chief executive Lip-Bu Tan said at the TomorrowX Summit in Austin on 18 November. Technology chief Pushkar Ranade later said the order covers 18A-P, which entered risk production in June. Intel did not name the customer, disclose volumes or give a production date. It also named Microsoft as the first public customer for its EMIB advanced packaging.
