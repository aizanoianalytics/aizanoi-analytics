Google drops the search button as AI takes the homepage

Google has made its AI-first desktop search homepage official after a test, removing the dedicated Search button and adding buttons for image generation, file analysis and brainstorming with Gemini. Users signed in to Google also get a sidebar of recent AI Mode conversations on the homepage and results page. Search vice-president Robby Stein confirmed the rollout; the “I’m Feeling Lucky” button remains.
