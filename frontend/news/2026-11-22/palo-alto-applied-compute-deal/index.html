Palo Alto Networks buys Applied Compute for $4.5bn

Palo Alto Networks agreed on 18 November to buy Applied Compute, a startup that helps companies post-train and run open-weight models, in an all-stock deal valued at $4.5 billion. The acquisition landed alongside fiscal first-quarter revenue of $3.44 billion, up 39%, and a raised full-year revenue forecast of $14.52–14.57 billion. Applied Compute chief executive Yash Patil will become co-chief AI officer; the deal is expected to close in fiscal 2027.
