Leeds hand Chelsea a 4-1 humiliation at Stamford Bridge

Leeds United won 4-1 at Chelsea on Saturday, their first league victory at Stamford Bridge since 1999, as Noah Okafor scored twice and Dominic Calvert-Lewin converted a penalty. Emmanuel Emegha had given Chelsea an early lead on his full debut, but the home side were booed off at half-time and full-time. It was Chelsea’s third Premier League defeat in four games and left Xabi Alonso under renewed pressure.
