A short Thanksgiving week tests the AI trade

US stocks enter a Thanksgiving-shortened week after the S&P 500 fell 8.6%, its worst week since April 2025, with the index and the Dow in correction and the VIX near 36. Dell and HP report on Tuesday, HPE on Monday, and Wednesday brings the second estimate of third-quarter GDP with October PCE inflation, forecast at 0.3% on the month. Markets are closed on Thursday and close early on Friday.
