Kane faces months out as Dortmund take the Bundesliga lead

Harry Kane tore the syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle in Bayern Munich’s 1-1 draw with Köln and had successful surgery on Saturday, with the club saying he will be out for months; German media expect about three. Borussia Dortmund took advantage, winning 1-0 at Borussia Mönchengladbach through Felix Nmecha’s 80th-minute goal to lead the Bundesliga on 24 points, two clear of Bayern and Freiburg.
