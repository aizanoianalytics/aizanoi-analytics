Denmark thrash Portugal 4-1 in Braga in the first match of the post-Ronaldo era

Denmark beat Portugal 4-1 in Braga, with Rasmus Hojlund scoring twice, in Portugal's first match since Cristiano Ronaldo retired from international football and the federation retired his number 7 shirt. Denmark moved into second place in Group A4 and closed on a quarter-final place. Norway beat Wales 3-1 in Oslo in the same group, leaving Wales bottom of the section.
