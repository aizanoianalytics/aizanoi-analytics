SK hynix puts $1.05bn behind Korean chip suppliers and a shared test line

SK hynix will spend 1.4 trillion won, about $1.05 billion, over five years supporting domestic makers of chip materials, parts and equipment, advancing up to half of early development costs and paying suppliers even when a project misses its targets. The centrepiece is Trinity Fab, an 874.6 billion won, 3,305-square-metre test line inside its Yongin plant that opens in May 2027. Korea's domestic sourcing rate in the segment is about 30%.
