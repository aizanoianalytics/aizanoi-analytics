Warsh opens a Fed forum on whether AI lowers or raises inflation

Federal Reserve chair Kevin Warsh convenes a three-hour public forum in San Francisco on Monday on AI, productivity and employment, the only open session among the five task forces he created. Officials are split: Warsh has argued AI can cut costs, while governor Lisa Cook says data-centre spending on power, chips and labour is delaying a return to 2%. The next rate decision is scheduled for 9 December.
