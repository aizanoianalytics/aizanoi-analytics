Amodei calls compute 'the deepest business dilemma' days before Anthropic's IPO

Speaking with Jamie Dimon at a JPMorgan event in New York, Anthropic chief executive Dario Amodei said there is "a huge uncertainty" about next year and that capacity has to be bought one to two years before anyone knows whether revenue will justify it. Bloomberg puts the company's commitments near $901 billion, with roughly $71 billion due by September 2027. The offering is expected to price on 18 November and trade as ANT.
