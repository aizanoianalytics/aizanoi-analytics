LangChain doubles its valuation to $3bn on agent governance demand

LangChain raised $300 million in a Series C led by CapitalG at a $3 billion valuation, more than double its $1.25 billion mark a year earlier, with GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP and Sapphire Ventures participating. The company says its LangSmith agent-management platform has 7,000 customers, including Nvidia, Toyota, Salesforce, Harvey and Rippling, and pitches model- and cloud-neutral governance as the differentiator.
