Asian chip stocks slide into Nvidia's earnings test

MSCI's Asia-Pacific index fell about 1% and its technology sub-index 3.6% on Monday, with Japan's Nikkei down 0.6% and Korea 0.2%, as investors waited for Nvidia's results on Tuesday. LSEG data put Nvidia's market value at $3.9 trillion, down from about $5.8 trillion in early October, with analysts expecting earnings growth of 90% on revenue of $109 billion. Minutes of the Fed's October meeting follow on Wednesday.
