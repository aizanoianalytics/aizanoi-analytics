Ronaldinho plays 45 minutes on his Ravenna debut at 46

Ronaldinho started and played the first half for Serie C side Ravenna against local rivals Forli, 11 years after his last professional appearance, having signed for the club in June. The 46-year-old wore the number 10 in front of a 5,000-capacity Stadio Bruno Benelli expanded by a temporary 1,800-seat stand named R10. "Is this my last one? I don't know," he said afterwards.
