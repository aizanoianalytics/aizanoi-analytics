Treasury yields near 5.15% keep a December rate rise in the price

The 10-year Treasury yield closed near 5.15% after its biggest weekly rise since April 2025, and futures price a quarter-point Federal Reserve increase next month as near certain, with the odds of a 50-basis-point move down to about 6% from 12% a day earlier. Cleveland's Beth Hammack said inflation is heading in the "wrong direction"; Kansas City's Jeff Schmid cited energy, goods, healthcare and AI build-out costs.
