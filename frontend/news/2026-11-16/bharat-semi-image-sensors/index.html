Bharat Semi raises $24m to put AI inside image sensors

New Delhi-based Bharat Semi has raised a $24 million seed round for programmable image sensors that combine capture, signal processing and inference on a single chip, which co-founder Vrinda Kapoor says can cut system costs by at least 70% against separate sensor, processor and system-on-chip designs. The pre-revenue startup claims design wins with General Atomics and Kaynes Semicon and targets first commercial production in 2027.
