Gemini 4 Carbon tops a maths benchmark at fifteen times the price

Google's Gemini 4 Carbon has become the first non-OpenAI model in some time to lead the MathArena benchmark, displacing GPT-6.1 Sol, with Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 second. The Decoder reports that Carbon's most common failure is exceeding the time limit and that, at the reasoning settings used in testing, it costs roughly 15 times as much as GPT-6.1 Sol, while Fable 5.2 costs about 40 times as much.
