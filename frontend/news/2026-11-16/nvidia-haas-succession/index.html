Nvidia's board sounds out Arm's Rene Haas as Huang's successor

Nvidia's board has approached Arm chief executive Rene Haas about succeeding Jensen Huang, the Financial Times reported, while saying it is still choosing a search firm and has not formally engaged any candidate. Haas spent about seven years at Nvidia and retains close ties to Huang and to board members. Chief financial officer Colette Kress is interim CEO and founder-board member Harvey Jones is the company's first chair. Analysts also name Ian Buck and AMD's Lisa Su.
