Premier League Sunday double-header: Moyes reunion as Everton host Manchester United at 14:00 BST

Premier League matchweek three closes on Sunday with two London and Merseyside fixtures: Everton host Manchester United at the Hill Dickinson Stadium at 14:00 BST and Arsenal face Chelsea at the Emirates at 16:30 BST, the Premier League confirmed in its full 2026/27 fixture list. United arrive on the back of a 5-2 home win over Ipswich in which captain Bruno Fernandes scored a hat-trick and recorded an assist, having lost 2-0 at promoted Hull on opening day. The two managers, David Moyes and Michael Carrick, are former colleagues from Moyes's 2013–14 tenure at Old Trafford.
