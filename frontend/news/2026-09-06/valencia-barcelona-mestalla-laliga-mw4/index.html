Barcelona visit Valencia at Mestalla on Sunday chasing a fourth straight La Liga win

La Liga matchday four closes on Sunday with leaders Barcelona travelling to the Mestalla to face a Valencia side that has collected only one point from its opening three matches, the league schedule confirms. Barça arrive top on nine points after opening wins over Elche (5-0), Athletic Club (2-0) and Rayo Vallecano (5-2), with Raphinha top of the Pichichi standings on five goals. Valencia, eighteenth in the table, are navigating a deep injury crisis that includes Umar Sadiq, Sergi Canós and Guido Rodríguez, with Lamine Yamal a doubt for the visitors.
