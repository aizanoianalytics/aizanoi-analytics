Frontier-model cadence tightens as Fable, Gemini 3.8 and Muse Spark 1.3 stack within four days

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 on 1 September, Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash on 2 September and Meta updated Muse Spark 1.3 the same day, with OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra following on 3 September, The Rundown AI summarised on 6 September. Anthropic claims fewer safeguard interruptions and lower costs at typical settings; Astra's public benchmark edge is real but contested on third-party indices, where Fable 5.1 still leads at the highest tier. The next competitive test, the note argues, will be completion rates and cost per task rather than headline scores.
