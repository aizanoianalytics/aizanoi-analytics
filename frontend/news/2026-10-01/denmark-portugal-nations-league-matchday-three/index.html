Portugal visit Denmark with Ronaldo's role the open question

Portugal travel to Parken Stadium in Copenhagen on Thursday evening defending top spot in Nations League Group A4, having beaten Wales 1-0 and won 2-1 in Norway. Denmark have three points after losing in Norway and beating Wales. Bruno Fernandes is sidelined with physical discomfort and Manchester United's Patrick Dorgu has returned to his club with a hamstring injury, while Denmark are without Christian Eriksen and the injured Albert Gronbaek. Portugal coach Jorge Jesus had planned to pair Goncalo Ramos, who scored in Oslo, with Joao Felix in attack.
