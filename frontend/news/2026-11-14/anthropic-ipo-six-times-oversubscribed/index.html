Anthropic's IPO is six times oversubscribed at a $1.4 trillion target

Orders for Anthropic's initial public offering exceed the shares on offer by a factor of six, Bloomberg reported, putting the Claude developer on course for the largest listing on record. The company aims to raise up to $64.4 billion in the offering and another $26 billion in a private placement at a valuation of roughly $1.4 trillion, above SpaceX's IPO. Annualised revenue reached $81 billion by late October, up from $71 billion in September.
