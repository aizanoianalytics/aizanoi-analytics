Salah's double and Nigeria's bench revive Afcon 2027 campaigns

Mohamed Salah scored twice late as Egypt beat Malawi 5-0 in Ali Maher's first match in charge, while Tolu Arokodare came off the bench to score twice in Nigeria's 3-1 win over Tanzania. Achraf Hakimi scored two and Yassine Bounou saved a penalty in Morocco's 5-1 win over Niger. Tunisia lost 2-0 at home to Libya and are in danger of missing the finals for the first time since 1992, and Kenya scored a last-minute equaliser to end South Africa's perfect start.
