Italy beat France 3-1 at San Siro to top their Nations League group

Italy came from behind to beat France 3-1 in Milan on Thursday, Pio Esposito scoring twice and Davide Frattesi once in front of 71,452 spectators. Michael Olise had given France a 10th-minute lead after a Donnarumma error; Esposito equalised on 26 minutes, Frattesi scored on 63 and Esposito converted a penalty on 75 after his brother Sebastiano had missed one. It was Italy's first home win over France in 64 years and Zinedine Zidane's first defeat as coach.
