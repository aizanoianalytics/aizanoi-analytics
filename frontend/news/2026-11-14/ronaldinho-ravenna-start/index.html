Ronaldinho, 46, is set for his first Ravenna start on Sunday

Ronaldinho will start for Ravenna against Forli in Italy's Serie C on Sunday, his first competitive start in more than a decade. The 46-year-old joined the club in August as an investor and has trained once with his team-mates since arriving in Italy. Coach Andrea Mandorlini said he would play as one of three forwards; the club has added a temporary stand for the expected crowd, and Ronaldinho is expected to take penalties as he chases a 300th career goal.
