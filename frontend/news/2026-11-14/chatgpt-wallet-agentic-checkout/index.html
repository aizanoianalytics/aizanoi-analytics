OpenAI lets ChatGPT shop and pay through a new Wallet

OpenAI launched a wallet for ChatGPT on Friday that lets its agents, called dots, research a purchase and complete checkout. It starts with a limited number of US ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over paying with a US-issued Visa, Mastercard or American Express. Users review the items, the total and the card before approving with Face ID or a fingerprint, and the payment runs on a token rather than raw card details. OpenAI says spending limits and category controls are planned later.
