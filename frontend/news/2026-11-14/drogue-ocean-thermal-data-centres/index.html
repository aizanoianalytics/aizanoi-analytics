A startup wants to run floating data centres on ocean heat

Drogue, founded six months ago in Washington, has raised $20 million to build data centres that generate their own power from the temperature difference between warm surface water and cold deep water, a process known as ocean thermal energy conversion. Investors include XYZ Venture Capital, Toyota Ventures, Boost VC and Slow Ventures. Chief executive Sam Sipe says a multi-megawatt prototype will go to sea on a conventional ship in early 2028, aiming at AI timelines rather than the decade utilities need.
