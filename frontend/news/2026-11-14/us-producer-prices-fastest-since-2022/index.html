US producer prices post their biggest annual rise since November 2022

The producer price index rose 0.3% in October, below the 0.4% forecast, but was up 6.5% over twelve months, the largest annual gain since November 2022, the Bureau of Labor Statistics said. Final demand prices had risen 1.4% in September. Core prices excluding food and energy rose 0.3% on the month and 5% on the year. The report followed a hotter-than-expected consumer price reading and left markets pricing a quarter-point Federal Reserve rate rise in December.
