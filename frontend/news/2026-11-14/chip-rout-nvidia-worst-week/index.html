Chip rout hands Nvidia its worst week since April 2025

A gauge of chipmakers fell 4.5% on Friday and Nvidia lost 14% over the week, its worst run since April 2025, as investors waited for the company's first earnings call without Jensen Huang on 17 November. The S&P 500 slipped 0.4% to 7,561.67 and the Nasdaq composite 0.9% to 25,136.27, while the Dow added 0.1%. All three still rose more than 2% over the week, the market's first winning week since early October, the Associated Press reported.
