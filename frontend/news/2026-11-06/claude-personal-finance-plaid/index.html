Claude links bank accounts through Plaid, read-only and US-only for now

Anthropic opened a beta of Personal Finance in Claude for Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers in the United States. Users connect bank and card accounts through Plaid and ask about balances, spending and upcoming bills; Claude can see account names, types, last four digits, balances, credit limits and up to two years of transactions. Access is read-only, the feature carries no ads or sponsored recommendations, and conversations in the finance hub are not used to train models.
