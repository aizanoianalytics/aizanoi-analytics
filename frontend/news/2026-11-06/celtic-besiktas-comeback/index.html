Celtic's substitutes rescue a 3-2 win over Besiktas

Celtic beat Besiktas 3-2 at Celtic Park for their first Europa League win of the campaign after Martin O'Neill turned to his bench. Junior Olaitan put the visitors ahead from a rebound after Sam Johnstone saved his penalty, but Callum Osmand equalised with his first touch and set up Camilo Duran to make it 2-1. Orkun Kokcu levelled from 25 yards with five minutes left, and Duran scored again 84 seconds later.
