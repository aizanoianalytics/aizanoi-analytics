Perplexity's Teammates turn always-on agents into a $200-a-month colleague

Perplexity launched Teammates, agents that own ongoing work rather than answer a single prompt. Each teammate carries over projects, skills and memory from Perplexity Computer, runs in its own desktop and browser, and can split a goal into parallel tasks or hand work to other agents. Progress is tracked in individual threads and through Slack. The feature is a research preview on the web for Max subscribers, who pay $200 a month.
