Ferencvaros leave San Siro with four wins from four as Milan stall

Lenny Joseph scored twice as Ferencvaros won 2-0 at Milan to stay perfect in the Europa League league phase. Milan's 21-year-old goalkeeper Lorenzo Torriani made five saves from Joseph alone on his full debut before the striker broke through early in the second half and added a second on the break. Bamidele Yusuf was sent off late for the visitors, but Milan could not capitalise and remain on three points from four matches.
