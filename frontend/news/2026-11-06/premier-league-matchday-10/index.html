Arsenal take a three-point lead into Premier League matchday 10

Arsenal lead the Premier League on 24 points after nine matches, three clear of Manchester City, following their 1-0 win at Liverpool on Sunday. Matchday 10 opens on Friday with Everton hosting Coventry City, then Leeds United play Tottenham Hotspur in Saturday's early kick-off, Arsenal meet Hull City and Nottingham Forest host City. Sunday brings Crystal Palace against Liverpool, Sunderland against Chelsea and Manchester United against Aston Villa.
