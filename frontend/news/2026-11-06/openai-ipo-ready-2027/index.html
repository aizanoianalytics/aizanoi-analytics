Brockman says OpenAI could be ready to list in 2027

OpenAI president Greg Brockman told a Wall Street Journal conference that the company could be ready for an initial public offering in 2027, answering "I think so" when asked. He said enterprise customers now provide the majority of revenue, up from roughly 30 percent, and that annualised revenue grew 70 percent quarter on quarter in the third quarter as the enterprise business doubled. No filing, valuation or listing date has been announced.
