Micron halts work on its $4.5 billion Taoyuan memory plant

Micron has all but stopped construction of a memory chip plant in Taoyuan, Taiwan, after a protracted labour dispute, Bloomberg reported. The Fab 11E project, budgeted at $3 billion to $4.5 billion and previously due to start production in 2028, was meant to make DRAM and high-bandwidth memory with top-end ASML equipment. Micron said its existing Taiwan operations continue normally. Taiwan produces three-fifths of the company's conventional chips.
