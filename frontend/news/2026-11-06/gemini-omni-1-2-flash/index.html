Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash stretches AI video to 15 seconds and cuts the price

Google made Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash generally available with native 15-second clips, up from 10 seconds in version 1.1, and cut the price 30 percent to $0.07 per second for 720p video. The model supports multi-turn conversational edits, stronger reference adherence for products and characters, and durations set in one-second steps from three to 15 seconds. It is available through the Gemini API, Google Cloud, Google Vids and Gemini for business.
