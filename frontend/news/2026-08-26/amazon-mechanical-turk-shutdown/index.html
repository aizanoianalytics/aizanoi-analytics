Amazon prepares to close Mechanical Turk’s long-running task marketplace

Amazon is shutting down Mechanical Turk, the human-task service once described by Jeff Bezos as “artificial artificial intelligence.” Its closure marks the end of an early model for combining software workflows with on-demand human judgment.
