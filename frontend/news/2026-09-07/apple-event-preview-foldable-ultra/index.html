Apple's September 9 "Surprise and Shine" event to host first foldable iPhone Ultra alongside iPhone 18 Pro, Watch Series 12 and AirPods 5

Apple has invited media to its September 9 keynote at Apple Park, themed "Surprise and Shine", where new CEO John Ternus will host his first major launch. Reporting from MacRumors, Tom's Guide and Firstpost sketches a lineup of the iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max with the A20 Pro chip and C2 modem, the long-rumoured foldable iPhone Ultra with a 7.8-inch inner display and Touch ID, Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, AirPods 5, and possibly a new HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K. Pre-orders are widely expected on September 12 with retail availability on September 18.
