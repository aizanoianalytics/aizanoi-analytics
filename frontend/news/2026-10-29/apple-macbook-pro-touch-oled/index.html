Apple puts touch and a tandem-OLED display on the MacBook Pro

Apple announced a redesigned MacBook Pro with a PROLED XDR tandem-OLED display, 2,000-nit peak brightness and touch input built into the panel rather than layered on top, a first for the Mac. The 14-inch M5 Pro model starts at $2,899 and the 16-inch at $3,399, with M5 Max versions from $4,499; pre-orders opened on 28 October and deliveries begin on 6 November.
