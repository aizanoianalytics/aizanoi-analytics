Bayern hit six and Dresden dump Schalke out of the DFB-Pokal

Bayern Munich beat Magdeburg 6-1 and Bayer Leverkusen survived Nürnberg 5-2 as the DFB-Pokal's second round closed, but Schalke lost 2-0 at Dynamo Dresden and Hoffenheim went out on penalties at Holstein Kiel. VfB Stuttgart needed Deniz Undav's goal in stoppage time to win 1-0 at Paderborn and Freiburg turned Wolfsburg over 3-2. Four lower-division clubs reached the last 16.
