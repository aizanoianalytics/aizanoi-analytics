Liverpool survive Chelsea on penalties as the Carabao Cup last eight takes shape

Liverpool beat Chelsea 6-5 on penalties after a 1-1 draw at Anfield, Bournemouth thrashed Aston Villa 3-0, Sunderland scored three times in ten minutes to beat Brentford 3-2 and Crystal Palace won 1-0 at Fulham. The quarter-final draw gave Liverpool a home tie with Bournemouth, Palace with Sunderland and Manchester City with Bradford City, while Fleetwood Town host the winners of Thursday's Everton-Newcastle match.
