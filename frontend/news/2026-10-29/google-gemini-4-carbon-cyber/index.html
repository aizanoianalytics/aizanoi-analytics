Google puts Gemini 4 Carbon behind the cyber-defender door first

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon, which it says ranks first on the v5.0 Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 59 and leads SEC-bench Pro at 88.2% for finding vulnerabilities, ExploitGym at 54.8% and CWE-bench at 71.0% for patching. Instead of a general launch, access starts with a subset of cyber defenders and widens through the Fairwind Program, at Argon's introductory $2 and $10 per million tokens before a $4 and $20 standard rate.
