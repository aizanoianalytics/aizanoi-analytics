Ballon d'Or 2026 shortlist of 30 men to be revealed on 8 September at ballondor.com

France Football and UEFA confirmed that the 30-man Ballon d'Or 2026 shortlist will be unveiled on 8 September 2026, with parallel shortlists for the women's awards, the Kopa Trophy, goalkeeper of the year, striker of the year, coach of the year and club of the year categories. The latest in-season reference points include Paris Saint-Germain's Ousmane Dembélé, Barcelona's Lamine Yamal and Viktor Gyökeres of Sporting CP, with last year's male forward of the year Harry Kane and male club of the year Real Madrid among the recent laureates.
