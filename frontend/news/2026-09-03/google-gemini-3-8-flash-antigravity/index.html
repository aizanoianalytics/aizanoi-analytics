Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash inside the Antigravity coding agent at $0.75 per million input tokens

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash in its Antigravity coding agent on 1 September 2026, marking the third Flash release in six weeks and positioning 3.8 Flash as a daily workhorse for agentic development at $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens. Google says the model closes most of the gap to higher-cost frontier models on code and reasoning while letting developers tune thinking levels per task, with 3.7 Flash remaining available for workflows that prioritise compute efficiency.
