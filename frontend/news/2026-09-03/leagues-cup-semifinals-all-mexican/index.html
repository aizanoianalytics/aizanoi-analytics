Toluca beat 10-man León 2-0 to reach first Leagues Cup final as Liga MX sweeps the semifinals

Toluca advanced to its first Leagues Cup final on 2 September 2026 with a 2-0 win over 10-man León at Shell Energy Stadium in Houston, with Federico Pereira opening the scoring before the break and a second-half goal sealing the result after Jhohan Romaña was sent off for a second yellow card. The result confirmed the first all-Liga MX Leagues Cup final in the modern 36-team format, guarantees a Mexican club the trophy and locks a Concacaf Champions Cup place; the 2026 final is scheduled for 6 September.
