Samsung adds a $799 14-inch Galaxy Book6 with 25-hour battery to its AI PC line-up

Samsung Electronics launched a new 14-inch Galaxy Book6 on 1 September 2026 in South Korea starting at ₩1.19 million (around $819) or $799 in the US, undercutting the rest of the Galaxy Book6 line-up by up to ₩1.02 million. The Windows laptop ships with Intel Core Series 3 or Core 5 processors, claims up to 25 hours of video playback, runs Samsung's Galaxy AI suite and comes in a single Gray colourway, with Samsung positioning it as a value-oriented everyday PC that still meets the AI PC bar.
