Aizanoi research notes

This is the non-WebGL companion to the Aizanoi reconstruction. It exposes the same project boundary, evidence vocabulary and source ledger without requiring the interactive renderer.

Reconstruction boundary

The live city represents the Penkalas valley urban core and uses the authored periods AD 225, 301 and 425. The browser layout compresses remote monuments where necessary and deliberately keeps playable city ground flat at Y=0.

What the 3D view claims

Named monuments and topographical relationships come from the project source records. The low-poly/blocky visual language is schematic: visual detail is not evidence of archaeological certainty.

Known reconstruction choices

Residential and workshop infill is explicitly plausible rather than an individually excavated footprint. The Early 5th-Century Colonnaded Street is period-gated, while the Roman River Dam and Meter Steunene sanctuary are spatially compressed in the browser layout.

Research Lens

The interactive world exposes a shared Research Lens. Press V or use the Evidence control to inspect the nearest labelled monuments and their evidence class.

Evidence vocabulary

Archaeological — physical archaeological remains and published excavation data support the represented monument; exact blocky restitution remains schematic.

Documented — topography or function is source-supported while exact restitution may remain incomplete.

Inferred — an informed reconstruction completes an unresolved architectural or urban gap. Legacy plausible records are grouped here by Research Lens without rewriting their authored source label.

Atmospheric — illustrative ambience is not a claim about an exact excavated object or placement.

Disputed — a represented identification or restitution is contested and must be read as an interpretation.
