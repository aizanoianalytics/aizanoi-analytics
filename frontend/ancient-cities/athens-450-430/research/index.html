# Athens · 450–430 BCE — research notes A source-led reconstruction of Classical Athens during the Periclean building programme. The page intentionally loads no CDN assets, no analytics, and no map tiles; it only links to public research material held by English Wikipedia, the American School of Classical Studies at Athens (Agora Excavations), the British Museum, Perseus Digital Library, ToposText and Pleiades. ## How to use This page is the static, non-WebGL version of the same source material the 3D experience consumes. If your browser disables WebGL, you still get here and the methodology below still explains what is and is not a historical claim. ## Reconstruction methodology - **Named monuments and major roads** are placed from archaeological evidence and Travlos / ASCSA / British Museum records. - **Domestic urban massing** outside the named monuments is deliberately schematic: it gives city scale without claiming to reconstruct individual houses. - **Terrain** preserves the Acropolis rock, Areopagus, Pnyx, the Hill of the Nymphs and the long slopes down to the Ilissos valley; local elevation is illustrative, not surveyed. - **Modern overlay** is a schematic alignment layer, not a claimed modern cadastral map. - **Audio** is opt-in and procedural only. ## Core spatial evidence - ASCSA Agora Excavations — [agora.ascsa.gr](https://www.agora.ascsa.gr/) and [agoraexcavations.org](https://www.agoraexcavations.org/) - Travlos, J., *Pictorial Dictionary of Ancient Athens* — the standard reference (library access; cited indirectly via the secondary sources below). - Perseus Digital Library — [perseus.tufts.edu](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/) - ToposText — [topostext.org](https://topostext.org/) - Pleiades — [pleiades.stoa.org](https://pleiades.stoa.org/) ## Period framing 450–430 BCE is the Periclean city in its mature shape: the Acropolis rebuilding complete (Parthenon finished 432 BCE; Propylaea finished 432 BCE), the Agora civic core established, the Piraeus harbour city connected by the Long Walls. The Erechtheion is under construction (421–406 BCE). The Plague of Athens begins in 430 BCE; we stop at the boundary. ## Major entries - **Akropolis**: Parthenon (447–432 BCE, finished); Propylaea (437–432 BCE, finished); Erechtheion (421–406 BCE, under construction); Temple of Athena Nike (ca. 427–424 BCE, finished); Old Parthenon predecessor (destroyed 480 BCE — not present); Chalkotheke; sanctuary of Artemis Brauronia. - **South slope of the Akropolis**: Theatre of Dionysus; Odeion of Pericles (ca. 443 BCE); Asclepieion. - **Agora of Athens**: Hephaisteion (rededicated 449 BCE); Stoa of Zeus Eleutherios (ca. 425 BCE); Royal Stoa (ca. 450 BCE); Stoa Poikile (Painted Stoa, ca. 460 BCE); Stoa of Hermes (ca. 443 BCE); Bouleuterion; Metroon; Tholos; Panathenaic Way. - **Outside the walls**: Kerameikos cemetery (Sacred Gate, Dipylon Gate, Pompeion); Pnyx; Areopagus; Olympieion (the small archaic sanctuary, not the later Roman giant); Academy (olive grove and gymnasium). - **Piraeus**: Hippodamian grid (mid-5th c.); Piraeus Long Walls; Agora of Hippalos; shipsheds of Zea and Munichia. ## Key event and monument references - Parthenon — [Wikipedia: Parthenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthenon) - Propylaea — [Wikipedia: Propylaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propylaea) - Erechtheion — [Wikipedia: Erechtheion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erechtheion) - Temple of Athena Nike — [Wikipedia: Temple of Athena Nike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_of_Athena_Nike) - Agora of Athens — [Wikipedia: Ancient Agora of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Agora_of_Athens) - Hephaisteion — [Wikipedia: Hephaisteion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hephaisteion) - Stoa Poikile — [Wikipedia: Stoa Poikile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stoa_Poikile) - Theatre of Dionysus — [Wikipedia: Theatre of Dionysus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theatre_of_Dionysus) - Kerameikos — [Wikipedia: Kerameikos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerameikos) - Pnyx — [Wikipedia: Pnyx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pnyx) - Areopagus — [Wikipedia: Areopagus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areopagus) - Piraeus — [Wikipedia: Piraeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piraeus) ## Period framing references - Pericles — [Wikipedia: Pericles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pericles) - Delian League — [Wikipedia: Delian League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delian_League) - Thirty Years' Peace — [Wikipedia: Thirty Years' Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirty_Years'_Peace) - Peloponnesian War lead-in — [Wikipedia: Peloponnesian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peloponnesian_War) - Plague of Athens — [Wikipedia: Plague of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plague_of_Athens) ← [Return to the 3D reconstruction](../)
