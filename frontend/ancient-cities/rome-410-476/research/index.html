ROME · AD 410–476
Research notes

This WebGL scene is an interpretive public-history model. Monument locations and historical claims are linked to a source ledger; unknown fifth-century elevations and most domestic massing are rendered as schematic fabric rather than fake certainty.

Sources · Core spatial evidence

  • Stanford Digital Forma Urbis Romae Project — fragments and plan evidence for imperial Rome.
  • Notitia / Curiosum Regionum XIIII — the late Roman catalogue of the 14 regions.

Late-antique framework

  • Richard Krautheimer, Rome: Profile of a City, 312–1308.
  • Amanda Claridge, Rome: An Oxford Archaeological Guide.
  • Bryan Ward-Perkins, The Fall of Rome and the End of Civilization.
  • Neil Christie, The Fall of the Western Roman Empire.

Repository research record

The complete working research archive is stored alongside this project under research/rome_410_476/, including a 14-region ledger, a 410–476 event report, and archived web snapshots. The public experience remains self-contained at runtime and loads no third-party map tiles, analytics, or assets.
