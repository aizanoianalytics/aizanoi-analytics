Methodology

Markets is a static-first descriptive product built from published close-price snapshots. Breadth and rankings use the selected universe, equal-weight comparisons and deterministic return horizons; missing observations remain missing rather than being filled. Prices are not a live quote stream, and this surface is not investment advice.

US coverage is the focused union of S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, NYSE U.S. 100 and DJIA constituents. Crypto coverage is a curated liquid-asset universe. Historical prices and indicators are calculated from the published daily series, with recent four-hour history shown only where available.
