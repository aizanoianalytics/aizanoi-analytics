How this is built

Sources. US equities: Fintable. Crypto: Binance USDT spot pairs.

Freshness. A private updater publishes static JSON shards. The page shows the last complete close snapshot, not a live tape.

Picks. Strong / Weak Momentum requires the same sign on at least 8 of 9 return horizons. The rule is deterministic and is not a recommendation.

Market observations are sourced from third-party data providers and processed independently by Aizanoi Analytics.

Returns, rankings, technical indicators and Aizanoi Picks are calculated independently by Aizanoi Analytics from source market observations.

Market data may be delayed, incomplete, corrected or temporarily unavailable.
