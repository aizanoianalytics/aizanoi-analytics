Method & coverage

Data source: Yahoo Finance. US coverage is built from active Nasdaq Trader listings on Nasdaq, NYSE, NYSE American, NYSE Arca and Cboe BZX, excluding OTC venues, test issues and ETFs. Daily close history begins at 2019 or the instrument’s first available session. Yahoo’s available four-hour close history is retained for the recent period.

Prices may be delayed, incomplete or corrected by the source. Relative strength uses the equal-weight internal market universe as its benchmark. Signals are descriptive and are not investment advice.
