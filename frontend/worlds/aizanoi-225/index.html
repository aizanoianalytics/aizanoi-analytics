AIZANOI · AD 225

Phrygia Epiktetos · Penkalas Valley · Roman Imperial Aizanoi

Experience the city of Zeus in Roman Phrygia. Walk beneath the majestic pseudodipteral colonnade of the Temple of Zeus, descend into the subterranean vault, visit the round Macellum whose walls preserve Diocletian’s AD 301 Price Edict, and cross the stone arches of Hadrian over the Penkalas river.

Period: Roman Imperial (AD 225)
Scale: 1.8 km River Valley Grid
Monuments: Temple of Zeus, Macellum, Theatre–Stadium
Sources: DPU Excavations, Tandoğan & Erdoğan (2020)
