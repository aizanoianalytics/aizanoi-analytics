Flagship world · Roman Phrygia, c. AD 225

Aizanoi

The flagship interactive world of Aizanoi Analytics — an evidence-aware, walkable reconstruction of Roman Aizanoi in Phrygia. Every structure is classified by evidence level, and you can reveal that classification at any time while you walk.

  • Requires WebGL 2
  • Desktop and mobile
Enter AizanoiOpen in AizanoiOS

Needs a browser with WebGL 2. If your device cannot run it, you can still read about the city and its evidence below.

What you will see

Temple of Zeus

Pseudodipteral Ionic sanctuary, its 8×15 colonnade, podium, cella and pediment reconstructed from archaeological evidence.

Penkalas River and the Roman bridges

The riverfront that shaped the city’s plan, crossed by several bridges including the well-preserved Bridge III.

Macellum and the Price Edict

The market hall whose walls carry the Price Edict of Diocletian.

Theatre–Stadium complex

A combined theatre and stadium for spectacle and athletics.

Great Bath and palaestra

The bath complex with its exercise yard and heated rooms.

Agora and the civic core

Propylon, colonnaded streets and the public buildings of the city.

Evidence and methodology

Every structure in Aizanoi is classified as documented (archaeologically verified), inferred (partially attested) or reconstructed (interpretive). Those distinctions are preserved throughout the experience rather than smoothed over, and evidence mode exposes them on demand while you explore. Sources and method are part of the product, not an appendix.

Controls

Desktop

WASD to move, mouse to look, E to interact.

Mobile

Virtual joystick to move, touch to look.

Fast travel

The map and teleport menu jump you between landmarks.

Evidence mode

Toggle it to see the evidence classification of what you are looking at.
