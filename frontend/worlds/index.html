Aizanoi

The flagship interactive world of Aizanoi Analytics — an evidence-aware, walkable reconstruction of Roman Aizanoi in Phrygia, c. AD 225. Every structure is classified by evidence level, and that classification stays visible to you while you walk.

Open in AizanoiOS Enter Aizanoi

What you will see

  • Temple of Zeus — pseudodipteral Ionic sanctuary, its 8×15 colonnade, podium, cella and pediment reconstructed from archaeological evidence.
  • Penkalas River and the Roman bridges — the riverfront that shaped the city's plan, crossed by several bridges including the well-preserved Bridge III.
  • Macellum and the Price Edict — the market hall whose walls carry the Price Edict of Diocletian.
  • Theatre–Stadium complex — a combined theatre and stadium for spectacle and athletics.
  • Great Bath and palaestra — the bath complex with its exercise yard and heated rooms.
  • Agora and the civic core — propylon, colonnaded streets and the public buildings of the city.

Evidence and methodology

Every structure in Aizanoi is classified as documented (archaeologically verified), inferred (partially attested) or reconstructed (interpretive). Those distinctions are preserved throughout the experience rather than smoothed over, and the in-world evidence mode exposes them on demand. Sources and method are part of the product, not an appendix.

Controls

  • Desktop — WASD to move, mouse to look, E to interact.
  • Mobile — virtual joystick to move, touch to look.
  • Fast travel — the map and teleport menu jump you between landmarks.
  • Evidence mode — toggle it to see the evidence classification of what you are looking at.
