# Fly House implementation plan

## Product decision

- Build the user-approved rustic house interior rather than the earlier 12×12 m ancient courtyard concept.
- Reproduce the reference composition closely: barred window + layered curtains, long bench/cabinet wall, old TV, central wood stove and pipe, dense floor textiles/clutter, open doorway and connected bedroom.
- Human visitors are ghost observers: they do not collide with, shade, make sound for, or appear in the future fly sensory world.
- Architecture remains N-fly-ready but the first experiment will use one fly.

## Sequence

1. Reference-locked room blockout and five benchmark views.
2. Blender asset replacement and PBR material pass.
3. Browser ghost-observer runtime.
4. Visual quality gate.
5. Only then: world physics/sensory semantics and Fly #001.

Procedural boxes/cylinders are permitted only as explicit blockout proxies. They are not acceptable as the visually approved final furniture.
