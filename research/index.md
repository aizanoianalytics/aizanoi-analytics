# Research Index

Scope: source material, evidence notes and research inputs, especially for Historical Worlds.

## Route by task

- Classical Athens, 450–430 BCE research → `athens_450_430/`
- Late Antique Rome, 410–476 CE research → `rome_410_476/`

## Boundary

Research evidence and public runtime implementation are separate concerns. Preserve documented/source-supported, archaeological/material, inferred, atmospheric and disputed evidence levels where applicable. Do not promote an inferred reconstruction to documented fact merely because related research material exists.
