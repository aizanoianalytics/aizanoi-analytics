# Research Index

Scope: source material, evidence notes and research inputs, especially for Historical Worlds.

## Route by task

- Historical/source research → locate the relevant world/topic within this area.
- Shared Historical Worlds runtime → `../frontend/ancient-world/engine/`
- Aizanoi public world → `../frontend/historic-world/`
- Rome / Athens public worlds → `../frontend/ancient-cities/`

## Boundary

Research evidence and public runtime implementation are separate concerns. Preserve documented/source-supported, archaeological/material, inferred, atmospheric and disputed evidence levels where applicable.

Do not promote an inferred reconstruction to documented fact merely because related research material exists.
